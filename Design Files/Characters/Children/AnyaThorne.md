# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Remembering and Living Are Not Opposites

Character Arc Start State: Anya quietly clings to treasured memories and familiar places, believing that moving forward may cause important people and moments to fade or be left behind.

Character Arc End State: Anya understands that creating new memories does not erase or diminish the people and moments she treasures. She carries the past with her while allowing herself to experience curiosity, happiness, and hope in the present.

Core Belief: The people and moments we love deserve to be remembered.

Core Misbelief: Moving forward means leaving the past—and the people connected to it—behind.

Core Traits: Reserved, Sensitive, Thoughtful

Defining Sentence: ”Even the smallest kindness has room to bloom.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To understand that remembrance and new happiness can coexist, and that living fully is another way of honoring what came before.

Favorite Location: Memorial Hill Grounds within the cemetery near Ian’s grave marker

Fears: Anya fears forgetting important people, losing treasured memories, and discovering that moments she believed would last forever have faded. She is also afraid that enjoying new experiences could mean she no longer cares enough about the past.

Goals: To preserve the stories, places, and small moments that matter to her; to protect Memorial Hill’s history; and eventually to help others recognize the beauty and meaning hidden in ordinary things.

Habit: Hums softly while tending flowers, barely realizing she’s doing it.

History: Anya has spent all of her life at Memorial Hill with her mother, Lena, and late father, Ian. The hill’s memorials, flowers, and quiet grounds became a source of comfort following the flood and the losses experienced throughout Blackmere. Naturally observant and sensitive, Anya began collecting pressed flowers, sketches, and small keepsakes connected to meaningful places and moments, especially those shared with her father. She rarely speaks openly about her sadness, instead preserving it privately through the objects and memories she carefully protects.

Hobbies: Pressing flowers, collecting small keepsakes, sketching plants and scenery, watching birds, reading quietly, visiting memorials, and recording details about places that other people overlook.

Motivation: Anya wants to ensure that meaningful people, places, and experiences are not forgotten. Her desire to preserve memories motivates her collecting, her interest in overlooked details, and her efforts to document Blackmere as it recovers.

Personality Colors: Indigo, Muted Mauve, Ash Gray

Personality Summary: Anya is a reserved and deeply sensitive child who spends more time observing than speaking. She notices subtle changes, quiet acts of kindness, and overlooked details that others often miss. Although cautious in social situations, she is exceptionally compassionate, loyal, and sincere once she feels safe. Her attachment to memories gives her remarkable emotional depth, but it can also leave her dwelling on what has been lost instead of embracing in what is happening around her.

Public Persona: Most residents see Anya as a quiet, polite, and somewhat solemn child who prefers the peaceful grounds of Memorial Hill to crowded gatherings. She rarely begins conversations, but listens attentively and often remembers small details others have forgotten.

Secret: Anya keeps a private collection of pressed flowers, sketches, notes, and small objects associated with people and moments she is afraid of forgetting. She sometimes revisits the collection when she worries that moving forward means leaving her father behind.

Speaking Tone: Guarded

Strength: Depth

Weakness: Melancholy

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Anya follows a quiet and relatively consistent routine centered around Memorial Hill, A. Whitmore Public School, and peaceful locations throughout Blackmere. She spends much of her free time tending flowers, sketching, reading, watching birds, collecting small keepsakes, and visiting places she considers meaningful.

Anya attends A. Whitmore Public School on weekdays while the school is open. Outside of school, she prefers quiet outdoor locations and usually avoids crowded areas unless spending time with friends.

Her routine remains particularly centered around Memorial Hill, where she lives with Lena and frequently spends time near Ian's grave marker.

### Rainy

Anya attends school normally on weekdays.

After school or during weekend free time, she avoids unnecessary travel through Blackmere and prefers quiet indoor or sheltered locations.

Preferred Locations:

- Memorial Hill Residence
- A. Whitmore Public Library on weekdays

From Day 1 through Day 3 of Spring, Anya follows the appropriate weekday from her School Closure Override rather than attempting to visit either location.

If severe weather occurs during the Early Spring Override, the Severe Weather Schedule takes priority.

## Town Restoration Routine Override

Anya's standard schedule remains centered around Memorial Hill, school, and quiet locations. Restoration changes should gradually widen the places she chooses to spend free time without removing Memorial Hill from her routine.

### Weathered / Rebuilding

Anya remains strongly centered around Memorial Hill. Damaged public areas and unfinished restoration sites rarely replace her normal destinations.

### Recovering / Renewed / Growing

As Blackmere becomes more comfortable and recognizable, selected afternoon blocks may be replaced with visits to restored gardens, Town Square, the library, and other peaceful public spaces. She still returns to Memorial Hill regularly.

### Prospering / Flourishing

Anya spends noticeably more free time beyond Memorial Hill, including restored community spaces and quiet gathering areas. Her routine should show that remembering Ian remains important without requiring her to remain physically close to the memorial every day.

## Routine Characterization

Anya's routine is intentionally centered heavily around Memorial Hill. Her limited travel reflects both her reserved personality and her emotional attachment to familiar places, memories, and the people she has lost.

Her regular trips to school, the library, Town Square, and other parts of Blackmere provide opportunities for the player to encounter her naturally without making her unusually social or adventurous.

Ian's grave is an important part of Anya's routine without becoming her only activity. She visits it regularly, particularly on Tuesday, Friday, and Sunday, while also tending flowers, watching birds, sketching, reading, and documenting the changing landscape of Memorial Hill.

Wednesday's library visit reinforces Anya's quiet and reflective nature while remaining within the library's weekday operating hours.

Her school closure routine deliberately becomes more insular. With fewer obligations drawing her into Blackmere, Anya naturally spends more time at Memorial Hill. Social visits on Tuesday and Friday prevent her from becoming completely isolated and maintain her friendships with Kira, Felix, Milo, and the other children.

As Anya's character arc progresses, special events may increasingly place her in new locations and social situations. These changes represent her growing understanding that experiencing new things and creating new memories do not require her to abandon the people and moments she treasures.
