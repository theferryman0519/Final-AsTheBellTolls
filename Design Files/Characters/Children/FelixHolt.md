# Personality Information

Character Arc: Finding the Courage to Explore

Character Arc Start State: Felix is endlessly curious about the world around him, but his fear of embarrassment and failure keeps many of his dreams and discoveries to himself.

Character Arc End State: Felix learns that courage is not the absence of fear, but the willingness to pursue the things that fascinate him despite his uncertainty. By trusting himself, he begins sharing his discoveries and embracing adventures that once intimidated him.

Core Belief: The world is full of amazing things waiting to be discovered.

Core Misbelief: If I make a mistake or look foolish, people will laugh at me or think less of me.

Core Traits: Curious, Shy, Thoughtful

Defining Sentence: ”Fortune favors those will to take the chance.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To discover that curiosity is worth pursuing even when success isn't guaranteed, and that mistakes are a natural part of learning.

Favorite Location: Graythorne River near the Winding Banks Inn

Fears: Felix fears disappointing others, being laughed at for his interests, and failing at something after finally finding the courage to try it. While naturally optimistic, he often imagines the worst possible outcome before taking his first step.

Goals: To discover everything Blackmere has to offer, build a collection of fascinating natural treasures, and one day become brave enough to explore beyond the places that currently feel safe.

Habit: Flips a coin across his knuckles whenever he’s thinking.

History: Felix has spent his entire life growing up at the Winding Banks Inn with his parents, Frederick and Helen. Surrounded by travelers, he developed a fascination with stories told by travelers, unusual objects they carried, and the places beyond Blackmere they described. Although he eagerly collects interesting feathers, stones, shells, flowers, and keepsakes, his naturally timid personality often prevents him from sharing his excitement with strangers. His best friend, Milo, is often the first person he trusts enough to show his newest discoveries.

Hobbies: Collecting feathers, stones, flowers, shells, and insects; exploring riverbanks; reading adventure stories; watching wildlife; sketching discoveries; organizing collections; asking questions about the world.

Motivation: Felix wants to understand the world around him. Every new discovery reminds him that there is always something wonderful waiting to be found if he can find the courage to pursue it.

Personality Colors: Periwinkle, Blush Pink, Sky Gray

Personality Summary: Felix is an endlessly curious child whose imagination is much larger than his confidence. He sees wonder in everyday objects and delights in discovering things that others overlook, yet often hesitates to share his excitement out of fear that people won't understand. His kindness, optimism, and empathy make him easy to befriend once he feels comfortable, and his gentle enthusiasm often encourages others to see the beauty in ordinary moments.

Public Persona: Most residents know Felix as the shy child from the Winding Banks Inn who is usually carrying a pocket full of interesting treasures. Although quiet around unfamiliar people, he lights up whenever someone shows genuine interest in one of his discoveries.

Secret: Felix secretly dreams of becoming an explorer and keeping a journal of every remarkable thing he discovers, but he worries that everyone will think his dream is childish or impossible.

Speaking Tone: Guarded

Strength: Empathy

Weakness: Timid
