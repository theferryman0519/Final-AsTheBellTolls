# Personality Information

Character Arc: Finding the Courage to Explore

Character Arc Start State: Felix is endlessly curious about the world around him, but his fear of embarrassment and failure keeps many of his dreams and discoveries to himself.

Character Arc End State: Felix learns that courage is not the absence of fear, but the willingness to pursue the things that fascinate him despite his uncertainty. By trusting himself, he begins sharing his discoveries and embracing adventures that once intimidated him.

Core Belief: The world is full of amazing things waiting to be discovered.

Core Misbelief: If I make a mistake or look foolish, people will laugh at me or think less of me.

Core Traits: Curious, Shy, Thoughtful

Defining Sentence: ”Fortune favors those will to take the chance.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To discover that curiosity is worth pursuing even when success isn't guaranteed, and that mistakes are a natural part of learning.

Favorite Location: Graythorne River near the Winding Banks Inn

Fears: Felix fears disappointing others, being laughed at for his interests, and failing at something after finally finding the courage to try it. While naturally optimistic, he often imagines the worst possible outcome before taking his first step.

Goals: To discover everything Blackmere has to offer, build a collection of fascinating natural treasures, and one day become brave enough to explore beyond the places that currently feel safe.

Habit: Flips a coin across his knuckles whenever he’s thinking.

History: Felix has spent his entire life growing up at the Winding Banks Inn with his parents, Frederick and Helen. Surrounded by travelers, he developed a fascination with stories told by travelers, unusual objects they carried, and the places beyond Blackmere they described. Although he eagerly collects interesting feathers, stones, shells, flowers, and keepsakes, his naturally timid personality often prevents him from sharing his excitement with strangers. His best friend, Milo, is often the first person he trusts enough to show his newest discoveries.

Hobbies: Collecting feathers, stones, flowers, shells, and insects; exploring riverbanks; reading adventure stories; watching wildlife; sketching discoveries; organizing collections; asking questions about the world.

Motivation: Felix wants to understand the world around him. Every new discovery reminds him that there is always something wonderful waiting to be found if he can find the courage to pursue it.

Personality Colors: Periwinkle, Blush Pink, Sky Gray

Personality Summary: Felix is an endlessly curious child whose imagination is much larger than his confidence. He sees wonder in everyday objects and delights in discovering things that others overlook, yet often hesitates to share his excitement out of fear that people won't understand. His kindness, optimism, and empathy make him easy to befriend once he feels comfortable, and his gentle enthusiasm often encourages others to see the beauty in ordinary moments.

Public Persona: Most residents know Felix as the shy child from the Winding Banks Inn who is usually carrying a pocket full of interesting treasures. Although quiet around unfamiliar people, he lights up whenever someone shows genuine interest in one of his discoveries.

Secret: Felix secretly dreams of becoming an explorer and keeping a journal of every remarkable thing he discovers, but he worries that everyone will think his dream is childish or impossible.

Speaking Tone: Guarded

Strength: Empathy

Weakness: Timid

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Felix follows a curious but cautious routine centered around the Winding Banks Inn, A. Whitmore Public School, the nearby riverbank, and other familiar areas of Blackmere.

Although Felix is fascinated by exploration, wildlife, unusual objects, and stories about distant places, he rarely ventures far from familiar surroundings when alone. Much of his free time is spent searching for feathers, stones, flowers, shells, insects, and other small discoveries close to home.

Felix attends A. Whitmore Public School on weekdays while the school is open. Outside of school, he frequently explores the riverbank near the Winding Banks Inn, reads adventure and nature books, organizes his collection, sketches discoveries, watches wildlife, and spends time with Milo.

Milo is Felix's preferred exploration companion. Felix is noticeably more willing to travel through Blackmere when Milo accompanies him than when exploring alone.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Felix attends school normally on weekdays.

During ordinary rainfall, Felix avoids long explorations but remains interested in observing how the river and surrounding environment change.

Preferred Locations:

- Winding Banks Inn
- A. Whitmore Public Library on weekdays
- A. Whitmore Public School during school hours
- Sheltered areas near the Winding Banks Inn

When at home, Felix frequently watches the rain from a window, reads adventure or nature books, sketches, or organizes his collection.

He may briefly investigate puddles, wet stones, fallen leaves, or other changes near the inn if conditions remain mild.

### Rainy (Severe)

A. Whitmore Public School and A. Whitmore Public Library are closed.

Felix follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

### Snowy

Felix generally follows his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

During free time, he becomes particularly interested in footprints, animal tracks, fallen branches, snow formations, and other environmental changes.

When exploring alone, he remains close to the Winding Banks Inn. When accompanied by Milo, he may travel slightly farther through familiar parts of Blackmere.

### Snowy (Severe)

## Routine Characterization

Felix's routine is intentionally centered around the Winding Banks Inn and nearby riverfront. His world is somewhat larger than Anya's emotionally, but much smaller than Kira's geographically.

Felix wants to explore. His hesitation is what stops him.

When alone, most of his exploration occurs close to the Winding Banks Inn, particularly along the nearby riverbank. This gives him somewhere that feels adventurous without requiring him to stray far from home.

His Wednesday visit to A. Whitmore Public Library reinforces another side of his curiosity. Felix is fascinated not only by physically discovering things but also by reading about animals, geography, explorers, and distant places he is not yet confident enough to visit himself.

The Winding Banks Inn is more than Felix's residence. Growing up around travelers is one of the major reasons he dreams about exploration, so several parts of his weekly routine deliberately place him in the inn's common spaces watching visitors, overhearing stories, or wondering about the objects travelers carry.

Milo plays a particularly important role in Felix's schedule. Felix is willing to travel farther when Milo accompanies him, making Saturday his most adventurous regular day. His friendship with Kira also occasionally pulls him into situations he would probably never initiate himself.

This creates an intentional contrast between Felix and Kira. Both are extremely curious, but Kira responds to curiosity by immediately going somewhere new. Felix responds by examining what is nearby, reading about what is farther away, and wondering whether he is brave enough to see it himself.

As Felix's character arc progresses, special events may gradually place him farther from the Winding Banks Inn and familiar riverfront areas. The change should be subtle rather than immediate: he begins exploring unfamiliar locations with friends, then eventually becomes willing to visit some of them without needing someone else to lead the way.

His routine therefore visually supports his character arc from the beginning of the game. The player can see that Felix already has the curiosity of an explorer; what he lacks is the confidence to follow that curiosity as far as he would like.
