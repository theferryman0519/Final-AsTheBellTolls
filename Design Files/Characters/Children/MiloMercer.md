# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Adventures Are Better When You Pay Attention

Character Arc Start State: Milo races from one exciting idea to the next, believing every adventure is about finding something new. His boundless enthusiasm often causes him to overlook the moments happening right in front of him.

Character Arc End State: Milo learns that the greatest adventures aren't measured by how many places he visits, but by the memories he creates and the people he shares them with.

Core Belief: Every day can become an adventure if you use your imagination.

Core Misbelief: If I stop moving, the fun will be over.

Core Traits: Curious, Playful, Trusting

Defining Sentence: ”Adventure always begins with one curious step.”

Dialogue Length: Long

Emotional Need: To discover that slowing down doesn't end an adventure—it helps create memories that last long after it's over.

Favorite Location: The fields and Graythorne River bordering Mercer Farms

Fears: Milo rarely fears danger itself. Instead, he fears being left behind, missing out on exciting experiences, or watching everyone else have fun without him.

Goals: To turn every ordinary day into an unforgettable adventure and someday share stories that inspire others to see the magic in everyday life.

Habit: Whistles cheerfully while walking through town.

History: Growing up on Mercer Farms with his older sister, Kira, Milo spent much of his childhood following her on adventures whenever he could keep up. While Kira searched for hidden places and mysteries, Milo transformed ordinary walks into grand expeditions through sheer imagination. Whether building forts, chasing butterflies, or pretending sticks were legendary swords, he found excitement almost anywhere. His cheerful personality and endless enthusiasm made him one of Blackmere's easiest children to befriend.

Hobbies: Playing make-believe; building forts; catching frogs and insects; climbing hay bales and trees; collecting funny-shaped sticks and rocks; drawing adventure maps; telling exaggerated stories; playing games with Felix.

Motivation: Milo wants everyone around him to experience the same excitement and wonder he feels every day. If he can make someone smile or join one of his adventures, he considers the day a success.

Personality Colors: Lemon Yellow, Aquamarine, Cloud Gray

Personality Summary: Milo is endlessly cheerful, imaginative, and full of infectious energy. He has a remarkable ability to transform even the simplest outing into an exciting adventure, drawing friends into elaborate games and stories with effortless enthusiasm. Although he rarely worries about the future, his eagerness to chase the next exciting idea often prevents him from fully appreciating the adventure he's already having. As he grows, he learns that the best memories are made by slowing down enough to truly experience them.

Public Persona: Everyone knows Milo as the child who's always laughing, running, or inviting someone to join his newest adventure. His imagination is so contagious that even ordinary errands can become exciting when he's around.

Secret: Milo secretly keeps an adventure journal filled with drawings, maps, and stories from his expeditions. Although he dreams of sharing it someday, he's afraid people will think his adventures are too ordinary to be interesting.

Speaking Tone: Energetic

Strength: Joyful

Weakness: Aloof

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Milo follows an energetic and playful routine centered around Mercer Farms, A. Whitmore Public School, the fields and riverbank bordering Mercer Farms, and places where he can spend time with Felix and the other children.

Milo rarely approaches the outdoors as a serious expedition. Instead, he transforms familiar fields, trees, riverbanks, paths, rocks, and ordinary objects into imaginary adventures. A fallen branch can become a legendary sword, a cluster of hay bales can become a fortress, and a short walk through the fields can become an expedition to somewhere entirely invented.

Milo attends A. Whitmore Public School on weekdays while the school is open. Outside of school, he frequently plays make-believe, builds forts, catches frogs and insects, climbs around the farm, collects funny-shaped sticks and stones, draws adventure maps, and invents games for his friends.

Felix is Milo's preferred playmate and closest friend. While Felix tends to approach exploration cautiously, Milo naturally turns their time together into playful adventures without pressuring Felix to venture farther than he is comfortable going.

Kira also influences Milo's routine. He occasionally follows his older sister on smaller adventures, but Milo is more interested in the fun they have together than in discovering or documenting something important.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Milo attends school normally on weekdays.

During ordinary rainfall, Milo reduces how far he travels but does not consider the weather a reason for the day to become boring.

He frequently turns puddles, dripping roofs, muddy paths, and the sound of the rain into parts of imaginary games.

Preferred Locations:

- Mercer Farms Residence
- Mercer Farms
- Winding Banks Inn
- A. Whitmore Public School during school hours

When conditions remain mild, Milo may play outside near his home for short periods but avoids extended time along the riverbank.

### Rainy (Severe)

A. Whitmore Public School is closed.

Milo follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

He does not play near the river or travel through Blackmere during severe rainfall.

### Windy

Milo generally follows his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

Because Milo likes windy weather, he spends additional free time outdoors whenever conditions are safe.

He particularly enjoys:

- Running through the Mercer Farms Fields
- Watching leaves and grass move in the wind
- Inventing games involving imaginary storms, ships, or flying creatures
- Seeing how far light objects such as leaves will travel
- Playing outdoors with Felix, Kira, or other friends

Windy weather makes Milo more likely to remain outdoors rather than returning home early.

### Snowy

Milo generally follows his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

He treats ordinary snow as another opportunity for make-believe, creating imaginary forts, trails, creatures, kingdoms, or expeditions out of the changed landscape.

He remains relatively close to Mercer Farms unless accompanied by friends or family.

### Snowy (Severe)

A. Whitmore Public School is closed.

Milo follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

He does not make optional trips away from Mercer Farms during dangerous snowfall.

## Routine Characterization

Milo's routine is intentionally adventurous without making him another version of Kira.

Kira explores because she wants to discover what is actually there. Milo explores because his imagination can transform what is already there into something extraordinary.

This distinction keeps the Mercer siblings from feeling interchangeable. Kira might inspect an unfamiliar trail because she thinks it leads somewhere important. Milo might spend an entire afternoon in an ordinary field because, in his imagination, that field has become a distant kingdom, a monster-filled wilderness, or the site of a legendary expedition.

The Mercer Farms Fields and nearby riverbank therefore function as Milo's primary play spaces. Unlike Kira, he does not need to travel into Gloamwood Forest or Gloamwood Depths to feel as though he has gone on an adventure.

His activities also reflect his comparatively lower diligence and duty. Milo helps with small farm chores, but he frequently turns those chores into games or becomes distracted by something more interesting. This contrasts with Kira, who is somewhat more capable of completing a task before racing toward her next discovery.

Felix is central to Milo's weekly routine. Wednesday and Saturday regularly bring the two together, but their friendship works differently for each character. Felix gains courage from having Milo beside him, while Milo gains someone who willingly participates in the imaginary worlds he creates.

Saturday is Milo's primary friendship day. Rather than undertaking Kira's serious Gloamwood expedition, Milo spends much of the day with Felix transforming familiar parts of the River Wharf District and Blackmere into an elaborate imagined adventure.

Sunday deliberately keeps Milo closer to Mercer Farms. His character arc ultimately concerns appreciating the people and moments already around him, so having one regular day centered on his family, familiar fields, Kira, and the nearby river gives his schedule an important emotional foundation.

Milo's secret adventure journal also appears repeatedly throughout his routine. Unlike Kira's practical explorer's journal, Milo's journal contains exaggerated maps, drawings, stories, characters, and memories. The two siblings may both appear to be drawing maps, but the purpose of those maps is entirely different.

His fondness for windy weather provides another distinctive behavior. Where some residents may seek shelter, Milo sees the moving grass, swirling leaves, and gusts as material for another game or story.

As Milo's character arc progresses, his schedule does not need to make him less energetic or imaginative. Instead, special events can increasingly show him remaining with friends or family after an activity rather than immediately racing toward the next one, listening when someone else speaks, or recording not only what happened during an adventure but who was there with him.

His routine therefore supports the core lesson of his arc: Milo already knows how to make almost anything exciting. What he eventually learns is how to slow down long enough to recognize why those adventures matter.
