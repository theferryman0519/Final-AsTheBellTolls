# Personality Information

Character Arc: Finding Confidence in His Own Judgment

Character Arc Start State: Adrian constantly questions his own decisions, preferring to observe and research rather than take action. He trusts facts more readily than he trusts himself.

Character Arc End State: Adrian learns to trust his own judgment, realizing that knowledge has little value if he never acts upon it. He becomes confident enough to share his ideas and preserve Blackmere's history with conviction.

Core Belief: Knowledge deserves to be preserved and shared.

Core Misbelief: If I make the wrong decision, I could cause irreversible harm.

Core Traits: Anxious, Kind, Reflective

Defining Sentence: ”Every book remembers someone.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To realize that thoughtful decisions do not have to be perfect to be worthwhile, and that confidence grows through action rather than certainty.

Favorite Location: On the banks of the Graythorne River near the library reading

Fears: Adrian fears making irreversible mistakes, disappointing those who rely on his expertise, and discovering that his years of research have little value to anyone else.

Goals: To preserve Blackmere's history, expand the library into a place where knowledge is accessible to everyone, and someday publish his own historical research.

Habit: Reaches for a notebook whenever he learns something interesting.

History: Adrian has spent the last couple years working at the A. Whitmore Public Library, where he quietly became one of Blackmere's foremost experts on local history. The Great Flood reinforced his belief that knowledge can disappear as easily as buildings, motivating him to preserve every journal, photograph, and document he could recover. Although respected for his intelligence and compassion, Adrian often second-guesses himself, choosing to revise his work repeatedly rather than risk making a mistake.

Hobbies: Reading historical journals; restoring damaged books; researching Blackmere's history; writing observations in personal notebooks; birdwatching in quiet parks; collecting antique bookmarks; drinking tea while reading.

Motivation: Adrian believes every story preserved today becomes a gift to future generations. He wants to ensure that Blackmere's history—and the people who shaped it—are never forgotten.

Personality Colors: Dusty Blue, Apricot, Lilac Gray

Personality Summary: Adrian is thoughtful, compassionate, and deeply introspective. He approaches life with patience and careful consideration, preferring to listen before speaking and observe before acting. Although his intelligence makes him an invaluable source of knowledge, his tendency to overthink often causes him to doubt himself. Through his journey, Adrian discovers that wisdom is not simply knowing the right answer—it is having the confidence to act on what he knows.

Public Persona: Most residents know Adrian as Blackmere's quiet librarian who always seems to have the answer to any historical question. While approachable and kind, he rarely seeks the spotlight and is happiest helping others from behind the scenes.

Secret: Adrian has spent years writing a comprehensive history of Blackmere, but he has never shown anyone the manuscript because he worries it isn't accurate—or important—enough to be published.

Speaking Tone: Gentle

Strength: Insightful

Weakness: Over-thinker
