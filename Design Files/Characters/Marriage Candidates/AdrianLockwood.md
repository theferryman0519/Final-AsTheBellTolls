# Personality Information

Character Arc: Finding Confidence in His Own Judgment

Character Arc Start State: Adrian constantly questions his own decisions, preferring to observe and research rather than take action. He trusts facts more readily than he trusts himself.

Character Arc End State: Adrian learns to trust his own judgment, realizing that knowledge has little value if he never acts upon it. He becomes confident enough to share his ideas and preserve Blackmere's history with conviction.

Core Belief: Knowledge deserves to be preserved and shared.

Core Misbelief: If I make the wrong decision, I could cause irreversible harm.

Core Traits: Anxious, Kind, Reflective

Defining Sentence: ”Every book remembers someone.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To realize that thoughtful decisions do not have to be perfect to be worthwhile, and that confidence grows through action rather than certainty.

Favorite Location: On the banks of the Graythorne River near the library reading

Fears: Adrian fears making irreversible mistakes, disappointing those who rely on his expertise, and discovering that his years of research have little value to anyone else.

Goals: To preserve Blackmere's history, expand the library into a place where knowledge is accessible to everyone, and someday publish his own historical research.

Habit: Reaches for a notebook whenever he learns something interesting.

History: Adrian has spent the last couple years working at the A. Whitmore Public Library, where he quietly became one of Blackmere's foremost experts on local history. The Great Flood reinforced his belief that knowledge can disappear as easily as buildings, motivating him to preserve every journal, photograph, and document he could recover. Although respected for his intelligence and compassion, Adrian often second-guesses himself, choosing to revise his work repeatedly rather than risk making a mistake.

Hobbies: Reading historical journals; restoring damaged books; researching Blackmere's history; writing observations in personal notebooks; birdwatching in quiet parks; collecting antique bookmarks; drinking tea while reading.

Motivation: Adrian believes every story preserved today becomes a gift to future generations. He wants to ensure that Blackmere's history—and the people who shaped it—are never forgotten.

Personality Colors: Dusty Blue, Apricot, Lilac Gray

Personality Summary: Adrian is thoughtful, compassionate, and deeply introspective. He approaches life with patience and careful consideration, preferring to listen before speaking and observe before acting. Although his intelligence makes him an invaluable source of knowledge, his tendency to overthink often causes him to doubt himself. Through his journey, Adrian discovers that wisdom is not simply knowing the right answer—it is having the confidence to act on what he knows.

Public Persona: Most residents know Adrian as Blackmere's quiet librarian who always seems to have the answer to any historical question. While approachable and kind, he rarely seeks the spotlight and is happiest helping others from behind the scenes.

Secret: Adrian has spent years writing a comprehensive history of Blackmere, but he has never shown anyone the manuscript because he worries it isn't accurate—or important—enough to be published.

Speaking Tone: Gentle

Strength: Insightful

Weakness: Over-thinker

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Adrian follows a structured and dependable routine centered around 3 Resident Lane, A. Whitmore Public Library, and quiet areas near the Graythorne River.

As Blackmere's librarian, Adrian spends most weekdays maintaining the library, assisting visitors, restoring damaged books, organizing historical materials, and researching the town's past. Even after his official workday ends, he frequently continues reading or writing privately.

Adrian prefers calm environments and predictable routines. He rarely seeks out crowded social gatherings without a reason, but he enjoys quiet conversations with people he trusts.

The Graythorne River near the library is Adrian's preferred place to unwind. He often brings a book or notebook there after work and uses the quiet surroundings to read, think, or work through ideas that have been occupying him.

Adrian lives at 3 Resident Lane with Sabrina Fairchild. Because Sabrina is his best friend, their shared home is an important part of his routine rather than simply somewhere he sleeps.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Adrian likes ordinary rain and generally follows his normal weekday or weekend routine.

On weekdays, he particularly enjoys the quiet atmosphere inside A. Whitmore Public Library during rainfall.

After work, he usually skips his riverside reading session and returns to 3 Resident Lane, where he reads beside a window, drinks tea, or continues historical research.

Preferred Locations:

- A. Whitmore Public Library
- 3 Resident Lane
- Winding Banks Inn

Rainy weather makes Adrian slightly more likely to remain indoors and continue reading beyond his normal routine.

### Rainy (Severe)

A. Whitmore Public Library is closed.

Adrian follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

### Snowy

Adrian generally follows his normal schedule.

After work, he may shorten or skip outdoor riverside reading and instead return to 3 Resident Lane.

He occasionally spends a short period observing the snow-covered river or town before returning indoors.

### Snowy (Severe)

A. Whitmore Public Library is closed.

Adrian follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal weekday or weekend schedule.

### Windy

Adrian dislikes windy weather.

He avoids reading outdoors beside the Graythorne River and returns directly home after work.

On weekends, outdoor reading and birdwatching are replaced with reading, writing, or research at 3 Resident Lane.

## Routine Characterization

Adrian's routine is deliberately dependable and repetitive in its broad structure because routine provides him with comfort.

His weekday life revolves around A. Whitmore Public Library. Unlike a character who simply stands behind a counter for the entire workday, Adrian moves conceptually between assisting visitors, cataloging materials, restoring books, researching local history, and preserving damaged documents.

His frequent historical research reinforces that Adrian is more than simply a shopkeeper or librarian. Preserving Blackmere's history is central to his identity.

The Graythorne River serves as the primary contrast to his working life. Adrian goes there not to accomplish another task, but to slow down. Reading beside the water, birdwatching, and writing observations provide some of the few parts of his routine where he is not actively trying to ensure that something is correct.

His shared residence with Sabrina is also important. Because Sabrina is Adrian's best friend, their evenings together reinforce a close relationship that exists independently of the player. They eat together, talk about their work, and provide one another with companionship despite both having their own lives.

Friday is Adrian's most social regular evening, but even then he chooses the Winding Banks Inn rather than a loud or highly energetic activity. This allows him to maintain friendships without behaving more socially than his personality supports.

Saturday is Adrian's primary independent research day. With the library closed, he occasionally visits Blackmere Town Hall during its operating hours to investigate civic records before retreating to the river to read and think.

Sunday is intentionally quieter. Adrian reads for pleasure, spends time beside the river, organizes his own materials, and prepares mentally for the coming week.

Adrian's manuscript appears repeatedly throughout his routine because it represents his central insecurity. He is not failing to write it—he is constantly writing, reviewing, correcting, and revising it. His problem is that he never believes the work is ready.

This means his character arc can eventually be reflected through subtle changes in animation and event behavior. Early Adrian may repeatedly cross out passages, compare multiple sources, hesitate before shelving a decision, or reopen a notebook he just closed. Later Adrian can still be thoughtful without constantly undoing his own conclusions.

Marriage also does not erase Adrian's identity. He remains Blackmere's librarian, continues his research, maintains his friendship with Sabrina, reads beside the river, and works on his historical manuscript while building a new life with the player.

His routine therefore reinforces the central conflict of his character: Adrian already possesses knowledge, discipline, compassion, and good judgment. The part he has yet to develop is the confidence to believe that those qualities are enough.
