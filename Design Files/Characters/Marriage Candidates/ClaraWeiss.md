# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Perfection Is Never Finished

Character Arc Start State: Clara believes every design can always be improved. She constantly revises her work, chasing perfection and often exhausting herself before allowing anything to be considered complete.

Character Arc End State: Clara learns that great work isn't defined by endless refinement, but by knowing when a creation is ready to serve the people it was meant for.

Core Belief: Beautiful design has the power to improve people's lives.

Core Misbelief: If my work isn't perfect, it isn't good enough.

Core Traits: Ambitious, Creative, Driven

Defining Sentence: ”We honor the past by building a better future.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To accept that progress and purpose are more valuable than perfection, and that finished work creates more change than flawless plans.

Favorite Location: At her drawing table in Weiss Design Studio

Fears: Clara fears wasting her potential by creating something ordinary. She worries that one overlooked flaw could define years of hard work.

Goals: To help rebuild Blackmere into a town future generations will admire and to leave behind architecture that outlives her.

Habit: Studies buildings with her head tilted, imagining improvements.

History: Clara inherited both her passion and talent for architecture at a young age, quickly becoming known for her innovative ideas and relentless work ethic. The Great Flood strengthened her resolve to rebuild Blackmere stronger than before, but it also reinforced her tendency to overwork herself in pursuit of perfection. She pours countless hours into every blueprint, often revising excellent designs long after they're ready to be built.

Hobbies: Sketching architectural concepts; restoring historical blueprints; visiting construction sites; photography; collecting unusual building materials; studying historic architecture; woodworking.

Motivation: Clara wants every building she designs to improve the lives of the people who use it. To her, architecture is about creating places where communities can thrive for generations.

Personality Colors: Slate Blue, Copper, Ivory

Personality Summary: Clara is confident, ambitious, and endlessly creative. She approaches every challenge as an opportunity to improve upon what already exists, believing thoughtful design can shape the future of an entire community. Although her determination inspires those around her, her pursuit of perfection often leads her to overwork herself and delay projects that are already exceptional. Over time, she learns that the greatest architects don't create flawless buildings—they create places people love.

Public Persona: Most residents know Clara as the brilliant architect who always seems to be carrying rolled blueprints under one arm. She's respected for her talent, admired for her work ethic, and known for setting impossibly high standards for herself.

Secret: Clara keeps a locked portfolio containing dozens of unfinished designs she has never shown anyone because she believes they still aren't good enough.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Visionary

Weakness: Over-doing
