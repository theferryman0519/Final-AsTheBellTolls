# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Perfection Is Never Finished

Character Arc Start State: Clara believes every design can always be improved. She constantly revises her work, chasing perfection and often exhausting herself before allowing anything to be considered complete.

Character Arc End State: Clara learns that great work isn't defined by endless refinement, but by knowing when a creation is ready to serve the people it was meant for.

Core Belief: Beautiful design has the power to improve people's lives.

Core Misbelief: If my work isn't perfect, it isn't good enough.

Core Traits: Ambitious, Creative, Driven

Defining Sentence: ”We honor the past by building a better future.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To accept that progress and purpose are more valuable than perfection, and that finished work creates more change than flawless plans.

Favorite Location: At her drawing table in Weiss Design Studio

Fears: Clara fears wasting her potential by creating something ordinary. She worries that one overlooked flaw could define years of hard work.

Goals: To help rebuild Blackmere into a town future generations will admire and to leave behind architecture that outlives her.

Habit: Studies buildings with her head tilted, imagining improvements.

History: Clara inherited both her passion and talent for architecture at a young age, quickly becoming known for her innovative ideas and relentless work ethic. The Great Flood strengthened her resolve to rebuild Blackmere stronger than before, but it also reinforced her tendency to overwork herself in pursuit of perfection. She pours countless hours into every blueprint, often revising excellent designs long after they're ready to be built.

Hobbies: Sketching architectural concepts; restoring historical blueprints; visiting construction sites; photography; collecting unusual building materials; studying historic architecture; woodworking.

Motivation: Clara wants every building she designs to improve the lives of the people who use it. To her, architecture is about creating places where communities can thrive for generations.

Personality Colors: Slate Blue, Copper, Ivory

Personality Summary: Clara is confident, ambitious, and endlessly creative. She approaches every challenge as an opportunity to improve upon what already exists, believing thoughtful design can shape the future of an entire community. Although her determination inspires those around her, her pursuit of perfection often leads her to overwork herself and delay projects that are already exceptional. Over time, she learns that the greatest architects don't create flawless buildings—they create places people love.

Public Persona: Most residents know Clara as the brilliant architect who always seems to be carrying rolled blueprints under one arm. She's respected for her talent, admired for her work ethic, and known for setting impossibly high standards for herself.

Secret: Clara keeps a locked portfolio containing dozens of unfinished designs she has never shown anyone because she believes they still aren't good enough.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Visionary

Weakness: Over-doing

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Clara follows a highly structured and work-intensive routine centered around Weiss Design Studio, construction and restoration sites throughout Blackmere, and the workshops of craftspeople whose work intersects with her architectural designs.

As Blackmere's architect, Clara spends most working days drafting blueprints, consulting with residents, reviewing restoration plans, studying materials, and revising designs. She is highly disciplined and rarely wastes time during the day.

Weiss Design Studio is both Clara's residence and workplace. Although the studio closes to the public at 4:00 PM, Clara frequently continues working privately long afterward.

Thursday is Weiss Design Studio's weekly closed day. Clara does not treat it as a traditional day off. Instead, she uses the day to leave her drawing table, inspect Blackmere's buildings, consult with Mara or Marcus, study construction techniques, and gather information for future designs.

Clara's greatest difficulty is stopping. Even after completing a productive workday, she frequently returns to her drawing table to make one more revision.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Clara generally follows her normal schedule during ordinary rainfall.

She avoids unnecessary photography or prolonged outdoor site inspections and spends additional time inside Weiss Design Studio refining blueprints, studying historical plans, or constructing design models.

Preferred Locations:

- Weiss Design Studio
- Klein Woodworks while open
- Ironveil Forge while open
- Blackmere Town Hall while open

Rainy weather does not significantly reduce Clara's productivity.

### Rainy (Severe)

Weiss Design Studio, Klein Woodworks, and Ironveil Forge are closed to the public.

Clara follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of her normal routine.

Because Weiss Design Studio is also her residence, she remains inside privately despite the public closure.

### Snowy

Clara generally follows her normal schedule.

Outdoor inspections are shorter, but Clara sometimes deliberately studies how snow collects on roofs, paths, stairs, drainage areas, and damaged structures because weather reveals design weaknesses that may not otherwise be obvious.

### Snowy (Severe)

Weiss Design Studio, Klein Woodworks, and Ironveil Forge are closed to the public.

Clara follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of her normal routine.

### Windy

Clara generally follows her normal schedule.

When inspecting buildings, she pays additional attention to exposed structures, roofing, signs, trees near buildings, and anything else affected by strong wind.

## Routine Characterization

Clara's routine is intentionally one of the most work-intensive schedules in Blackmere.

Her profession is not merely somewhere she goes during business hours. Architecture occupies much of the way Clara thinks, observes, and interacts with the world.

Weiss Design Studio therefore dominates her week. It is her workplace, residence, favorite location, creative space, and the place where her perfectionism is most visible.

Clara reliably opens the studio at 7:00 AM and remains available throughout its public hours. Her ambition, diligence, and sense of duty make that consistency appropriate for her character.

What distinguishes Clara from a generic workaholic is that she does not simply work longer—she keeps finding new ways to improve work that is already good enough. Her evening schedule repeatedly returns her to completed blueprints, alternative concepts, additional measurements, and unnecessary refinements.

Thursday is therefore especially important. Weiss Design Studio being closed forces Clara away from her drawing table during normal business hours, but she immediately converts the supposed day off into fieldwork.

Her visits to Mara and Marcus also make professional sense rather than existing only for social variety. An architect rebuilding Blackmere would naturally need regular communication with a carpenter and blacksmith about materials, fabrication, structural elements, and what can realistically be built.

Mara's role is particularly important because she is Clara's best friend. Their relationship can comfortably blend professional consultation with genuine friendship. Clara may arrive intending to discuss timber specifications and remain longer because Mara is one of the few people capable of getting her to talk about something other than work.

Saturday evening is Clara's primary regular social period. Even then, it comes only after a full day at the studio and a walk through Blackmere studying architecture.

Sunday is deliberately not a true day off. Clara works, but the nature of the work changes. She spends more time on personal concepts, historic architecture, woodworking, and ideas that interest her rather than immediate client demands.

This also gives her locked portfolio a real place in her routine. Clara's secret is not that she lacks ideas. She has dozens of them. Her problem is that she cannot decide they are finished enough to deserve being seen.

As Clara's character arc progresses, her schedule does not need to make her less ambitious or less hardworking. Those qualities are strengths.

Instead, progression can subtly change when she stops.

Early Clara might close Weiss Design Studio at 4:00 PM and immediately resume drafting at 7:00 PM. Later events can increasingly show her leaving a blueprint alone, spending an evening with Mara or the player, or looking at a completed design without finding one more line to redraw.

Her routine therefore reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: Clara does not need to learn how to create something extraordinary. She needs to learn when extraordinary is already enough.
