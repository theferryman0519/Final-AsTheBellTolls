# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Inspiration Is Meant to Be Shared

Character Arc Start State: Dante believes the greatest ideas belong in museums and history books, where they can be admired and protected. He hesitates to share his own thoughts, convinced they could never stand beside history's greatest achievements.

Character Arc End State: Dante realizes that history is created by ordinary people willing to contribute their own ideas. Inspired by the player, he begins sharing his own discoveries and helping Blackmere create its future instead of only preserving its past.

Core Belief: The achievements of the past deserve to inspire future generations.

Core Misbelief: Nothing I create could ever be as meaningful as what already exists.

Core Traits: Idealistic, Introverted, Thoughtful

Defining Sentence: ”Wonder survives only if someone chooses to preserve it.”

Dialogue Length: Excruciating

Emotional Need: To understand that preserving history is only half of his purpose—helping create tomorrow's history is equally important.

Favorite Location: In the galleries of the Hall of Wonder

Fears: Dante fears becoming a caretaker of other people's accomplishments while never leaving behind anything worth remembering himself.

Goals: To transform the Hall of Wonder into a place that inspires innovation as much as it preserves history, while contributing meaningful discoveries of his own.

Habit: Carefully dusts exhibits while talking to visitors.

History: Dante has always been fascinated by history, artifacts, and the stories ordinary objects can tell. As curator of the Hall of Wonder, he devoted himself to preserving Blackmere's cultural heritage, especially after the Great Flood threatened to erase so much of it. Although respected for his knowledge and thoughtful exhibits, Dante often dismisses his own ideas, believing his role is to preserve greatness rather than create it.

Hobbies: Researching historical artifacts; restoring antiques; sketching exhibit layouts; reading philosophy and history; collecting unusual relics; writing exhibition notes; visiting historical landmarks.

Motivation: Dante wants every visitor to leave the museum believing the past still has something valuable to teach the present.

Personality Colors: Burgundy, Gold, Slate Gray

Personality Summary: Dante is thoughtful, articulate, and endlessly fascinated by history. He sees beauty in forgotten artifacts and believes every object carries a story worth preserving. While his intelligence and curiosity make him an exceptional curator, he often places historical achievements on such a high pedestal that he struggles to recognize the value of his own contributions. Through his journey, Dante learns that preserving history and creating history are equally meaningful pursuits.

Public Persona: Most residents know Dante as the soft-spoken curator of the Hall of Wonder who can spend hours explaining the history behind a single artifact. Although reserved, his passion becomes unmistakable whenever he speaks about the past. However, most residents are unable to compete with his sophisticated and thesaurus-laden vocabulary.

Secret: Dante has spent years developing ideas for a permanent exhibition showcasing Blackmere's recovery after the Great Flood, but he has never presented it because he worries his vision isn't worthy of standing beside the museum's historical collections.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Intelligent

Weakness: Anxious

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Dante follows a deliberate and intellectually focused routine centered around the Hall of Wonder, 5 Resident Lane, historical landmarks throughout Blackmere, and places where he can research the stories behind artifacts and objects.

As curator of the Hall of Wonder, Dante spends most working days maintaining exhibits, cataloging artifacts, researching provenance, restoring antiques, writing exhibition notes, and explaining the historical significance of objects to visitors.

Dante approaches museum work with considerable reverence. He does not simply consider artifacts valuable because they are rare; he believes even ordinary objects can reveal something meaningful about the people who once used them.

The Hall of Wonder is Dante's favorite location, and he regularly remains there for the majority of its public operating hours.

Wednesday is the Hall of Wonder's weekly closed day. Dante uses it for research, historical-site visits, exhibit planning, and private work that is difficult to complete while visitors are present.

Dante lives at 5 Resident Lane with Madison Remington and Victor Cross. Victor is Dante's best friend, so their shared residence and occasional social outings form an important counterbalance to Dante's otherwise solitary routine.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Dante likes ordinary rain and generally follows his normal schedule.

Because most of his work takes place indoors, rainfall has relatively little effect on his professional routine.

Rainy weather makes Dante more inclined to remain in the Hall of Wonder after completing individual tasks rather than taking unnecessary trips through town.

Preferred Locations:

- Hall of Wonder
- 5 Resident Lane
- Blackmere Town Hall while open
- Winding Banks Inn while open

On Wednesday, outdoor historical-site visits are replaced with additional indoor research whenever prolonged rain makes those visits impractical.

### Rainy (Severe)

The Hall of Wonder has no severe-rain closure.

Dante continues his normal museum schedule whenever the Hall of Wonder is scheduled to be open.

On Wednesday, he remains primarily at 5 Resident Lane or visits indoor research locations rather than conducting outdoor historical-site surveys.

### Snowy

Dante generally follows his normal schedule.

He may spend additional time considering how seasonal conditions affect historical buildings, monuments, and exterior artifacts, but he does not significantly alter his museum routine.

### Snowy (Severe)

The Hall of Wonder has no severe-snow closure.

Dante continues his normal museum schedule whenever the Hall of Wonder is scheduled to be open.

On Wednesday, outdoor research is replaced with work at 5 Resident Lane or other available indoor locations.

### Windy

Dante dislikes windy weather.

He avoids unnecessary outdoor historical-site visits and prefers to remain inside the Hall of Wonder, 5 Resident Lane, or another indoor location.

His museum work continues normally.

## Routine Characterization

Dante's routine is deliberately centered around the Hall of Wonder because the museum is not merely his workplace. It represents the way he understands his own purpose.

Where Adrian preserves written knowledge, Dante preserves objects and the stories attached to them.

Much of Dante's working day therefore involves more than standing inside the museum. He inspects displays, dusts exhibits, restores antiques, catalogs donations, researches provenance, writes interpretive notes, rearranges galleries, and speaks with visitors.

His habit of carefully dusting exhibits while talking to people can appear repeatedly throughout these work periods and gives him an immediately recognizable museum animation.

Dante also spends unusually long periods at work. The Hall of Wonder is open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and his diligence and devotion to the collection make it believable that he personally oversees most of that window.

Wednesday provides the necessary contrast.

Because the museum is closed, Dante leaves the controlled environment of its galleries and investigates the history that still exists throughout Blackmere. He studies buildings, civic records, monuments, landmarks, and other places that could eventually contribute to future exhibits.

This keeps him from feeling like a character who only exists inside one building.

His private Great Flood recovery exhibition is also deliberately embedded into his routine.

Dante does not merely have an abstract idea for the exhibit. He repeatedly researches it, sketches it, organizes material for it, and revises it. The reason it never appears publicly at the beginning of the game is not lack of effort; it is his belief that his own vision is not worthy of being placed alongside the achievements he admires.

Victor plays an important social role in Dante's week.

As Dante's best friend and housemate, Victor is one of the few people around whom Dante can comfortably leave the formal curator persona behind. Their Friday meal and quieter conversations at home give Dante a social life without making an introverted character unrealistically outgoing.

Living with Madison also gives Dante regular exposure to someone whose profession and personality are far more practical and immediate than his own historical interests, creating a useful contrast inside 5 Resident Lane.

Dante's extraordinarily long and sophisticated dialogue also makes sense within his routine. When the player catches him in a gallery, historical site, or archival setting, he is almost always surrounded by something he can explain at unreasonable length.

Unlike Clara, Dante's problem is not that he cannot decide when his work is finished.

His problem is that he places the achievements of other people above his own.

As his character arc progresses, his schedule does not need to pull him away from history. Instead, events can gradually change what he does with that history.

Early Dante researches, preserves, catalogs, and admires.

Later Dante begins designing new exhibits, presenting his own interpretations, asking others for feedback, and eventually treating the recovery of Blackmere itself as history worth documenting while it is still being created.

His routine therefore reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: Dante does not need to stop looking backward. He needs to realize that the people living in the present—including himself—are already creating the history that someone else may one day choose to preserve.
