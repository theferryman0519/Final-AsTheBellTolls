# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Being Yourself Is Always Worth It

Character Arc Start State: Leo freely expresses himself through his clothing, creativity, and cheerful personality, but criticism lingers with him far longer than he lets anyone see. Whenever someone dismisses his work or makes fun of him, he quietly wonders if he should become someone easier to accept.

Character Arc End State: Leo embraces the idea that authenticity is more valuable than universal approval. Rather than changing himself to fit others' expectations, he learns that the right people will appreciate him precisely because he is genuine.

Core Belief: Everyone deserves to express who they truly are.

Core Misbelief: If people reject what I create, they might really be rejecting me.

Core Traits: Creative, Playful, Sensitive

Defining Sentence: ”The finest things are stitched together one thread at a time.”

Dialogue Length: Long

Emotional Need: To separate his self-worth from the opinions of others and realize that vulnerability is a strength, not a weakness.

Favorite Location: In Town Square around the fountains

Fears: Leo fears becoming someone who hides his personality simply to avoid criticism. More than failure, he fears losing the confidence to express himself honestly.

Goals: To make Thread & Thimble a place where everyone can discover clothing that helps them feel comfortable, confident, and uniquely themselves.

Habit: Measures fabric with his fingers without thinking.

History: Leo discovered his love of sewing and fashion at a young age, fascinated by how clothing could tell a story before someone ever spoke. After spending time away studying tailoring and design, he returned to Blackmere to reopen Thread & Thimble with fresh ideas and a belief that fashion should celebrate individuality rather than conformity. While his outgoing personality makes him one of the town's most approachable residents, he quietly takes criticism of his work to heart, often questioning whether his creativity is truly appreciated.

Hobbies: Designing seasonal clothing collections; sketching outfits in cafés; embroidery; dyeing fabrics with flowers; attending local festivals; photography; people-watching for fashion inspiration.

Motivation: Leo wants everyone who walks into Thread & Thimble to leave feeling more confident than when they arrived.

Personality Colors: Cornflower Blue, Lavender, Coral

Personality Summary: Leo is warm, expressive, flamboyant, and effortlessly social. He finds joy in helping others discover styles that reflect who they are and believes creativity should be shared without fear of judgment. His playful humor and infectious enthusiasm make him one of Blackmere's easiest people to befriend, yet beneath his cheerful exterior lies a sensitive heart that struggles to separate criticism of his work from criticism of himself. Through his journey, Leo learns that authenticity is more meaningful than approval.

Public Persona: Most residents know Leo as the energetic tailor who remembers everyone's favorite colors, enthusiastically compliments new outfits, and somehow convinces even the shyest customers to try something unexpected.

Secret: Leo keeps a trunk filled with bold clothing designs he has never displayed because they're too personal. He worries that if people dislike his most authentic creations, he'll lose the confidence to keep designing.

Speaking Tone: Compassionate

Strength: Expressive

Weakness: Vulnerable

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Leo follows a creative, social, and highly expressive routine centered around Thread & Thimble, Town Square, and the people of Blackmere.

As Blackmere's tailor, Leo spends most working days designing clothing, assisting customers, altering garments, sewing, embroidering, arranging displays, and helping residents find clothing that makes them feel comfortable and confident.

Thread & Thimble is both Leo's residence and workplace. Although the shop closes to the public at 7:00 PM, Leo frequently continues sketching, sewing, or experimenting with personal designs afterward.

Unlike many of Blackmere's more work-focused residents, Leo deliberately seeks inspiration outside his workplace. He enjoys people-watching, photography, flowers, seasonal colors, festivals, and observing how residents express themselves through clothing.

Town Square, particularly the area around the fountains, is Leo's favorite place. He frequently visits the square to sketch outfits, observe people, photograph interesting combinations of colors and textures, or simply socialize.

Monday is Thread & Thimble's weekly closed day. Leo uses it as a combination of creative day, social day, and personal day rather than treating it as another full workday.

Leo is highly sociable and maintains friendships throughout Blackmere. Roland is his best friend, so Leo regularly makes time to visit or spend an evening with him.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Leo generally follows his normal schedule during ordinary rainfall.

He does not mind rain, although he spends less time outdoors in Town Square after work.

Rainy evenings usually replace his fountain visits with additional time at Thread & Thimble, where he sketches, embroiders, experiments with fabrics, or works on personal clothing designs.

Rain also provides Leo with visual inspiration through umbrellas, coats, wet stone, reflected colors, and the different clothing residents choose for poor weather.

Preferred Locations:

- Thread & Thimble
- Winding Banks Inn
- Indoor social locations while available

### Rainy (Severe)

Thread & Thimble is closed to the public.

Leo follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal routine.

Because Thread & Thimble is also his residence, he remains inside privately despite the public closure.

Leo dislikes severe rain and does not make optional trips through Blackmere during dangerous conditions.

### Snowy

Leo likes ordinary snow and generally follows his normal schedule.

Snowy weather particularly interests him because of winter clothing, layered outfits, scarves, coats, gloves, boots, and the way bright colors contrast against the snow.

After work, Leo may spend a shorter period around Town Square observing winter clothing and taking photographs before returning home.

### Snowy (Severe)

Thread & Thimble is closed to the public.

Leo follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal routine.

### Windy

Leo generally follows his normal schedule.

Outdoor sketching and photography sessions are shortened when strong wind makes working with paper or fabric inconvenient.

## Shop Closure Override

Thread & Thimble is closed every Monday.

Because Thread & Thimble is also Leo's residence, the closure prevents normal public business but does not prevent Leo from remaining inside privately.

Leo's normal Monday schedule already functions as his weekly Shop Closure Override.

Unlike several other business owners, Leo deliberately avoids turning his closed day into another complete workday.

He uses Monday primarily for:

- Personal clothing designs
- Sketching near the Town Square fountains
- Photography
- People-watching
- Visiting Roland
- Spending time with friends
- Looking for colors and textures that inspire future clothing
- Working on creative ideas without customer expectations

Leo may still sketch or sew on Monday, but the work is primarily recreational and personally expressive rather than commissioned.

If severe weather occurs on Monday, the Severe Weather Schedule takes priority.

## Routine Characterization

Leo's routine is intentionally one of the most socially active schedules in Blackmere.

Thread & Thimble remains the center of his professional life, but Leo is not someone who would willingly spend every waking hour inside his shop.

His work itself is social.

Leo does not merely sell clothing. He talks with customers, remembers their preferences, suggests unexpected combinations, performs alterations, and tries to help people find clothing that makes them feel more confident.

This reflects his belief that fashion should help people express themselves rather than force them into conformity.

Town Square provides the strongest contrast to his working hours.

The fountains are Leo's favorite location because they allow him to combine several things he loves at once: people-watching, fashion, photography, conversation, color, movement, and creative inspiration.

His visits there are therefore not generic wandering.

Leo may be sketching someone's silhouette, noticing a color combination, photographing reflected light, complimenting a resident's outfit, or simply enjoying being around people.

Monday is particularly important to his characterization.

Thread & Thimble being closed gives Leo one day when his creativity does not have to satisfy a customer. He sketches what interests him, photographs whatever catches his attention, spends time with Roland, socializes, and allows himself to enjoy fashion rather than treating it exclusively as a profession.

Roland's place in the schedule is also intentional.

Because Roland is Leo's best friend, Leo should maintain that relationship independently of the player. His regular Monday visit reinforces that these characters have lives and relationships that continue whether or not the player participates in them.

Leo's private trunk of designs is embedded directly into his Sunday routine.

These designs should not feel like something that suddenly exists when the player reaches the appropriate relationship level. The player can potentially notice that Leo regularly works privately after closing, but the significance of those sessions is not revealed until later.

The distinction between Leo's public and private design work is important.

During normal shop hours, Leo is confident, enthusiastic, playful, and eager to show people what he has made.

When working from the trunk, his behavior can become quieter.

He may hold a garment up, study it, make an adjustment, hesitate, and eventually put it away rather than placing it on display.

That small behavioral difference visually communicates the insecurity underneath his otherwise expressive personality.

His schedule should also make him one of Blackmere's easiest residents to encounter naturally. Between Thread & Thimble, Town Square, Winding Banks Inn, visits with Roland, and festivals, Leo participates visibly in town life.

This fits his extremely high sociability and warmth without making him feel as though he exists solely to entertain other people.

As Leo's character arc progresses, his schedule does not need to make him more expressive.

He already knows exactly who he wants to be.

Instead, progression can gradually change which designs he is willing to let other people see.

Early Leo keeps his boldest work inside the private trunk.

Later events may show pieces from that collection sitting temporarily on his worktable.

Eventually, one might appear on a mannequin.

After completing his arc, some of those designs can become part of Thread & Thimble's ordinary displays.

Nothing about the designs themselves needed to change.

Only Leo's willingness to let them be seen did.

His routine therefore reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: Leo does not need to become someone worthy of acceptance. He needs to trust that being fully himself is worth the possibility that not everyone will approve.
