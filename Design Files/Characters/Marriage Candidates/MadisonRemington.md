# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Leadership Is Meant to Be Shared

Character Arc Start State: Madison believes a leader must carry every burden herself. Having been entrusted with succeeding Blackmere's late sheriff, she refuses to rely on others, convinced that every mistake is hers alone to prevent.

Character Arc End State: Madison learns that true leadership is built on trust, not solitude. By allowing others to support her and accepting that she doesn't have to replace Ian Thorne, she becomes the sheriff Blackmere needs in her own right.

Core Belief: People deserve to feel safe, and it's my responsibility to protect them.

Core Misbelief: If I fail to protect this town, I'll prove they chose the wrong person to succeed Ian.

Core Traits: Confident, Disciplined, Protective

Defining Sentence: ”People sleep easier when someone stays awake.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To understand that she wasn't chosen to become another Ian Thorne—she was chosen because Blackmere needed Madison Remington.

Favorite Location: Around the different paths throughout Blackmere

Fears: Madison fears making a decision that results in someone else's suffering. She also fears never living up to the trust Beatrice and the people of Blackmere placed in her when they asked her to become sheriff.

Goals: To build a community where every resident feels safe enough to pursue their dreams, knowing someone is always looking out for them.

Habit: Adjusts her belt before a difficult conversation or task.

History: Madison spent years serving as the Chief Ranger of Morvanya, where she was responsible for protecting its forests, trails, wildlife, and the people who traveled through them. When the Great Flood devastated Blackmere, she joined the region's recovery efforts, coordinating rescues, securing dangerous areas, and helping displaced families. Among those lost was Sheriff Ian Thorne, whose death left Blackmere without its longtime protector. More than a year later, with the town steadily rebuilding but still lacking a sheriff, Mayor Beatrice Whitmore asked Madison to fill the vacant position. Though she had never served in law enforcement, Madison accepted the responsibility, determined to honor Ian's legacy while protecting Blackmere as it entered a new chapter.

Hobbies: Trail hiking; horseback riding; maintaining survival and rescue equipment; target practice; camping beneath the stars; woodworking; reading maps and regional history.

Motivation: Madison wants every resident to feel secure enough to build a life in Blackmere without fear, just as Ian once made the town feel before the flood.

Personality Colors: Navy Blue, Forest Green, Bronze

Personality Summary: Madison is a confident and dependable leader who places duty above personal comfort. Her years as Chief Ranger taught her to remain calm under pressure and make difficult decisions when others cannot. Although she projects unwavering confidence, she quietly struggles with the immense responsibility of succeeding Blackmere's beloved former sheriff. She believes protecting others is worth any personal sacrifice, but her journey teaches her that leadership is strengthened—not diminished—by trust, vulnerability, and community.

Public Persona: Most residents know Madison as the dependable sheriff who always seems to arrive exactly when she's needed. Calm, decisive, and fair, she has earned the town's respect through consistency rather than authority. Even those who knew Ian Thorne have come to trust that Blackmere remains in capable hands.

Secret: Madison still carries Ian Thorne's old sheriff's badge in her desk drawer. Whenever she questions one of her decisions, she quietly takes it out as a reminder of the responsibility she inherited—and wonders whether she's truly earned the right to wear the badge that replaced it.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Decisive

Weakness: Isolated

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Madison follows a disciplined and highly active routine centered around Blackmere Town Hall, regular patrols throughout Blackmere, and periodic inspections of the surrounding region.

As Blackmere's sheriff, Madison divides her working time between administrative responsibilities at Town Hall and physically patrolling the community.

Madison does not believe that protecting Blackmere can be accomplished from behind a desk. She regularly walks the town's roads and paths, checks public areas, speaks with residents, inspects potentially hazardous locations, and keeps herself familiar with changes occurring throughout the community.

Her years as Chief Ranger of Morvanya continue to influence how she approaches her position. She pays particular attention to trails, waterways, damaged structures, isolated areas, weather conditions, and anything that could become a safety concern.

Blackmere Town Hall is open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM every day. Madison regularly begins and ends her official workday there, but unlike Mayor Beatrice, she spends significant portions of those hours outside the building.

Madison lives at 5 Resident Lane with Dante and Victor.

Although Madison maintains friendships throughout Blackmere, she does not spend much time casually socializing. Her closest relationships are with Beatrice and Lena, and both play important but different roles in her life.

Madison's greatest difficulty is allowing herself to stop being responsible for everyone around her.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Madison generally follows her normal schedule during ordinary rainfall.

Rain does not prevent her from patrolling, but she modifies her routes to pay additional attention to:

- Slippery paths
- Rising water
- Drainage problems
- Damaged structures
- Muddy roads
- Areas previously affected by flooding

She spends less time on unnecessary administrative work and more time checking conditions throughout Blackmere.

Madison carries appropriate rain and emergency equipment while patrolling.

### Rainy (Severe)

Madison dislikes severe rain but considers it one of the most important conditions in which she must remain on duty.

She follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of her normal routine.

Her patrols become shorter and more targeted rather than stopping entirely.

Because of Blackmere's history with the Great Flood, severe rainfall makes Madison particularly vigilant around waterways, damaged structures, roads, and low-lying areas.

### Snowy

Madison generally follows her normal schedule.

She modifies patrols to inspect:

- Slippery roads and paths
- Accumulating snow
- Blocked entrances
- Isolated residents
- Damaged roofs or structures
- Areas where travel may become difficult

### Snowy (Severe)

Madison follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of her normal routine.

She dislikes severe snow but remains actively involved in protecting the community.

Travel outside is limited to essential inspections and emergencies.

### Windy

Madison generally follows her normal schedule.

She pays additional attention to:

- Loose signs
- Damaged trees
- Unstable structures
- Roofing
- Debris on roads and paths
- Other hazards created by strong wind

## Regional Patrol Override

Madison periodically conducts longer patrols outside Blackmere because of her experience as the former Chief Ranger of Morvanya.

These patrols replace portions of her normal Thursday schedule when appropriate.

Possible patrol locations include:

- Gloamwood Forest
- Steelridge Watch
- Graythorne Lake
- Areas surrounding Mercer Farms
- Roads and trails connecting Blackmere to the surrounding region

Regional patrols are professional safety inspections rather than recreational exploration.

During these patrols, Madison may:

- Inspect trails
- Check bridges and crossings
- Look for storm damage
- Assess dangerous terrain
- Check travel routes
- Inspect signs and markers
- Monitor areas affected by restoration work
- Speak with residents working outside Blackmere

Madison generally returns to Blackmere Town Hall before its 5:00 PM closing time to document her findings.

She does not routinely enter Gloamwood Depths or Ashfall Mines as part of ordinary patrols unless a specific event, emergency, or quest requires it.

## Routine Characterization

Madison's routine is intentionally one of the most mobile professional schedules in Blackmere.

Although Blackmere Town Hall is her workplace, Madison should not behave like a character whose profession requires her to remain behind a desk.

Her favorite location is not Town Hall.

It is the network of paths running throughout Blackmere.

That distinction is important.

Madison feels most useful when she is moving through the community, observing what has changed, noticing problems before they become emergencies, and remaining accessible to residents who may need her.

Her patrol routes also draw directly from her history as Chief Ranger of Morvanya.

Madison approaches Blackmere almost as though the entire town is a territory under her care. She notices damaged railings, muddy paths, unsafe construction areas, rising water, loose signs, unfamiliar activity, and anything else that could become a problem.

Her schedule therefore alternates between patrol and documentation.

Madison observes something in the field, returns to Town Hall, records it, coordinates with Beatrice or another resident, and then goes back out.

This creates a very different professional rhythm from Beatrice even though both work from Blackmere Town Hall.

Beatrice governs Blackmere.

Madison watches over it.

Her relationship with Beatrice should also be visible throughout her routine.

Because Beatrice is one of Madison's best friends as well as the mayor, their regular morning meetings serve both a professional and personal purpose. Beatrice trusts Madison with the town's safety, while Madison trusts Beatrice enough to discuss concerns she may not raise with everyone else.

Lena serves a different role.

As Madison's mentor and another best friend, Lena represents a connection to Madison's ranger experience and to the person she was before becoming sheriff. Events and occasional schedule overrides involving Lena can therefore provide some of the few situations where Madison is not automatically the person expected to have every answer.

Madison's relatively low sociability also matters.

She is respected and approachable, but she does not need frequent evenings at the inn or long social visits throughout the week. Her Friday dinner provides a regular opportunity to connect with others without turning her into an extroverted character.

Her hobbies mostly reinforce this independence.

Equipment maintenance, woodworking, map reading, hiking, horseback riding, and camping all allow Madison to decompress while still doing something practical.

This is also why she has difficulty truly resting.

Even her walk home becomes another patrol.

Even cleaning her equipment becomes preparation for the next emergency.

Even reading a map can become planning tomorrow's route.

The Sunday-night badge routine gives her internal conflict a physical presence within her ordinary life.

Ian Thorne's badge should not suddenly materialize when the player reaches the appropriate relationship event.

Madison already has it.

Every Sunday, after another week spent trying to protect the town he once protected, she quietly takes it from the drawer.

She does not wear it.

She does not display it.

She simply looks at it.

Early in her arc, that moment represents comparison.

She is asking herself whether Ian would have made the same decisions and whether Blackmere would have been safer if he were still there.

As her character arc progresses, the animation can subtly change.

She may spend less time looking at the badge.

Eventually, she may open the drawer without taking it out.

After completing her arc, the badge can remain where it belongs: as something that honors Ian rather than something Madison uses to measure her own worth.

Her schedule can reflect the same progression in other ways.

Early Madison personally performs almost every inspection and repeatedly checks areas she has already visited.

Later Madison may delegate a check to someone she trusts, accept information from another resident without verifying everything herself, or occasionally return home directly after work instead of taking another unnecessary patrol.

She does not become less responsible.

She becomes a better leader because responsibility is no longer something she believes must be carried alone.

Madison's routine therefore reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: protecting Blackmere does not require her to replace Ian Thorne or personally prevent every possible mistake. The town chose Madison because of the leader she already was, and trusting other people to stand beside her does not make that leadership any weaker.
