# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Trust Is Built Together

Character Arc Start State: Mara believes the only way to guarantee something is done right is to do it herself. She rarely asks for help and keeps others at arm's length, convinced that depending on people only leads to disappointment.

Character Arc End State: Mara learns that the strongest foundations are built together. By trusting others and allowing herself to rely on them, she discovers that partnership makes both her work and her life stronger.

Core Belief: Strong foundations create lasting futures.

Core Misbelief: If I depend on other people, they'll eventually let me down.

Core Traits: Driven, Independent, Practical

Defining Sentence: ”Anything worth having is worth building well.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To realize that trusting others isn't a weakness—it allows stronger relationships and greater accomplishments than she could ever achieve alone.

Favorite Location: Amongst the trees in Gloamwood Forest

Fears: Mara fears watching something she's built fall apart because she trusted the wrong person. She also fears becoming someone who needs others more than they need her.

Goals: To rebuild Blackmere with structures that will stand for generations and prove that quality craftsmanship can outlast any hardship.

Habit: Brushes sawdust from her hands before greeting anyone.

History: Mara grew up surrounded by sawdust, lumber, and the constant rhythm of construction. Long before the Great Flood, she was learning the trade at Klein Woodworks, developing a reputation for solving problems through practical thinking and relentless effort. The destruction left behind by the flood only strengthened her resolve to rebuild Blackmere piece by piece. While many admire her reliability and craftsmanship, Mara often insists on carrying the workload herself, believing that depending on others only creates opportunities for things to go wrong.

Hobbies: Wood carving; hiking through Gloamwood Forest to study different tree species; restoring antique furniture; whittling; fishing; sketching structural ideas; camping.

Motivation: Mara wants every building she helps create to become a place where families can make memories for generations.

Personality Colors: Walnut Brown, Forest Green, Rust

Personality Summary: Mara is practical, dependable, tomboyish, and endlessly hardworking. She approaches every challenge with determination, preferring action over lengthy discussion and solutions over excuses. Her confidence and resourcefulness make her one of Blackmere's most trusted craftspeople, but her fierce independence often prevents her from accepting help even when she needs it. Through her journey, Mara learns that the strongest things in life—whether buildings or relationships—are built through trust and collaboration.

Public Persona: Most residents know Mara as the carpenter who can fix almost anything with enough lumber and determination. She's respected for her honesty, admired for her work ethic, and known for speaking plainly, even when the truth is difficult to hear.

Secret: Mara secretly keeps the first wooden sign she ever carved as a child hidden in her workshop. It's crooked, uneven, and full of mistakes, but she refuses to throw it away because it reminds her that even the strongest builders start somewhere.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Problem-solver

Weakness: Defensive

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Mara follows a practical, physically active routine centered around Klein Woodworks, restoration projects throughout Blackmere, and Gloamwood Forest.

As Blackmere's carpenter, Mara spends most working days cutting and preparing lumber, constructing fabricated items, repairing furniture, assembling structural components, maintaining tools, and completing restoration-related work.

Klein Woodworks is both Mara's residence and workplace. Although the workshop closes to the public at 5:00 PM, Mara frequently continues private projects afterward.

Mara prefers solving problems through direct action. She is less interested in long meetings or theoretical planning than in testing whether something will actually hold together once it is built.

Gloamwood Forest is Mara's favorite location. She regularly hikes there to study tree species, examine fallen timber, observe how trees respond to weather, and spend time away from the noise of town.

Friday is Klein Woodworks' weekly closed day. Mara uses it primarily for fieldwork, gathering observations, inspecting restoration projects, spending time in Gloamwood Forest, and occasionally fishing or camping.

Clara is Mara's best friend and one of her most frequent professional collaborators. Their relationship often blends friendship with discussions about architecture, materials, structural design, and what can realistically be constructed.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Mara dislikes ordinary rain but generally follows her normal workshop schedule.

Outdoor restoration inspections and trips into Gloamwood Forest are shortened or replaced with indoor work.

Rainy weather gives Mara additional time for:

- Furniture restoration
- Tool maintenance
- Wood carving
- Fabricated-item construction
- Structural sketching
- Organizing lumber and materials

She avoids unnecessary time outdoors because wet conditions make both construction work and lumber handling more difficult.

### Rainy (Severe)

Klein Woodworks is closed to the public.

Mara follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of her normal routine.

Because Klein Woodworks is also her residence, she remains inside privately despite the public closure.

Gloamwood Forest itself remains available, but Mara does not make recreational forest trips during severe rain.

### Snowy

Mara generally follows her normal schedule.

She shortens outdoor work and pays additional attention to:

- Weight accumulating on structures
- Warped or stressed wood
- Exterior supports
- Roof framing
- Moisture damage
- Paths around construction sites

She may still visit Gloamwood Forest briefly when conditions are safe.

### Snowy (Severe)

Klein Woodworks is closed to the public.

Mara follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of her normal routine.

She avoids optional trips outside Blackmere during dangerous snowfall.

### Windy

Mara likes windy weather and generally follows her normal schedule.

On her free time, she becomes more likely to visit Gloamwood Forest and observe:

- How different trees move under wind load
- Flexible versus brittle branches
- Fallen limbs
- Root stability
- Natural stress patterns in trunks

Windy weather can directly inspire structural ideas for future construction.

## Shop Closure Override

Klein Woodworks is closed every Friday.

Because Klein Woodworks is also Mara's residence, the closure prevents normal public business but does not prevent Mara from remaining inside privately.

Mara's normal Friday schedule already functions as her weekly Shop Closure Override.

Unlike a traditional day off, Mara uses Friday primarily for:

- Hiking through Gloamwood Forest
- Studying tree species
- Observing fallen timber
- Sketching structural ideas
- Inspecting restoration sites
- Fishing when appropriate
- Camping or outdoor recreation during special schedule variants
- Maintaining a connection to the natural materials she works with every day

Friday is intentionally different from Mara's normal workshop days.

Her time in Gloamwood Forest is not simply about gathering lumber. She studies living trees, damaged trees, growth patterns, bark, flexibility, weathering, and other characteristics that influence how she understands wood as a building material.

If severe weather occurs on Friday, the Severe Weather Schedule takes priority.

## Routine Characterization

Mara's routine is intentionally built around physical work.

Where Clara spends much of her professional life designing what should be built, Mara spends hers making sure it can actually be built.

Her workshop schedule therefore emphasizes:

- Cutting lumber
- Shaping components
- Repairing furniture
- Constructing fabricated items
- Maintaining tools
- Completing structural work
- Solving practical problems

Mara is highly diligent and dependable, so she reliably keeps Klein Woodworks open throughout its public hours.

However, her independence creates an important weakness inside that work ethic.

Mara routinely takes on jobs herself that could reasonably be shared with someone else.

She carries lumber herself.

She repairs tools herself.

She checks measurements herself.

She stays involved in every step because trusting another person with the work feels riskier than simply doing more.

This means her overworking should feel different from Clara's.

Clara overworks because she believes a design can always be improved.

Mara overworks because she believes a task is safest in her own hands.

That distinction should remain visible throughout their routines.

Friday is particularly important because it shows a side of Mara that is not centered on customer work.

Gloamwood Forest is her favorite location and one of the strongest expressions of her personality.

Mara understands wood not only as a construction material but as something living.

She notices grain, flexibility, bark, growth, weathering, damage, and the way different species respond to their environment.

Her hikes therefore support both her profession and her enjoyment of the outdoors without turning every visit into another job.

Windy weather is especially suitable for her.

Where many residents simply experience strong wind as an inconvenience, Mara watches trees bend and studies how natural structures distribute force.

That kind of practical observation fits the way she thinks.

Her friendship with Clara also deserves a visible place in the schedule.

Clara and Mara work in closely related professions, but their personalities naturally create friction and balance.

Clara may arrive with a precisely revised blueprint.

Mara may look at it for ten seconds and immediately point out the part that will be difficult to build.

Clara thinks in terms of design.

Mara thinks in terms of execution.

Because they are best friends, those disagreements do not weaken the relationship. They are part of why the partnership works.

Marcus serves a similar but more technical purpose.

Mara depends on metal hardware, tools, fasteners, reinforcement, and fabricated components, so regular professional contact with the forge makes sense without forcing an artificial social relationship.

Mara's childhood wooden sign is also incorporated directly into her Sunday routine.

The object matters because it contradicts the standard she applies to herself now.

It is crooked.

It is uneven.

It contains mistakes.

And Mara kept it anyway.

Early in her arc, the sign reminds her that she once had to learn everything herself.

Later, its meaning can change.

It can become evidence that imperfect work can still matter and that someone does not have to prove their competence every moment of their life.

Her character progression can also appear subtly in the way she works.

Early Mara may refuse assistance animations, carry materials alone, repeatedly check someone else's work, or immediately take over a task.

Later Mara can increasingly hand another person a tool, allow someone to hold a piece in place, leave a shared task unfinished without personally checking every detail, or ask someone for help before reaching the point of exhaustion.

She does not become less capable.

She becomes secure enough in her own ability that she no longer needs to prove she can carry everything alone.

Mara's routine therefore reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: strong foundations are not strong because one person built every part of them. They are strong because each part can depend on the others.
