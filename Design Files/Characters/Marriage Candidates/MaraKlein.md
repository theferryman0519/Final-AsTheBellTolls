# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Trust Is Built Together

Character Arc Start State: Mara believes the only way to guarantee something is done right is to do it herself. She rarely asks for help and keeps others at arm's length, convinced that depending on people only leads to disappointment.

Character Arc End State: Mara learns that the strongest foundations are built together. By trusting others and allowing herself to rely on them, she discovers that partnership makes both her work and her life stronger.

Core Belief: Strong foundations create lasting futures.

Core Misbelief: If I depend on other people, they'll eventually let me down.

Core Traits: Driven, Independent, Practical

Defining Sentence: ”Anything worth having is worth building well.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To realize that trusting others isn't a weakness—it allows stronger relationships and greater accomplishments than she could ever achieve alone.

Favorite Location: Amongst the trees in Gloamwood Forest

Fears: Mara fears watching something she's built fall apart because she trusted the wrong person. She also fears becoming someone who needs others more than they need her.

Goals: To rebuild Blackmere with structures that will stand for generations and prove that quality craftsmanship can outlast any hardship.

Habit: Brushes sawdust from her hands before greeting anyone.

History: Mara grew up surrounded by sawdust, lumber, and the constant rhythm of construction. Long before the Great Flood, she was learning the trade at Klein Woodworks, developing a reputation for solving problems through practical thinking and relentless effort. The destruction left behind by the flood only strengthened her resolve to rebuild Blackmere piece by piece. While many admire her reliability and craftsmanship, Mara often insists on carrying the workload herself, believing that depending on others only creates opportunities for things to go wrong.

Hobbies: Wood carving; hiking through Gloamwood Forest to study different tree species; restoring antique furniture; whittling; fishing; sketching structural ideas; camping.

Motivation: Mara wants every building she helps create to become a place where families can make memories for generations.

Personality Colors: Walnut Brown, Forest Green, Rust

Personality Summary: Mara is practical, dependable, tomboyish, and endlessly hardworking. She approaches every challenge with determination, preferring action over lengthy discussion and solutions over excuses. Her confidence and resourcefulness make her one of Blackmere's most trusted craftspeople, but her fierce independence often prevents her from accepting help even when she needs it. Through her journey, Mara learns that the strongest things in life—whether buildings or relationships—are built through trust and collaboration.

Public Persona: Most residents know Mara as the carpenter who can fix almost anything with enough lumber and determination. She's respected for her honesty, admired for her work ethic, and known for speaking plainly, even when the truth is difficult to hear.

Secret: Mara secretly keeps the first wooden sign she ever carved as a child hidden in her workshop. It's crooked, uneven, and full of mistakes, but she refuses to throw it away because it reminds her that even the strongest builders start somewhere.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Problem-solver

Weakness: Defensive
