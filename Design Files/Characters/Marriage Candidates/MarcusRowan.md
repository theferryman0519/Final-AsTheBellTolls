# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That He Deserves to Build a Life for Himself

Character Arc Start State: Marcus believes his own wants should always come after his responsibilities. As long as the forge is running and the town has what it needs, he considers his own happiness unimportant.

Character Arc End State: Marcus realizes that caring for himself isn't selfish. By allowing himself to pursue his own dreams and relationships, he discovers that a fulfilled life makes him an even better craftsman and friend.

Core Belief: The people who rely on me deserve my very best.

Core Misbelief: My own happiness can wait. Other people need me more.

Core Traits: Hardworking, Honest, Loyal

Defining Sentence: ”If it’s work making, it’s worth making right.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To understand that his value isn't measured only by what he provides for others.

Favorite Location: At the base of Ironveil Peak

Fears: Marcus fears letting people down more than failure itself. The thought of someone being unable to work, cook, or rebuild because he didn't finish something on time weighs heavily on him.

Goals: To craft tools and equipment sturdy enough to serve Blackmere for generations while helping the town rebuild one project at a time.

Habit: Spins a hammer before setting it down.

History: Marcus was raised around the heat and rhythm of the forge, learning early that quality work requires patience, honesty, and consistency. Rather than chasing fame as a master smith, he chose to remain in Blackmere, believing the town needed dependable craftspeople more than another celebrated artisan. After the Great Flood, Marcus devoted himself to repairing tools, reinforcing damaged structures, and forging whatever the community required to rebuild. He rarely complains about the workload, quietly believing that as long as others can move forward, the long hours are worth it.

Hobbies: Camping near Ironveil Peak; fishing for salmon; chopping firewood; restoring antique tools; hiking mountain trails; carving simple wooden handles for his finished tools; tending a small vegetable garden behind the forge.

Motivation: Marcus wants to give people reliable tools they can depend on, believing that every well-made hammer, shovel, or horseshoe helps someone build a better future.

Personality Colors: Charcoal Gray, Silver, Pine Green

Personality Summary: Marcus is dependable to his core. Honest, hardworking, and quietly kind, he's the sort of person who shows his care through actions rather than words. He never seeks recognition for his work and is happiest when he knows someone else can succeed because of something he built. His greatest flaw is believing that his own needs should always come last, making it difficult for him to accept help or pursue happiness for himself.

Public Persona: Residents know Marcus as the blacksmith who never cuts corners and never breaks a promise. If he says something will be finished tomorrow, everyone trusts that it will be. Though he's a man of few words, his reliability has earned him tremendous respect throughout Blackmere.

Secret: Marcus has quietly turned down opportunities to apprentice under renowned master blacksmiths outside Morvanya. He tells everyone he never wanted to leave, but the truth is he couldn't bring himself to abandon the people who depended on him after the flood.

Speaking Tone: Gentle

Strength: Reliable

Weakness: Simple

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Marcus follows a dependable and physically demanding routine centered around Ironveil Forge, Ironveil Peak, and the practical needs of Blackmere's residents.

As Blackmere's blacksmith, Marcus spends most working days forging and repairing tools, producing metal components, maintaining equipment, upgrading the player's tools, restoring antique implements, and completing work needed for the town's reconstruction.

Ironveil Forge is both Marcus's residence and workplace. Although the forge closes to the public at 5:00 PM, Marcus regularly continues smaller private tasks afterward.

Marcus is exceptionally reliable and takes deadlines seriously. If he tells someone a tool or component will be ready, he organizes his day around making sure that promise is kept.

Tuesday is Ironveil Forge's weekly closed day. Marcus uses it for outdoor work, personal maintenance, trips to Ironveil Peak, fishing, gathering information about available ore and materials, and completing tasks that are difficult to handle while running the forge.

Ironveil Peak is Marcus's favorite location. He goes there to hike, camp, fish, gather firewood, inspect natural materials, and spend time away from the constant demands of the forge.

Marcus also maintains a small vegetable garden behind Ironveil Forge and tends it regularly when his schedule allows.

Mara is Marcus's best friend and friendly rival. Their professional relationship often involves comparing craftsmanship, exchanging practical opinions, and challenging one another to produce better work.

Claudia is Marcus's friend and mentor, making her an important professional connection regarding mining, ore, and the practical realities of obtaining the materials he works with.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Marcus generally follows his normal schedule during ordinary rainfall.

Most of his work takes place indoors, so regular rain has little effect on Ironveil Forge.

Outdoor trips to Ironveil Peak, restoration sites, or the garden are shortened when necessary.

Rainy weather gives Marcus additional time for:

- Tool restoration
- Forging
- Handle carving
- Equipment maintenance
- Inventory organization
- Completing overdue repairs

### Rainy (Severe)

Ironveil Forge is closed to the public.

Marcus follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal routine.

Because Ironveil Forge is also his residence, he remains inside privately despite the public closure.

Marcus dislikes severe rain and avoids unnecessary travel.

### Snowy

Marcus likes ordinary snow and generally follows his normal schedule.

He enjoys the colder conditions around Ironveil Peak and may spend slightly longer outdoors after work when conditions are safe.

Snow also gives Marcus additional reasons to inspect:

- Outdoor tools
- Metal fittings
- Hinges
- Structural fasteners
- Equipment exposed to moisture and cold

### Snowy (Severe)

Ironveil Forge is closed to the public.

Marcus follows the Severe Weather Schedule instead of his normal routine.

Ironveil Peak remains available, but Marcus does not make recreational trips there during dangerous snowfall.

### Windy

Marcus generally follows his normal schedule.

Outdoor work is shortened if strong wind makes forge deliveries, firewood handling, or hiking inconvenient.

Ironveil Forge is closed every Tuesday.

Because Ironveil Forge is also Marcus's residence, the closure prevents normal public business but does not prevent Marcus from remaining inside privately.

Marcus's normal Tuesday schedule already functions as his weekly Forge Closure Override.

He uses Tuesday primarily for:

- Visiting Ironveil Peak
- Hiking
- Fishing
- Gathering firewood
- Inspecting naturally exposed ore and stone
- Maintaining outdoor equipment
- Tending his vegetable garden
- Performing light tool maintenance
- Taking time away from customer work

Tuesday is intentionally Marcus's most personal day.

Although many of his activities are still practical, they are not centered on fulfilling customer orders.

If severe weather occurs on Tuesday, the Severe Weather Schedule takes priority.

## Routine Characterization

Marcus's routine is intentionally steady and dependable.

Where several other craftspeople have schedules built around experimentation, fieldwork, or perfectionism, Marcus is defined by consistency.

Residents know where to find him.

They know when Ironveil Forge will open.

They know that if he promises something will be completed, it will be completed.

That reliability is one of Marcus's greatest strengths.

It is also the source of his central problem.

Marcus does not merely care about doing his work well.

He believes other people's needs should automatically take priority over his own.

This means his overworking should look different from both Clara and Mara.

Clara continues working because she believes the design could still be better.

Mara continues working because she believes relying on someone else creates unnecessary risk.

Marcus continues working because someone is waiting for what he promised.

If a resident needs a repaired hoe tomorrow, Marcus stays late.

If a restoration project needs another bracket, he makes it.

If someone else's tool breaks, his own evening plans become optional.

His Monday and Friday evenings demonstrate this behavior by keeping him at the forge slightly beyond closing when work remains unfinished.

Sunday makes the conflict even more explicit.

Marcus finally begins making something for himself.

Then another person's need appears.

He sets his own project aside.

Nothing dramatic happens.

That is precisely why the moment fits him.

Marcus has likely made that same small decision hundreds of times.

Ironveil Peak provides the strongest contrast to his professional life.

It is Marcus's favorite location because nothing there requires him to produce something for someone else.

He can hike.

He can fish.

He can gather firewood.

He can sit quietly.

The mountain gives him space to exist without immediately measuring the usefulness of his time.

Tuesday therefore needs to remain important even if Marcus sometimes performs practical tasks while there.

It is the closest thing he has to a day that belongs to him.

His relationship with Mara also deserves a distinct place in the schedule.

They are best friends and rivals because their professions overlap enough for each to understand exactly how skilled the other is.

Mara works primarily with wood.

Marcus works primarily with metal.

Both are practical.

Both are hardworking.

Both care deeply about quality.

Their rivalry can emerge through small details:

- Who made the stronger component
- Whose repair lasted longer
- Whether a wooden or metal solution is more appropriate
- Who finished a difficult job first
- Whether the other is taking an unnecessarily complicated approach

The rivalry remains friendly because neither doubts the other's competence.

Claudia fills a different role.

As Marcus's mentor, Claudia connects him directly to the materials he depends on.

Marcus may understand exactly how iron behaves at the forge, while Claudia understands where that iron came from, what conditions produced it, and what it took to extract it safely.

Occasional trips to Ashfall Mines therefore deepen Marcus's profession without turning him into a miner.

His hobbies also reinforce his personality.

Tool restoration reflects his respect for useful objects.

Handle carving supports his craftsmanship.

Gardening gives him a productive but quieter routine.

Fishing provides one of the few activities where there is no deadline.

Camping and hiking move him away from the constant sound of the forge.

These quieter interests are important because Marcus's character arc is not about becoming less responsible.

He should remain one of Blackmere's most reliable people.

The progression is about allowing reliability to coexist with a personal life.

Early Marcus may routinely stay late.

Later Marcus may occasionally close Ironveil Forge at exactly 5:00 PM and actually leave.

Early Marcus interrupts a personal project when another nonurgent task appears.

Later Marcus writes the task down for tomorrow and keeps working on his own project.

Early Marcus treats Tuesday as a chance to accomplish different kinds of work.

Later he might actually sit at Ironveil Peak and do nothing useful for a while.

That small change would represent enormous growth for him.

His routine therefore reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: Marcus does not need to stop being someone Blackmere can depend on. He needs to understand that his own life is also something worth building.
