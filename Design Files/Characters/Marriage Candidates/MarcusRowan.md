# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That He Deserves to Build a Life for Himself

Character Arc Start State: Marcus believes his own wants should always come after his responsibilities. As long as the forge is running and the town has what it needs, he considers his own happiness unimportant.

Character Arc End State: Marcus realizes that caring for himself isn't selfish. By allowing himself to pursue his own dreams and relationships, he discovers that a fulfilled life makes him an even better craftsman and friend.

Core Belief: The people who rely on me deserve my very best.

Core Misbelief: My own happiness can wait. Other people need me more.

Core Traits: Hardworking, Honest, Loyal

Defining Sentence: ”If it’s work making, it’s worth making right.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To understand that his value isn't measured only by what he provides for others.

Favorite Location: At the base of Ironveil Peak

Fears: Marcus fears letting people down more than failure itself. The thought of someone being unable to work, cook, or rebuild because he didn't finish something on time weighs heavily on him.

Goals: To craft tools and equipment sturdy enough to serve Blackmere for generations while helping the town rebuild one project at a time.

Habit: Spins a hammer before setting it down.

History: Marcus was raised around the heat and rhythm of the forge, learning early that quality work requires patience, honesty, and consistency. Rather than chasing fame as a master smith, he chose to remain in Blackmere, believing the town needed dependable craftspeople more than another celebrated artisan. After the Great Flood, Marcus devoted himself to repairing tools, reinforcing damaged structures, and forging whatever the community required to rebuild. He rarely complains about the workload, quietly believing that as long as others can move forward, the long hours are worth it.

Hobbies: Camping near Ironveil Peak; fishing for salmon; chopping firewood; restoring antique tools; hiking mountain trails; carving simple wooden handles for his finished tools; tending a small vegetable garden behind the forge.

Motivation: Marcus wants to give people reliable tools they can depend on, believing that every well-made hammer, shovel, or horseshoe helps someone build a better future.

Personality Colors: Charcoal Gray, Silver, Pine Green

Personality Summary: Marcus is dependable to his core. Honest, hardworking, and quietly kind, he's the sort of person who shows his care through actions rather than words. He never seeks recognition for his work and is happiest when he knows someone else can succeed because of something he built. His greatest flaw is believing that his own needs should always come last, making it difficult for him to accept help or pursue happiness for himself.

Public Persona: Residents know Marcus as the blacksmith who never cuts corners and never breaks a promise. If he says something will be finished tomorrow, everyone trusts that it will be. Though he's a man of few words, his reliability has earned him tremendous respect throughout Blackmere.

Secret: Marcus has quietly turned down opportunities to apprentice under renowned master blacksmiths outside Morvanya. He tells everyone he never wanted to leave, but the truth is he couldn't bring himself to abandon the people who depended on him after the flood.

Speaking Tone: Gentle

Strength: Reliable

Weakness: Simple
