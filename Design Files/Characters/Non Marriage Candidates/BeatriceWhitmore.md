# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Leadership Means Moving Forward

Character Arc Start State: Beatrice believes every loss Blackmere suffered during the Great Flood is ultimately her responsibility. She carries the weight of every decision she made, convinced that a better leader could have prevented more tragedy.

Character Arc End State: Beatrice learns that leadership isn't measured by never making mistakes—it's measured by giving people hope, even after unimaginable loss. By forgiving herself, she allows Blackmere to move forward alongside her.

Core Belief: A leader exists to serve the people before themselves.

Core Misbelief: If I had been a better mayor, more people would still be alive.

Core Traits: Authoritative, Dependable, Guarded

Defining Sentence: ”A community grows strongest when everyone carries a little of the weight.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To accept that she cannot change the past, and that honoring those who were lost means helping the living build a better future.

Favorite Location: Within Town Square amongst the crowds and buildings

Fears: Beatrice fears failing the people who have placed their trust in her. More than criticism or political failure, she fears making another decision that costs innocent lives.

Goals: To restore Blackmere into a thriving community where every resident has the opportunity to rebuild not only their home, but also their future.

Habit: Straightens a stack of paperwork while she thinks.

History: Born to Agatha and Roland Whitmore, two of Blackmere's founding families, Beatrice was raised with a deep sense of duty to her community. Although she never aspired to become mayor, she accepted the role because she believed Blackmere needed someone willing to put its people first. The Great Flood forever changed both the town and her, and despite leading rescue and recovery efforts, she continues to quietly blame herself for every life that was lost. Ever since, she has dedicated herself to rebuilding Blackmere, determined to create a future worthy of those the town lost.

Hobbies: Walking through Town Square before sunrise; tending the flower beds surrounding Town Hall; reading civic history and biographies; organizing community events; writing letters to neighboring towns; quietly enjoying tea with her parents.

Motivation: Beatrice wants every resident of Blackmere to believe that tomorrow can be better than yesterday, no matter what they have lost.

Personality Colors: Royal Blue, Ivory, Burgundy

Personality Summary: Beatrice is a composed and dependable leader who places the needs of Blackmere above her own. Calm under pressure and unwavering in her sense of duty, she rarely allows others to see the emotional burden she carries. While she projects confidence and authority, much of her determination is fueled by lingering guilt over the lives lost during the Great Flood. She believes leadership is a responsibility to be earned every day, often holding herself to standards no one else expects of her. Through her journey, she learns that a leader's greatest strength isn't perfection—it's the courage to keep guiding others forward despite their own regrets.

Public Persona: Most residents see Beatrice as the steady heart of Blackmere's recovery. Professional, organized, and unwaveringly dependable, she is often the first to arrive during a crisis and the last to leave once the work is finished. Though some mistake her reserved demeanor for emotional distance, those who know her understand that every difficult decision she makes comes from a genuine desire to protect the people of Blackmere.

Secret: Every year on the anniversary of the Great Flood, Beatrice quietly visits Memorial Hill before dawn with a fresh bouquet of dahlias. She reads through the names of those who were lost and privately apologizes to each of them, believing she should have done more to save them.

Speaking Tone: Guarded

Strength: Leadership

Weakness: Distrusting
