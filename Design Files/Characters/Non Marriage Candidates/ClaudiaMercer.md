# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Strength Also Means Letting Others Carry the Weight

Character Arc Start State: Claudia believes protecting her family means carrying every burden herself. She works tirelessly, convinced that if she slows down or asks for help, she's failing the people who depend on her.

Character Arc End State: Claudia learns that the strongest families aren't built on one person's sacrifice—they're built on sharing life's burdens together.

Core Belief: The people I love should never have to wonder if they'll be provided for.

Core Misbelief: If I stop being strong, my family will suffer.

Core Traits: Determined, Practical, Protective

Defining Sentence: ”The deepest foundations are the one nobody can see.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To realize that allowing her family to support her doesn't make her weaker—it allows them to show the same love she's always shown them.

Favorite Location: At home in Mercer Farms surrounded by family

Fears: Claudia fears being unable to provide for her family. More than injury or hardship, she fears becoming someone her loved ones have to take care of instead of the other way around.

Goals: To build a secure future where her family never has to experience the uncertainty and hardship she has endured.

Habit: Brushes stone dust from her gloves after leaving the mines each day.

History: Claudia has spent most of her life balancing long days in the Ashfall Mines with raising a family at Mercer Farms alongside her husband, Jonas. Known throughout Blackmere for her unmatched work ethic, she became the family's unwavering foundation, taking pride in providing through honest labor. She rarely complains about exhaustion, believing that every hour spent working is another investment in her family's future. Though fiercely dependable, Claudia often forgets that the people she's protecting are just as willing to protect her.

Hobbies: Hiking mountain trails; sharpening mining tools; woodworking with Jonas; tending the family vegetable garden; teaching Kira and Milo practical skills; cooking hearty family meals; collecting unusual minerals.

Motivation: Claudia wants to build a life where her children grow up knowing that hard work, honesty, and family will always see them through life's hardships.

Personality Colors: Slate Gray, Copper, Forest Green

Personality Summary: Claudia is steadfast, dependable, and fiercely devoted to her family. She approaches every challenge with determination and rarely complains, believing that actions speak louder than words. Her instinct is to solve problems through hard work rather than conversation, often carrying responsibilities that no one asked her to bear. Though she can appear emotionally reserved, every long day in the mines and every sacrifice she makes comes from a profound desire to give her family a safe and stable life. Her greatest lesson is learning that strength isn't measured by how much she can carry alone, but by trusting the people she loves to carry life's burdens alongside her.

Public Persona: Most residents know Claudia as one of the hardest-working people in Blackmere. She's respected for her honesty, admired for her endurance, and trusted to follow through on every promise she makes. While she isn't particularly expressive, everyone knows that if Claudia says she'll be there, they can depend on her without question.

Secret: Claudia quietly keeps every drawing, handwritten notes from Jonas, and small handmade gift Kira and Milo have ever given her tucked away in a wooden keepsake box. She rarely speaks about her emotions, but those simple reminders mean more to her than any treasure she has ever uncovered in the mines.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Endurance

Weakness: Emotional Hardness

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Claudia follows a disciplined and physically demanding routine centered around Ashfall Mines and Mercer Farms.

As a miner, Claudia spends most working days extracting ore, inspecting tunnels, maintaining mining equipment, sorting usable minerals, and helping keep work areas safe.

Ashfall Mines are available from 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM every day and have no regular or weather closures.

Claudia does not spend the entire available window underground. She deliberately returns to Mercer Farms in the afternoon so she can remain involved in her family's daily life.

Mercer Farms is Claudia's residence and favorite location.

She lives there with her husband Jonas, her daughter Kira, and her son Milo. Jasmine is also an important part of the Mercer family.

Although Claudia works extremely hard, much of that effort is motivated by her family rather than professional ambition.

Her free time is usually practical. She tends the vegetable garden, sharpens tools, cooks hearty meals, works on small woodworking projects with Jonas, teaches Kira and Milo practical skills, or organizes unusual minerals collected from the mines.

Claudia rarely takes a full day for herself. Even her lighter days usually become opportunities to finish something useful around Mercer Farms.

Her greatest difficulty is recognizing when she is carrying work or responsibility that someone else would willingly share.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Claudia generally follows her normal schedule.

Ashfall Mines and Mercer Farms remain available during ordinary rain.

Because most of Claudia's professional work takes place underground, rainfall has relatively little effect on her mining schedule.

Outdoor farm work is shortened or replaced with:

- Tool maintenance
- Woodworking
- Cooking
- Mineral sorting
- Equipment repair
- Time with her family

### Rainy (Severe)

Claudia dislikes severe rain.

Ashfall Mines remain available and have no severe-rain closure.

However, Claudia does not automatically remain underground simply because the mines are open.

She follows the Severe Weather Schedule and prioritizes staying closer to Mercer Farms.

Because of Blackmere's history with flooding, severe rain makes Claudia especially protective of her family.

### Snowy

Claudia likes ordinary snow and generally follows her normal schedule.

She enjoys the cooler conditions during the trip between Mercer Farms and Ashfall Mines.

Outdoor farm work may be shortened depending on accumulation.

### Snowy (Severe)

Ashfall Mines remain available and have no severe-snow closure.

Claudia follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

She avoids unnecessary travel and spends more time at Mercer Farms with her family.

### Windy

Claudia generally follows her normal schedule.

Outdoor gardening or repair work may be shortened if conditions become inconvenient.

## Routine Characterization

Claudia's routine is intentionally divided between two responsibilities: the work she believes provides for her family and the family she is actually working for.

She is one of Blackmere's hardest-working residents, but her favorite location being Mercer Farms is important. The mines are where she fulfills her responsibility; home is where that responsibility has meaning.

Her schedule therefore avoids keeping her underground until evening every day.

She works an intense early shift and returns home while there is still time to garden, cook, repair things, teach her children, and spend time with Jonas.

Sunday is especially important because it is the one day Claudia deliberately remains away from the mines.

Even then, she struggles to truly rest.

Her character progression can gradually change that.

Early Claudia takes over every difficult farm task herself.

Later she allows Jonas, Kira, Milo, or another person to help.

Early Claudia treats Sunday as another workday with a different location.

Later she becomes capable of spending part of it simply being with her family.

Her routine reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: providing for the people she loves does not require carrying every burden alone.
