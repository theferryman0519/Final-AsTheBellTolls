# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Duty Can Become Family

Character Arc Start State: Edward believes his purpose is to faithfully carry out the duties entrusted to him, never placing his own needs above his responsibilities. Though he deeply cares for those around him, he keeps an emotional distance, convinced that a butler should serve—not become part of the family.

Character Arc End State: Edward comes to realize that loyalty isn't diminished by affection. By allowing himself to accept the player as family rather than simply his employer, he discovers that the greatest honor of his service is the relationships it has built.

Core Belief: A life devoted to serving others is a life well lived.

Core Misbelief: If I become emotionally attached, I cannot remain objective in my duty.

Core Traits: Loyal, Observant, Well-spoken

Defining Sentence: ”A well-kept home is built from a thousand quiet acts of care.”

Dialogue Length: Long

Emotional Need: To understand that genuine loyalty and professional duty can coexist with love, friendship, and family.

Favorite Location: At the manor amongst the different rooms

Fears: Edward fears failing the promises he made to Myrtle Pendrelle, particularly his vow to protect both Pendrelle Manor and the secret of the grandfather clock. He also fears becoming unable to fulfill the duties that have defined his life.

Goals: To restore Pendrelle Manor to the warm and welcoming home it once was while ensuring the next generation is prepared to safeguard its legacy.

Habit: Straightens his gloves before speaking. Bows when leaving a room from another person.

History: Edward devoted most of his life to serving Myrtle Pendrelle as the butler of Pendrelle Manor, taking immense pride in maintaining the estate and caring for everyone who called it home. Over the years, Myrtle entrusted him with one of the manor's greatest secrets—the extraordinary power hidden within its grandfather clock. Swearing to protect that knowledge, Edward remained at the manor even after Myrtle's passing, patiently awaiting the arrival of her grandchild. Now, he serves as both caretaker and guide, determined to honor Myrtle's legacy while helping the player rebuild the manor and learn when—and how—the clock's power should be used.

Hobbies: Preparing afternoon tea; polishing antique silver; tending the manor gardens; restoring old furniture; reading classic literature; organizing the manor archives; quietly feeding birds in the courtyard.

Motivation: Edward wishes to see Pendrelle Manor become a true home once again, believing Myrtle's legacy lives on not through the building itself, but through the people who fill it with life.

Personality Colors: Navy Blue, Ivory, Mahogany

Personality Summary: Edward is the embodiment of quiet devotion. Impeccably mannered, observant, and unfailingly dependable, he approaches every task with grace and precision. Years of service have taught him that true loyalty is expressed through consistency rather than grand gestures, and he takes immense pride in fulfilling even the smallest responsibilities to the highest standard. Though his reserved nature often hides his emotions, Edward cares deeply for those entrusted to him. His journey is one of realizing that the bonds he has formed over a lifetime of service make him more than a butler—they make him family.

Public Persona: Residents know Edward as the distinguished butler of Pendrelle Manor, a man whose impeccable manners and unwavering professionalism have become legendary throughout Blackmere. Calm, courteous, and remarkably perceptive, he always seems to anticipate what others need before they ask. Though some mistake his formal demeanor for emotional distance, those who know him understand that his quiet acts of service speak louder than words ever could.

Secret: Edward possesses the knowledge of Pendrelle Manor's greatest secret: the grandfather clock's ability to manipulate time. Entrusted with its protection by Myrtle herself, he has guarded that secret for decades, believing such power should only ever be used by someone with wisdom, compassion, and restraint. Until the player arrives, he carries that burden alone.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Responsibility

Weakness: Bottling Emotions
