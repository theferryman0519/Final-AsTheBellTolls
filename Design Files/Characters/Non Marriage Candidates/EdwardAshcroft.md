# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Duty Can Become Family

Character Arc Start State: Edward believes his purpose is to faithfully carry out the duties entrusted to him, never placing his own needs above his responsibilities. Though he deeply cares for those around him, he keeps an emotional distance, convinced that a butler should serve—not become part of the family.

Character Arc End State: Edward comes to realize that loyalty isn't diminished by affection. By allowing himself to accept the player as family rather than simply his employer, he discovers that the greatest honor of his service is the relationships it has built.

Core Belief: A life devoted to serving others is a life well lived.

Core Misbelief: If I become emotionally attached, I cannot remain objective in my duty.

Core Traits: Loyal, Observant, Well-spoken

Defining Sentence: ”A well-kept home is built from a thousand quiet acts of care.”

Dialogue Length: Long

Emotional Need: To understand that genuine loyalty and professional duty can coexist with love, friendship, and family.

Favorite Location: At the manor amongst the different rooms

Fears: Edward fears failing the promises he made to Myrtle Pendrelle, particularly his vow to protect both Pendrelle Manor and the secret of the grandfather clock. He also fears becoming unable to fulfill the duties that have defined his life.

Goals: To restore Pendrelle Manor to the warm and welcoming home it once was while ensuring the next generation is prepared to safeguard its legacy.

Habit: Straightens his gloves before speaking. Bows when leaving a room from another person.

History: Edward devoted most of his life to serving Myrtle Pendrelle as the butler of Pendrelle Manor, taking immense pride in maintaining the estate and caring for everyone who called it home. Over the years, Myrtle entrusted him with one of the manor's greatest secrets—the extraordinary power hidden within its grandfather clock. Swearing to protect that knowledge, Edward remained at the manor even after Myrtle's passing, patiently awaiting the arrival of her grandchild. Now, he serves as both caretaker and guide, determined to honor Myrtle's legacy while helping the player rebuild the manor and learn when—and how—the clock's power should be used.

Hobbies: Preparing afternoon tea; polishing antique silver; tending the manor gardens; restoring old furniture; reading classic literature; organizing the manor archives; quietly feeding birds in the courtyard.

Motivation: Edward wishes to see Pendrelle Manor become a true home once again, believing Myrtle's legacy lives on not through the building itself, but through the people who fill it with life.

Personality Colors: Navy Blue, Ivory, Mahogany

Personality Summary: Edward is the embodiment of quiet devotion. Impeccably mannered, observant, and unfailingly dependable, he approaches every task with grace and precision. Years of service have taught him that true loyalty is expressed through consistency rather than grand gestures, and he takes immense pride in fulfilling even the smallest responsibilities to the highest standard. Though his reserved nature often hides his emotions, Edward cares deeply for those entrusted to him. His journey is one of realizing that the bonds he has formed over a lifetime of service make him more than a butler—they make him family.

Public Persona: Residents know Edward as the distinguished butler of Pendrelle Manor, a man whose impeccable manners and unwavering professionalism have become legendary throughout Blackmere. Calm, courteous, and remarkably perceptive, he always seems to anticipate what others need before they ask. Though some mistake his formal demeanor for emotional distance, those who know him understand that his quiet acts of service speak louder than words ever could.

Secret: Edward possesses the knowledge of Pendrelle Manor's greatest secret: the grandfather clock's ability to manipulate time. Entrusted with its protection by Myrtle herself, he has guarded that secret for decades, believing such power should only ever be used by someone with wisdom, compassion, and restraint. Until the player arrives, he carries that burden alone.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Responsibility

Weakness: Bottling Emotions

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Edward follows a highly structured and service-oriented routine centered almost entirely around Pendrelle Manor.

As the manor's butler and caretaker, Edward begins his day before most residents are awake and spends his time maintaining the estate, preparing meals and tea, organizing rooms, caring for antiques, tending the grounds, monitoring supplies, and assisting the player.

Pendrelle Manor is both Edward's residence and workplace.

His private residence is the Butler Quarters.

Edward's schedule changes somewhat as Pendrelle Manor is restored because additional rooms, services, and responsibilities become available over time.

Even during the manor's Weathered stage, Edward maintains whatever portions of the estate remain usable.

Edward rarely travels into Blackmere without a specific purpose.

Unlike most residents, his social life comes primarily through visitors to Pendrelle Manor, occasional errands, and relationships developed over decades of service.

Martin is Edward's son and best friend.

Roland is Edward's mentor and one of the people whose judgment he deeply respects.

Edward's greatest difficulty is separating service from belonging. He instinctively views almost every act of care as part of his duty rather than evidence that Pendrelle Manor and the people within it have become his family.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Edward likes ordinary rain and generally follows his normal schedule.

Because most of his responsibilities take place inside Pendrelle Manor, rainfall has little effect on his routine.

Outdoor groundskeeping and bird-feeding activities are replaced with:

- Furniture restoration
- Manor archives
- Silver polishing
- Household organization
- Reading
- Additional interior cleaning

Edward particularly enjoys preparing tea during rainy afternoons.

### Rainy (Severe)

Edward dislikes severe rain.

He follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

He remains inside Pendrelle Manor and makes repeated inspections for:

- Leaks
- Drafts
- Water intrusion
- Damaged windows
- Roof problems
- Flood-related concerns

Because of the Great Flood and his responsibility for Pendrelle Manor, severe rain makes Edward noticeably more vigilant.

### Snowy

Edward generally follows his normal schedule.

Outdoor activities are shortened, but interior household duties continue normally.

### Snowy (Severe)

Edward follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

He remains inside Pendrelle Manor and focuses on ensuring the estate remains warm, supplied, and secure.

### Windy

Edward generally follows his normal schedule.

He checks windows, doors, shutters, and exterior fixtures more frequently.

## Restoration Progression Override

Edward's responsibilities expand as Pendrelle Manor is restored.

His core daily routine remains recognizable throughout the game, but additional tasks replace portions of his normal cleaning, maintenance, or groundskeeping periods.

### Weathered

Edward follows the standard schedule.

Butler Help is available.

During this stage, Edward focuses primarily on:

- Keeping usable rooms clean
- Protecting surviving furnishings
- Maintaining the Kitchen and Pantry
- Monitoring damaged areas
- Helping the player understand what should be restored next
- Protecting the grandfather clock and its secret

Edward does not perform activities in manor areas that have not yet become functional.

### Rebuilding

Daily Advice becomes available.

Edward provides the player with a morning tip or piece of advice.

The Garden and Orchard become functional.

Edward begins including brief inspections of these areas in his morning or afternoon manor circuits.

### Recovering

Weather Forecast becomes available.

Edward begins incorporating weather information into his morning advice and household preparation.

He adjusts outdoor manor tasks when upcoming weather suggests preparation is necessary.

### Renewed

Manor Report becomes available.

Edward reviews available crop and animal information as part of his morning manor inspection.

He may briefly visit the Garden, Orchard, Barn, or Coop when those areas are currently in use.

### Growing

Lost & Found becomes available.

Edward provides the player with a different non-food ingredient item at the start of the day.

This occurs during his morning interaction with the player and does not significantly alter the remainder of his schedule.

### Prospering

Groundskeeper becomes available.

Edward begins harvesting ready crops and flowers and places the harvested items into the Pantry or Storage Chest.

Groundskeeper duties generally replace portions of his morning grounds inspection.

Edward does not harvest crops or flowers that are not ready.

### Flourishing

Butler Tunnels become available.

Edward gains access to fast-travel locations across Blackmere.

His standard routine remains primarily centered around Pendrelle Manor, but Butler Tunnel access allows special errands, events, and assistance schedules to move him between Blackmere locations more efficiently.

## Toll of Hearths Override

The Toll of Hearths festival occurs in the Pendrelle Manor Dining Hall on Day 25 of Winter.

The festival takes priority over Edward's standard routine.

Edward begins preparations earlier than normal and spends much of the day:

- Preparing the Dining Hall
- Organizing table settings
- Checking household supplies
- Preparing tea and refreshments
- Greeting guests
- Assisting with the festival
- Restoring the manor after guests leave

Because Pendrelle Manor is Edward's home and responsibility, Toll of Hearths is one of his busiest days of the year.

## Grandfather Clock Override

The grandfather clock in the Central Room is one of Edward's most important private responsibilities.

Edward periodically checks the clock as part of his standard routine.

Before the player learns the truth about the clock, these inspections appear to be ordinary care for an important Pendrelle family heirloom.

After the clock's secret is revealed, Edward's behavior may include:

- Inspecting the clock mechanism
- Monitoring its condition
- Discussing responsible use with the player
- Reviewing information Myrtle entrusted to him
- Responding to story events involving Time Manipulation

Clock-related story events take priority over Edward's normal household tasks.

## Town Restoration Routine Override

Edward's existing Manor restoration progression remains the primary influence on his household duties. Blackmere's broader restoration additionally changes how often he leaves Pendrelle Manor and how much of his time is spent responding to recovery needs.

### Weathered / Rebuilding

Edward remains strongly centered around Pendrelle Manor and essential household work. Trips into Blackmere are primarily practical errands, restoration discussions, or support for the player.

### Recovering / Renewed / Growing

As the Manor becomes more stable and Blackmere's public spaces reopen, selected afternoon blocks may include Town Square, Town Hall, the General Store, or visits connected to household and community needs.

### Prospering / Flourishing

Edward spends less time inspecting damage and more time using restored Manor and town spaces socially. Occasional garden walks, Town Square visits, Inn visits, and community errands may replace maintenance blocks, showing that he increasingly experiences Pendrelle Manor and Blackmere as home rather than only as responsibilities.

## Routine Characterization

Edward's routine is intentionally centered almost entirely on Pendrelle Manor.

Unlike residents who leave home for work, Edward's home and profession are inseparable. The player should frequently encounter him naturally while moving between manor rooms.

His schedule also changes as the manor improves. Early Edward is preserving what remains. Later Edward is actively maintaining a functioning estate filled with gardens, guests, supplies, and new responsibilities.

The grandfather clock should remain a subtle recurring part of his routine even before its importance is fully understood.

Edward's progression can appear through small behavioral changes as well.

Early Edward speaks and behaves almost exclusively as a servant fulfilling his obligations.

Later he becomes more comfortable sitting with the player, remaining for conversation after serving tea, or participating in manor life without needing to justify every moment as a duty.

His routine reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: Edward never stops being Pendrelle Manor's butler, but he gradually accepts that he is also one of the people who makes it a home.
