# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Not Everything Worth Investing In Can Be Measured

Character Arc Start State: Elise believes the safest life is one built on careful planning, calculated risks, and emotional restraint. She trusts numbers because people are unpredictable.

Character Arc End State: Elise learns that while careful planning is valuable, life's greatest rewards often come from trusting people rather than simply calculating outcomes.

Core Belief: Security comes from preparation.

Core Misbelief: If I can't predict the outcome, it's too dangerous to risk.

Core Traits: Calculating, Composed, Disciplined

Defining Sentence: ”Security is built one careful decision at a time.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To realize that vulnerability isn't recklessness, and that trusting others can be just as valuable as protecting herself.

Favorite Location: At the edge of the River Ward overlooking the Graythorne River

Fears: Elise fears making a decision that ruins someone else's future. She carries every financial approval, denied loan, and difficult conversation long after everyone else has moved on, believing one poor judgment could change a family's life forever.

Goals: To help Blackmere recover financially by ensuring families and businesses can rebuild on stable foundations instead of temporary solutions.

Habit: Organizes coins into perfect stacks while talking at her desk.

History: Elise built her reputation through discipline, precision, and unwavering professionalism, eventually becoming the trusted banker of Blackmere. Having witnessed how quickly lives can be overturned by poor planning and unexpected tragedy, she approaches every decision with careful consideration. While some mistake her caution for coldness, Elise simply believes that protecting someone's future sometimes means making difficult choices in the present.

Hobbies: Reading financial journals; balancing ledgers by hand; birdwatching along the river; solving logic puzzles; tending a small collection of potted herbs; collecting polished gemstones; evening walks along the riverbank.

Motivation: Elise wants to give the people of Blackmere the financial stability needed to rebuild their lives, believing that security provides the foundation for hope to flourish.

Personality Colors: Emerald, Charcoal, Gold

Personality Summary: Elise is intelligent, disciplined, and remarkably composed under pressure. She rarely allows emotion to cloud her judgment, preferring to approach every problem with careful analysis and thoughtful planning. Though often perceived as distant, her caution stems from a genuine desire to protect others from unnecessary hardship. She believes that hope should be supported by preparation, and while she tends to expect the worst, it is only because she wants the people around her to be ready for it. Beneath her reserved exterior is someone who quietly cares far more deeply than she lets on.

Public Persona: Most residents know Elise as the calm and meticulous banker of Blackmere. She's respected for her honesty, trusted with people's livelihoods, and admired for her ability to remain level-headed during difficult times. While her reserved demeanor can make her seem intimidating at first, those who know her understand that every decision she makes comes from a sincere desire to safeguard the town's future.

Secret: Elise keeps a private ledger documenting every resident whose loan payments she's quietly delayed, reduced, or personally covered during difficult times. She never speaks about these acts of kindness, believing true generosity shouldn't expect recognition.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Planning

Weakness: Pessimistic

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Elise follows a precise and carefully planned routine centered around Blackmere Bank & Exchange, Riverbend Fishery, and the Graythorne River.

As Blackmere's banker, Elise spends Monday through Friday managing accounts, reviewing loans, balancing ledgers, evaluating financial requests, handling Bellnotes, and helping residents and businesses make financially sustainable decisions.

Blackmere Bank & Exchange is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Monday through Friday.

The bank is closed Saturday and Sunday.

Elise generally arrives before opening so she can prepare her desk, review the day's appointments, and ensure the previous day's accounts are balanced.

She lives at Riverbend Fishery with Nora, her best friend.

Elise's favorite location is the edge of the River Ward overlooking the Graythorne River.

She frequently walks along the river before or after work, particularly when she needs time to think through a difficult financial decision.

Her hobbies include reading financial journals, balancing ledgers by hand, solving logic puzzles, birdwatching, tending potted herbs, collecting polished gemstones, and walking along the riverbank.

Elise prefers predictable routines and quiet environments.

Her weekends are intentionally less structured than her workweek, although she still has difficulty resisting financial journals, ledgers, and planning.

Her greatest difficulty is accepting that a decision can be worthwhile even when she cannot guarantee its outcome.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Elise generally follows her normal schedule.

Blackmere Bank & Exchange remains open during ordinary rain.

Morning and evening river walks are shortened or replaced with:

- Reading financial journals
- Solving logic puzzles
- Tending potted herbs
- Organizing gemstones
- Balancing personal ledgers

### Rainy (Severe)

Blackmere Bank & Exchange remains open during severe rain.

Elise follows her normal working hours but avoids unnecessary travel before and after work.

She is particularly attentive to financial concerns that severe weather may create for Blackmere's residents and businesses.

River walks and birdwatching are replaced with indoor activities.

### Snowy

Elise generally follows her normal schedule.

Blackmere Bank & Exchange remains open during ordinary snow.

River walks may be shortened depending on conditions.

### Snowy (Severe)

Blackmere Bank & Exchange is closed.

Elise follows the Severe Snow Schedule.

She remains primarily at Riverbend Fishery and uses the unexpected closure to review financial records, read, solve puzzles, and prepare for the bank's reopening.

### Windy

Elise dislikes windy weather.

She generally follows her normal professional schedule but avoids optional walks along the river.

Outdoor activities are replaced with indoor hobbies at Riverbend Fishery.

## Bank Closure Override

Blackmere Bank & Exchange is closed every Saturday and Sunday.

Elise's normal Saturday and Sunday schedules already account for these closures.

Elise does not enter the bank during normal weekend closures.

Saturday is her more genuinely recreational day and focuses on:

- River walks
- Birdwatching
- Potted herbs
- Gemstone collecting
- Logic puzzles
- Reading
- Time with Nora

Sunday is somewhat more structured.

Elise uses part of Sunday to prepare for the coming financial week and privately maintain records that she does not keep at the bank.

She still deliberately avoids turning the entire weekend into additional bank work.

## Private Ledger Override

Elise keeps a private ledger documenting residents whose loan payments she has quietly delayed, reduced, or personally covered.

The ledger is kept at Riverbend Fishery rather than openly at Blackmere Bank & Exchange.

Elise usually reviews it on Sunday afternoon when she is unlikely to be interrupted.

Possible activities include:

- Reviewing struggling accounts
- Recording delayed payments
- Reducing payment requirements
- Recording Bellnotes she personally contributed
- Recalculating repayment schedules
- Checking whether a resident's financial situation has improved

Elise does not discuss the ledger with other residents.

Early in her character progression, she treats these actions as private corrections to financial problems rather than acts of compassion.

The Private Ledger Override may be replaced by friendship or story events involving Elise's secret.

## Routine Characterization

Elise's routine is intentionally controlled.

She arrives early, works predictable hours, balances the day's accounts, and returns to the same quiet riverside home each evening.

Her river walks are important because they are one of the few parts of her life without a measurable outcome. She cannot balance the river, predict every bird she will see, or determine exactly what Nora will say.

The private ledger also reveals the contradiction beneath Elise's professional exterior. She insists decisions should be based on numbers, yet she is already quietly bending those numbers because she cares about people.

Her progression should gradually make her more comfortable acknowledging that contradiction.

Early Elise asks whether a decision is safe.

Later she begins asking whether it is worthwhile.

Her routine reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: preparation can protect a future, but sometimes building one requires trusting something that cannot be guaranteed.
