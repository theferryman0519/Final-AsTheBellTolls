# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That You Can't Pour From an Empty Cup

Character Arc Start State: Frederick believes a good host should always put everyone else first. Whether it's family, guests, or neighbors, he happily sacrifices his own time and energy to make others feel welcome.

Character Arc End State: Frederick learns that caring for himself allows him to care for others for years to come. Rest isn't selfish—it's necessary.

Core Belief: Everyone deserves a place where they feel welcome.

Core Misbelief: If someone leaves disappointed, I've failed them.

Core Traits: Family-oriented, Hospitable, Pragmatic

Defining Sentence: ”There’s always room for one more.”

Dialogue Length: Excruciating

Emotional Need: To realize that he doesn't have to solve every problem himself and that accepting help is another way of caring for those around him.

Favorite Location: At the Winding Banks Inn surrounded by family

Fears: Frederick fears becoming unable to provide the welcoming home his family and the people of Blackmere rely upon. More than losing the inn itself, he fears losing the sense of comfort and belonging it provides.

Goals: To make the Winding Banks Inn a place where every resident and traveler feels like family the moment they walk through the front door.

Habit: Wipes down the counter while talking to guests.

History: Raised in Blackmere, Frederick inherited the Winding Banks Inn with his wife, Helen, and together they've devoted themselves to making it the town's gathering place. Under the guidance of mentors like Roland and Edward, he learned that true hospitality is about more than serving meals or offering beds—it's about making people feel they belong. Whether welcoming weary travelers or celebrating milestones with neighbors, Frederick has built the inn into the warm heart of Blackmere, even if it often comes at the expense of his own well-being.

Hobbies: Experimenting with family recipes; fishing along the Graythorne River; hosting game nights; collecting regional teas; carving wooden serving boards; gardening fresh herbs; swapping stories with travelers late into the evening.

Motivation: Frederick wants every person who enters the Winding Banks Inn to leave feeling happier, more hopeful, and reminded that they'll always have a place to return to.

Personality Colors: Amber, Chestnut Brown, Forest Green

Personality Summary: Frederick is warm, generous, and endlessly welcoming. He has an uncanny ability to make strangers feel like old friends within minutes of meeting them, often remembering the smallest details about the people who visit his inn. While his cheerful demeanor rarely falters, he quietly carries the weight of ensuring everyone around him is cared for. His greatest challenge is learning that a welcoming home isn't built through endless sacrifice alone—it's built by allowing others to care for him in return.

Public Persona: To the people of Blackmere, Frederick is the friendly face behind the Winding Banks Inn and one of the town's biggest sources of comfort. Whether someone needs a hot meal, a listening ear, or simply a place to rest, they know Frederick will greet them with a smile and an open door. Many residents consider the inn the social heart of the town, and Frederick is the reason why.

Secret: Frederick keeps a small notebook hidden behind the inn's counter where he records every resident's favorite meal, birthday, and meaningful life event. He pretends to have an incredible memory, but the notebook ensures no celebration, hardship, or special occasion is ever forgotten.

Speaking Tone: Compassionate

Strength: Consistent

Weakness: Over-working
