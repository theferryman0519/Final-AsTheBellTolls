# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That You Can't Pour From an Empty Cup

Character Arc Start State: Frederick believes a good host should always put everyone else first. Whether it's family, guests, or neighbors, he happily sacrifices his own time and energy to make others feel welcome.

Character Arc End State: Frederick learns that caring for himself allows him to care for others for years to come. Rest isn't selfish—it's necessary.

Core Belief: Everyone deserves a place where they feel welcome.

Core Misbelief: If someone leaves disappointed, I've failed them.

Core Traits: Family-oriented, Hospitable, Pragmatic

Defining Sentence: ”There’s always room for one more.”

Dialogue Length: Excruciating

Emotional Need: To realize that he doesn't have to solve every problem himself and that accepting help is another way of caring for those around him.

Favorite Location: At the Winding Banks Inn surrounded by family

Fears: Frederick fears becoming unable to provide the welcoming home his family and the people of Blackmere rely upon. More than losing the inn itself, he fears losing the sense of comfort and belonging it provides.

Goals: To make the Winding Banks Inn a place where every resident and traveler feels like family the moment they walk through the front door.

Habit: Wipes down the counter while talking to guests.

History: Raised in Blackmere, Frederick inherited the Winding Banks Inn with his wife, Helen, and together they've devoted themselves to making it the town's gathering place. Under the guidance of mentors like Roland and Edward, he learned that true hospitality is about more than serving meals or offering beds—it's about making people feel they belong. Whether welcoming weary travelers or celebrating milestones with neighbors, Frederick has built the inn into the warm heart of Blackmere, even if it often comes at the expense of his own well-being.

Hobbies: Experimenting with family recipes; fishing along the Graythorne River; hosting game nights; collecting regional teas; carving wooden serving boards; gardening fresh herbs; swapping stories with travelers late into the evening.

Motivation: Frederick wants every person who enters the Winding Banks Inn to leave feeling happier, more hopeful, and reminded that they'll always have a place to return to.

Personality Colors: Amber, Chestnut Brown, Forest Green

Personality Summary: Frederick is warm, generous, and endlessly welcoming. He has an uncanny ability to make strangers feel like old friends within minutes of meeting them, often remembering the smallest details about the people who visit his inn. While his cheerful demeanor rarely falters, he quietly carries the weight of ensuring everyone around him is cared for. His greatest challenge is learning that a welcoming home isn't built through endless sacrifice alone—it's built by allowing others to care for him in return.

Public Persona: To the people of Blackmere, Frederick is the friendly face behind the Winding Banks Inn and one of the town's biggest sources of comfort. Whether someone needs a hot meal, a listening ear, or simply a place to rest, they know Frederick will greet them with a smile and an open door. Many residents consider the inn the social heart of the town, and Frederick is the reason why.

Secret: Frederick keeps a small notebook hidden behind the inn's counter where he records every resident's favorite meal, birthday, and meaningful life event. He pretends to have an incredible memory, but the notebook ensures no celebration, hardship, or special occasion is ever forgotten.

Speaking Tone: Compassionate

Strength: Consistent

Weakness: Over-working

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Frederick follows an active and highly social routine centered around Winding Banks Inn and his family.

As Blackmere's innkeeper, Frederick spends most of his day greeting guests, managing room rentals, checking guest rooms, maintaining the common areas, helping Helen and Theo, handling supplies, and making sure everyone who enters the inn feels welcome.

Winding Banks Inn is open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

The inn has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Frederick lives at Winding Banks Inn with his wife Helen, their son Felix, and Helen's brother Theo.

The inn is also Frederick's favorite location.

Unlike many residents, Frederick genuinely enjoys being at work because he does not view the inn simply as a business. He considers it an extension of his family's home and an important gathering place for Blackmere.

Frederick is responsible for room rentals and remains available at the inn for most operating hours.

He does not remain behind the counter continuously.

Throughout the day, he rotates between the front counter, guest rooms, common areas, storage, kitchen assistance, and conversations with guests.

Frederick's hobbies include experimenting with family recipes, fishing, collecting regional teas, carving wooden serving boards, gardening herbs, hosting game nights, and swapping stories with travelers.

He rarely gives himself significant time away from the inn.

His greatest difficulty is recognizing that Helen, Theo, Felix, and the rest of Blackmere do not need him to personally handle everything.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Frederick likes rainy weather and generally follows his normal schedule.

Winding Banks Inn remains open during rain.

Frederick particularly enjoys rainy days because residents and travelers tend to linger inside the inn.

Optional outdoor activities are replaced with:

- Talking with guests
- Experimenting with recipes
- Carving serving boards
- Organizing regional teas
- Additional family time
- Hosting games in the common area

### Rainy (Severe)

Frederick dislikes severe rain.

Winding Banks Inn remains open.

He stays at the inn for the entire day and becomes particularly attentive to travelers or residents seeking shelter.

Fishing and outdoor breaks are canceled.

Frederick repeatedly checks that:

- Guest rooms are prepared
- Towels and dry linens are available
- Food and warm drinks are stocked
- Travelers have somewhere safe to remain
- Felix and the rest of his family are accounted for

### Snowy

Frederick likes snowy weather.

Winding Banks Inn remains open.

He spends additional time maintaining a warm and comfortable common area and enjoys seeing residents gather inside.

Outdoor fishing or river activities are replaced when necessary.

### Snowy (Severe)

Winding Banks Inn remains open.

Frederick remains at the inn throughout the day and prioritizes shelter, warm meals, guest rooms, and keeping the common areas comfortable.

### Windy

Frederick generally follows his normal schedule.

Fishing and outdoor breaks may be replaced with indoor activities.

## Room Rental Availability

Frederick manages room rentals at Winding Banks Inn.

Rooms may only be rented before 9:00 PM.

Frederick therefore remains available somewhere within Winding Banks Inn throughout the room-rental period unless a specific schedule override places Helen or Theo in charge.

When Frederick is:

- Checking guest rooms
- Preparing supplies
- Helping in another part of the inn
- Eating with his family
- Performing another nearby inn task

He can temporarily return to the counter when the player requests a room.

Frederick does not accept new room rentals beginning at 9:00 PM.

The inn itself remains open until 10:00 PM.

## Inn Restoration Override

Frederick's routine remains centered around Winding Banks Inn throughout its restoration.

As the inn progresses, the atmosphere and scale of his work change.

### Weathered

Frederick focuses on:

- Keeping surviving guest rooms usable
- Maintaining the common area
- Welcoming travelers despite the inn's condition
- Repairing small furnishings
- Preserving the inn's reputation for hospitality

### Rebuilding

Frederick spends additional time coordinating around guest-room and common-area repairs.

### Recovering

More of his routine shifts back toward hosting as repaired rooms become comfortable again.

### Renewed

Frederick spends more evenings actively circulating among a growing number of guests.

### Growing

Decorative gardens and outdoor seating become part of Frederick's regular inspections and herb-tending routine.

### Prospering

Expanded amenities increase guest traffic and Frederick's tendency to overwork.

### Flourishing

Winding Banks Inn becomes one of Blackmere's most active gathering places.

Frederick's schedule becomes socially busier, but his character progression should simultaneously make him better at allowing Helen, Theo, and others to share responsibility.

## Routine Characterization

Frederick's routine is intentionally one of the busiest and most social in Blackmere.

The inn is simultaneously his workplace, home, favorite location, and primary way of caring for his community.

That is exactly why his schedule creates a problem for him.

There is almost always someone to greet, something to clean, a room to check, or a meal he could help prepare.

His Saturday fishing trip is therefore important. It gives Frederick a small period every week where nobody needs him to be the innkeeper.

His progression should gradually expand those moments.

Early Frederick believes good hospitality means personally ensuring everyone is satisfied.

Later he learns that trusting Helen, Theo, and others to help does not make him a worse host.

His routine reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: a person who spends his life caring for everyone else also has to allow himself to be cared for.
