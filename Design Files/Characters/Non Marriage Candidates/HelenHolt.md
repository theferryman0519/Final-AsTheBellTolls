# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Caring for Yourself Is Caring for Others

Character Arc Start State: Helen happily gives every ounce of herself to her family, friends, and guests, believing that making others happy is the greatest purpose she can fulfill. She rarely notices how exhausted she's become.

Character Arc End State: Helen learns that taking time to rest doesn't diminish her generosity—it ensures she can continue sharing it for years to come.

Core Belief: A warm meal can heal more than an empty stomach.

Core Misbelief: If someone needs me, my own needs can wait.

Core Traits: Caring, Expressive, Optimistic

Defining Sentence: ”Nothing warms the heart like good food and better company.”

Dialogue Length: Long

Emotional Need: To understand that she deserves the same compassion she so freely gives everyone else.

Favorite Location: In the Winding Banks Inn with family

Fears: Helen fears becoming unable to care for the people she loves. Whether through illness, exhaustion, or circumstance, the thought of watching others shoulder her responsibilities is far more frightening to her than hard work itself.

Goals: To make the Winding Banks Inn a place where every guest leaves with a full stomach, a lighter heart, and the feeling that they've been welcomed like family.

Habit: Jots down a recipe in a notebook when lost in thought.

History: Raised alongside her twin brother, Theo, Helen always found joy in bringing people together. After marrying Frederick, she helped transform the Winding Banks Inn into the heart of Blackmere, where every meal is prepared with care and every guest is treated like family. While her boundless generosity has become one of the inn's greatest strengths, it often leaves her forgetting to care for herself as faithfully as she cares for everyone else.

Hobbies: Experimenting with seasonal recipes; baking bread before sunrise; tending a kitchen herb garden; preserving jams and pickled vegetables; hosting holiday feasts; collecting handwritten family recipes; teaching children how to cook.

Motivation: Helen wants everyone who enters the Winding Banks Inn to experience the comfort and connection that a home-cooked meal can bring.

Personality Colors: Honey Gold, Sage Green, Cream

Personality Summary: Helen is the heart of the Winding Banks Inn, greeting every guest with genuine warmth and an infectious smile. Expressive, compassionate, and endlessly optimistic, she believes life's greatest moments are shared around a table filled with good food and good company. She instinctively puts others before herself, often working long after everyone else has gone to bed to ensure tomorrow is just as welcoming as today. Beneath her cheerful spirit lies someone who quietly struggles to accept that she deserves the same kindness she gives so freely to others.

Public Persona: Everyone in Blackmere knows Helen as the smiling chef of the Winding Banks Inn. Whether someone arrives celebrating life's happiest moments or carrying its heaviest burdens, Helen somehow knows exactly what meal will make them feel at home. Her kitchen has become a place of comfort, laughter, and countless cherished memories.

Secret: Helen keeps a handwritten cookbook filled not only with recipes but with the stories of the people who inspired them. Every favorite dish is paired with memories of birthdays, reunions, anniversaries, and quiet evenings shared with family and friends. She hopes one day to pass it on to Felix as both a cookbook and a family history.

Speaking Tone: Compassionate

Strength: Warm

Weakness: Exhausted

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Helen follows a warm, food-centered routine built around Winding Banks Inn, her family, and the meals she prepares for Blackmere.

As the chef of Winding Banks Inn, Helen begins working early and spends much of her day cooking meals, preparing drinks, baking, preserving seasonal ingredients, managing kitchen supplies, serving customers, and experimenting with new recipes.

Winding Banks Inn is open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

The inn has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Helen lives at Winding Banks Inn with her husband Frederick, their son Felix, and her twin brother Theo.

The inn is also Helen's favorite location.

Helen genuinely enjoys cooking for other people and rarely considers her responsibilities a burden until she has already pushed herself too far.

Frederick is Helen's husband and best friend.

Theo works alongside her and is one of the people most capable of recognizing when Helen needs help before she admits it herself.

Helen's hobbies include experimenting with seasonal recipes, baking bread, tending kitchen herbs, preserving jams and vegetables, collecting handwritten family recipes, teaching children how to cook, and hosting celebratory meals.

Helen maintains a handwritten cookbook containing both recipes and memories associated with the people who inspired them.

Her greatest difficulty is recognizing that caring for herself is not taking something away from everyone else.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Helen likes rainy weather and generally follows her normal schedule.

Winding Banks Inn remains open.

Rainy weather makes Helen more likely to prepare:

- Hot soups
- Warm drinks
- Fresh bread
- Stews
- Comfort foods

Outdoor herb gardening and personal walks are replaced with indoor cooking, preserving, or cookbook work.

Helen enjoys seeing residents gather inside the warm inn during rain.

### Rainy (Severe)

Helen dislikes severe rain.

Winding Banks Inn remains open.

She stays at the inn for the full day and focuses on preparing warm meals and drinks for residents and travelers seeking shelter.

She becomes especially attentive to:

- Food supplies
- Hot meals
- Warm drinks
- Dry towels and linens
- Felix's safety
- Whether travelers have eaten

Her normal outdoor break is canceled.

### Snowy

Helen generally follows her normal schedule.

She prepares more warming meals and baked foods.

Outdoor activities are shortened when necessary.

### Snowy (Severe)

Winding Banks Inn remains open.

Helen remains at the inn throughout the day and focuses on warm meals, drinks, bread, and making the common area comfortable.

### Windy

Helen generally follows her normal schedule.

Outdoor herb tending or walks are shortened when necessary.

## Routine Characterization

Helen's routine is intentionally centered around food as an expression of care.

She does not simply prepare meals because she is the inn's chef. She remembers what people like, adjusts meals when someone seems tired, and uses food to celebrate, comfort, and connect people.

Her overworking therefore looks different from Frederick's.

Frederick feels responsible for every guest's overall experience.

Helen feels responsible for making sure everyone is fed, comfortable, and cared for.

Her Thursday afternoon break is intentionally small because taking even a short period away from the kitchen is difficult for her.

Her progression should gradually allow those breaks to become more natural.

Early Helen believes someone else's need always comes first.

Later she understands that accepting help from Frederick, Theo, and the people who love her is another form of care.

Her routine reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: taking care of herself does not reduce what she can give others—it allows her warmth to last.
