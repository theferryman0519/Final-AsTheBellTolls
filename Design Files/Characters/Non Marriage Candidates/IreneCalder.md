# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Honoring the Past Doesn't Mean Living in It

Character Arc Start State: After losing James, Irene devoted herself to preserving the routines and traditions they once shared. She believes remaining steadfast is the best way to honor his memory, even if it means never truly moving forward herself.

Character Arc End State: Irene realizes that James's memory doesn't live within the bell tower or its traditions—it lives in the life she continues to build. By embracing change, she discovers she isn't leaving him behind but carrying him with her.

Core Belief: Traditions endure because someone chooses to preserve them.

Core Misbelief: If I let my life change, I'll lose the last part of James I have left.

Core Traits: Disciplined, Private, Solemn

Defining Sentence: ”Every moment matters because none of them return.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To understand that healing doesn't require forgetting, and that embracing tomorrow doesn't diminish yesterday.

Favorite Location: In Town Square around the base of the Bell Tower

Fears: Irene fears becoming the last person who remembers the traditions that shaped Blackmere. More personally, she fears that as the years pass, memories of James will slowly fade until all that remains is silence.

Goals: To ensure Blackmere's bell continues to ring for generations to come, preserving the traditions and sense of continuity that unite the town.

Habit: Winds a pocket watch with the initials JC whenever she’s deep in thought.

History: After her husband, James, unexpectedly passed away in his thirties, Irene inherited his role as Blackmere's Bellwarden. What began as a way to fulfill his final responsibility gradually became her life's calling. For decades she has faithfully maintained the bell tower, marking each day with unwavering precision and quietly preserving one of the town's oldest traditions. Though many see her as reserved and unchanging, every bell she rings is a tribute to the life she and James once shared.

Hobbies: Maintaining antique clocks and bells; polishing brass fixtures; reading local history; tending white flowers around the bell tower; birdwatching from the tower balcony; writing observations in a daily journal.

Motivation: Irene believes traditions provide stability during life's greatest hardships. By preserving Blackmere's oldest customs, she hopes future generations will always feel connected to those who came before them.

Personality Colors: Bronze, Slate Blue, Ivory

Personality Summary: Irene is disciplined, private, and deeply devoted to preserving Blackmere's traditions. She speaks only when she has something meaningful to say, preferring quiet observation over unnecessary conversation. Though her solemn demeanor can make her seem distant, she carries a profound sense of responsibility for the town's history and the people who shaped it. Every bell she rings is both a duty and a remembrance, reflecting her belief that honoring the past gives meaning to the present. Her journey is learning that the future deserves the same care she has always given the past.

Public Persona: Most residents know Irene as the steadfast Bellwarden who has faithfully rung Blackmere's bell for decades. Quiet, punctual, and unwavering in her routines, she has become as much a part of the town's daily rhythm as the bell itself. Though she rarely seeks conversation, her reliability has earned the respect of nearly everyone in Blackmere.

Secret: Every evening after the final bell, Irene quietly climbs to the top of the tower and spends a few moments speaking to James as though he were still beside her. She knows no one can hear her above the wind, and she's perfectly content to keep it that way.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Traditional

Weakness: Reluctant
