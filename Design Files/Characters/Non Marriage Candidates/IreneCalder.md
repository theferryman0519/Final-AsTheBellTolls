# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Honoring the Past Doesn't Mean Living in It

Character Arc Start State: After losing James, Irene devoted herself to preserving the routines and traditions they once shared. She believes remaining steadfast is the best way to honor his memory, even if it means never truly moving forward herself.

Character Arc End State: Irene realizes that James's memory doesn't live within the bell tower or its traditions—it lives in the life she continues to build. By embracing change, she discovers she isn't leaving him behind but carrying him with her.

Core Belief: Traditions endure because someone chooses to preserve them.

Core Misbelief: If I let my life change, I'll lose the last part of James I have left.

Core Traits: Disciplined, Private, Solemn

Defining Sentence: ”Every moment matters because none of them return.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To understand that healing doesn't require forgetting, and that embracing tomorrow doesn't diminish yesterday.

Favorite Location: In Town Square around the base of the Bell Tower

Fears: Irene fears becoming the last person who remembers the traditions that shaped Blackmere. More personally, she fears that as the years pass, memories of James will slowly fade until all that remains is silence.

Goals: To ensure Blackmere's bell continues to ring for generations to come, preserving the traditions and sense of continuity that unite the town.

Habit: Winds a pocket watch with the initials JC whenever she’s deep in thought.

History: After her husband, James, unexpectedly passed away in his thirties, Irene inherited his role as Blackmere's Bellwarden. What began as a way to fulfill his final responsibility gradually became her life's calling. For decades she has faithfully maintained the bell tower, marking each day with unwavering precision and quietly preserving one of the town's oldest traditions. Though many see her as reserved and unchanging, every bell she rings is a tribute to the life she and James once shared.

Hobbies: Maintaining antique clocks and bells; polishing brass fixtures; reading local history; tending white flowers around the bell tower; birdwatching from the tower balcony; writing observations in a daily journal.

Motivation: Irene believes traditions provide stability during life's greatest hardships. By preserving Blackmere's oldest customs, she hopes future generations will always feel connected to those who came before them.

Personality Colors: Bronze, Slate Blue, Ivory

Personality Summary: Irene is disciplined, private, and deeply devoted to preserving Blackmere's traditions. She speaks only when she has something meaningful to say, preferring quiet observation over unnecessary conversation. Though her solemn demeanor can make her seem distant, she carries a profound sense of responsibility for the town's history and the people who shaped it. Every bell she rings is both a duty and a remembrance, reflecting her belief that honoring the past gives meaning to the present. Her journey is learning that the future deserves the same care she has always given the past.

Public Persona: Most residents know Irene as the steadfast Bellwarden who has faithfully rung Blackmere's bell for decades. Quiet, punctual, and unwavering in her routines, she has become as much a part of the town's daily rhythm as the bell itself. Though she rarely seeks conversation, her reliability has earned the respect of nearly everyone in Blackmere.

Secret: Every evening after the final bell, Irene quietly climbs to the top of the tower and spends a few moments speaking to James as though he were still beside her. She knows no one can hear her above the wind, and she's perfectly content to keep it that way.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Traditional

Weakness: Reluctant

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Irene follows one of the most precise and repetitive routines in Blackmere.

As Blackmere's Bellwarden, she spends nearly every day maintaining the Blackmere Bell Tower, inspecting its mechanisms, polishing its brass fixtures, tending the flowers around its base, documenting its condition, and ensuring the town bell continues to mark the passage of time.

Blackmere Bell Tower is open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

The Bell Tower has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Irene lives alone at 4 Resident Lane.

Her home is available from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM every day.

Irene generally arrives at the Bell Tower shortly before or at its opening and remains there until after the final evening bell.

She rarely leaves Town Square during the day without a specific reason.

Town Square around the base of the Bell Tower is Irene's favorite location.

Her hobbies include maintaining antique clocks and bells, polishing brass fixtures, reading local history, tending white flowers around the Bell Tower, birdwatching from the tower balcony, and recording observations in a daily journal.

Irene is extremely private and does not maintain a regular social outing schedule.

Her strongest relationships tend to appear through occasional deliberate visits rather than frequent casual gatherings.

Agatha is Irene's best friend and mentor.

Edward, Lena, Lucian/Vivian, Roland, and Walter are also among the relatively small group of people Irene considers genuine friends.

Irene's schedule is intentionally repetitive.

For Irene, routine is not merely organization.

It is how she has preserved the life she once shared with James.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Irene generally follows her normal schedule.

Blackmere Bell Tower remains open during ordinary rain.

Flower tending and extended time around the base of the tower are replaced with:

- Bell maintenance
- Brass polishing
- Reading local history
- Reviewing maintenance records
- Journal writing
- Antique clock maintenance

Irene does not consider ordinary rain sufficient reason to alter the Bell Tower's routine.

### Rainy (Severe)

Irene dislikes severe rain.

Blackmere Bell Tower remains open.

Irene follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

She remains inside the Bell Tower and repeatedly checks:

- Windows
- Stonework
- Bell mechanisms
- Water intrusion
- Structural movement
- Exposed fixtures

Severe rain makes Irene particularly attentive to the Bell Tower's condition because of the damage Blackmere suffered during the Great Flood.

### Snowy

Irene generally follows her normal schedule.

Outdoor flower tending and balcony birdwatching may be shortened.

### Snowy (Severe)

Blackmere Bell Tower remains open.

Irene follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

She avoids unnecessary outdoor activity but continues maintaining the bell and ensuring its normal tolling schedule.

### Windy

Irene generally follows her normal schedule.

She spends less time on the tower balcony and performs additional inspections of exposed fixtures.

## Bell Toll Routine

The Blackmere Bell Tower marks each hour while it is operating.

Irene's primary responsibility is ensuring the bell remains capable of maintaining this schedule.

The hourly toll does not require Irene to stop whatever activity she is performing for a significant period.

When an hour approaches, she temporarily returns to the appropriate mechanism or position, ensures the toll occurs correctly, and then resumes her current task.

The bell tolls throughout Irene's normal workday from the tower's opening until its final evening toll.

The 10:00 PM toll is particularly important to Irene.

She considers it the formal conclusion of both the Bell Tower's day and her own responsibilities as Bellwarden.

---

## Evening James Routine

After the final bell at 10:00 PM, Irene follows a private ritual.

She climbs to the upper portion of the Bell Tower after it has closed to the public.

She spends several minutes alone overlooking Blackmere.

During this time, Irene speaks quietly to James as though he were standing beside her.

She may tell him:

- What happened in Blackmere that day
- Something unusual she observed
- Whether the bell required maintenance
- Something another resident said
- How the town's restoration is progressing
- A memory she thought about that day

Before leaving, Irene winds the pocket watch bearing James's initials.

She then returns to 4 Resident Lane.

Irene does not intentionally allow other residents to witness this routine.

Friendship or story events may eventually allow the player to discover it.

## Bell Tower Restoration Override

Irene's routine changes subtly as Blackmere Bell Tower is restored.

### Weathered

The Bell Tower is cracked, weathered, and partially silent.

Irene spends significantly more time:

- Inspecting damage
- Protecting surviving mechanisms
- Cleaning debris
- Recording structural concerns
- Attempting minor repairs
- Preserving components that cannot yet be repaired

If portions of the bell mechanism cannot function, Irene still arrives and leaves according to her normal schedule.

The silence frustrates her deeply.

### Rebuilding

Active repairs become part of Irene's routine.

She spends additional time inspecting repaired components and coordinating around restoration work.

Irene is reluctant to accept changes that deviate from the tower's historical construction.

### Recovering

The Bell Tower is stabilized and the bell rings once again.

The restoration of the bell's normal tolling is one of Irene's most emotionally significant restoration milestones.

Her routine begins shifting away from emergency preservation and back toward traditional maintenance.

### Renewed

The tower is fully restored.

Irene resumes a more traditional Bellwarden routine centered around maintenance, historical records, flowers, and observation.

### Growing

Decorative banners and landscaping expand Irene's exterior maintenance responsibilities.

She is initially uncertain about decorative changes but gradually accepts them.

### Prospering

Improved stonework and lighting require additional inspections but reduce the amount of emergency maintenance Irene must perform.

### Flourishing

The Bell Tower becomes one of Blackmere's defining landmarks.

Irene spends less time preserving a damaged structure and more time teaching, documenting, and ensuring future generations understand how to care for it.

This stage should support her character progression by showing that preservation can include allowing something to become new again.

The changes should remain subtle.

Irene does not suddenly abandon the Bell Tower or stop caring about tradition.

Instead, occasional schedule blocks begin changing.

Possible progression changes include:

- Leaving the Bell Tower slightly earlier when someone else can responsibly watch it
- Accepting an invitation to tea

## Routine Characterization

Irene's routine is intentionally more repetitive than most NPC schedules.

That repetition is part of her story.

The Bell Tower gives Irene a world in which every hour arrives when expected, every mechanism has a purpose, and every tradition can be repeated exactly as it was before.

Her private conversation with James after the final bell reveals why that consistency matters so much to her.

Her progression should therefore not involve abandoning her routines.

Instead, the player gradually sees small variations appear inside them.

Early Irene preserves each day because it resembles the life she remembers.

Later Irene begins allowing new experiences to become traditions of their own.

Her routine reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: honoring what came before does not require making tomorrow identical to yesterday.
