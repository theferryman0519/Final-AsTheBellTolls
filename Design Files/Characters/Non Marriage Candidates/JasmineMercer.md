# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That You Can't Carry Everyone's Happiness

Character Arc Start State: Jasmine believes it's her responsibility to brighten everyone's day. If someone is hurting, lonely, or upset, she immediately throws herself into cheering them up, often ignoring her own emotions in the process.

Character Arc End State: Jasmine learns that simply being present is sometimes more valuable than trying to fix everything. She discovers that love doesn't always require solutions.

Core Belief: Life is better when it's shared with others.

Core Misbelief: If the people around me aren't smiling, I'm not doing enough.

Core Traits: Compassionate, Empathetic, Outgoing

Defining Sentence: ”Curiosity is the beginning of every great discovery.”

Dialogue Length: Excruciating

Emotional Need: To realize she doesn't have to carry everyone else's emotions and that allowing herself to be vulnerable brings people even closer.

Favorite Location: At Mercer Farms amongst the animals and her niece and nephew

Fears: Jasmine fears watching the people she loves slowly drift apart. She treasures family and friendship so deeply that the thought of someone feeling alone breaks her heart.

Goals: To make Mercer Farms a place where neighbors, friends, and family always feel welcome, believing every community needs somewhere people can gather without feeling like strangers.

Habit: Grabs a chicken to hug it and twirl with it when she’s excited.

History: Jasmine has always been the loudest laugh in any room and the first to welcome newcomers with open arms. After joining the Mercer family through her brother Jonas's marriage to Claudia, she embraced life on the farm and devoted herself to caring for its animals. Over the years, she became the unofficial social glue of Blackmere, always organizing gatherings, checking on neighbors, and making sure no one felt forgotten. Though she appears endlessly cheerful, she rarely allows herself to admit when she's the one in need of support.

Hobbies: Caring for farm animals; organizing community picnics; baking treats to share with neighbors; decorating for seasonal festivals; birdwatching with Kira and Milo; knitting blankets for newborn animals; collecting funny stories from around town.

Motivation: Jasmine wants every person who visits Mercer Farms to leave feeling happier than when they arrived, believing kindness has a way of spreading from one person to the next.

Personality Colors: Sunflower Yellow, Terracotta, Meadow Green

Personality Summary: Jasmine is vibrant, compassionate, and impossible to ignore. She has an infectious enthusiasm for life and a remarkable ability to make complete strangers feel like lifelong friends within minutes. Whether she's chatting over a fence, introducing neighbors to one another, or doting on the farm's animals, Jasmine pours her whole heart into every interaction. Beneath her boundless optimism, however, is someone who quietly believes she must always be the cheerful one. Her greatest lesson is discovering that the people she cares for will gladly support her, just as she has always supported them.

Public Persona: Everyone in Blackmere knows Jasmine as the friendliest face at Mercer Farms. She greets nearly everyone by name, somehow remembers every birthday, and has an endless supply of stories, hugs, and homemade treats. For many residents, a conversation with Jasmine is enough to turn a difficult day into a better one.

Secret: Jasmine keeps a small journal filled with birthdays, anniversaries, favorite foods, and little milestones for nearly every resident in Blackmere. She insists she simply has a "good memory," but the journal ensures no one she cares about ever feels forgotten. A secondary secret she carries is her affection for Theo, which (turns out) is not very secretive as most of the town knows...except for Theo.

Speaking Tone: Compassionate

Strength: Strong-willed

Weakness: Over-the-top

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Jasmine follows an energetic and highly social routine centered around Mercer Farms, its animals, her family, and the people of Blackmere.

As the rancher of Mercer Farms, Jasmine spends most days feeding animals, checking their health, cleaning their living areas, maintaining ranch supplies, helping customers purchase animals, and making sure every animal receives individual attention.

Mercer Farms is open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM every day.

The farm has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Jasmine lives at Mercer Farms with her brother Jonas, her sister-in-law Claudia, her niece Kira, and her nephew Milo.

Mercer Farms is also Jasmine's favorite location.

She particularly enjoys being among the animals while Kira and Milo are nearby.

Jasmine manages animal sales at Mercer Farms and therefore remains available at the farm for most of its operating hours.

She does not remain at a sales counter continuously.

Throughout the day, she moves between the animal areas, farmyard, residence, and customer area.

Jasmine's hobbies include caring for animals, organizing community picnics, baking treats for neighbors, decorating for seasonal festivals, birdwatching with Kira and Milo, knitting blankets for newborn animals, and collecting funny stories from around Blackmere.

She is one of Blackmere's most socially connected residents and frequently talks with visitors while completing ranch work.

Her greatest difficulty is believing she must improve everyone else's mood.

Even when Jasmine herself is tired, worried, or unhappy, she instinctively becomes more cheerful when someone else approaches.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Jasmine likes ordinary rain and generally follows her normal schedule.

Mercer Farms remains open.

Jasmine still performs all necessary animal care.

Outdoor social activities and picnics are replaced with:

- Spending additional time with the animals
- Knitting
- Baking treats
- Journal writing
- Preparing seasonal decorations
- Talking with family
- Welcoming visitors indoors when appropriate

Jasmine particularly enjoys listening to rain while working around the animals.

### Rainy (Severe)

Jasmine dislikes severe rain.

Mercer Farms remains open.

Jasmine follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Her primary concern is ensuring every animal is:

- Sheltered
- Dry
- Fed
- Calm
- Accounted for

She repeatedly checks Kira, Milo, and the rest of her family as well.

All optional travel is canceled.

### Snowy

Jasmine generally follows her normal schedule.

Outdoor ranch activities may take longer because she checks animal shelter and bedding more carefully.

### Snowy (Severe)

Jasmine dislikes severe snow.

Mercer Farms remains open.

She follows the Severe Weather Schedule and focuses on shelter, warmth, feed, water, and keeping the animals comfortable.

### Windy

Jasmine generally follows her normal schedule.

Outdoor picnics and extended birdwatching are canceled when necessary.

- Feeding animals
- Cleaning animal areas

- Knitting nearby
- Eating with her family
- Baking
- Working elsewhere on Mercer Farms

She can temporarily return to the customer area when the player initiates an animal purchase.

## Animal Care Override

Animal care always takes priority over Jasmine's optional activities.

If an animal is:

- Sick
- Injured
- Giving birth
- Newly purchased
- Distressed
- Missing
- Otherwise requiring special attention

Jasmine temporarily abandons her normal schedule.

She remains with the animal until the immediate situation is resolved.

Optional social visits, picnics, baking, birdwatching, and other hobbies are canceled when necessary.

Jasmine is especially attentive to newborn animals.

During periods with newborn livestock, knitting and additional barn visits become more common.

## Emotional Override

Jasmine instinctively notices when another person appears unhappy.

Early in her character progression, this may cause her to interrupt a low-priority activity in an attempt to cheer them up.

Possible behaviors include:

- Offering food
- Telling jokes
- Inviting someone to Mercer Farms
- Giving them a hug
- Introducing them to an animal
- Suggesting a gathering
- Telling an elaborate story

These overrides should not occur constantly.

They are most appropriate during scripted events involving residents Jasmine knows well.

As Jasmine progresses through her character arc, these interactions change.

Instead of immediately attempting to improve the person's mood, she becomes more capable of simply sitting with them, listening, or allowing them to feel unhappy without treating that emotion as something she must fix.

## Routine Characterization

Jasmine's routine is intentionally much more social than Claudia's or Jonas's despite all three working at Mercer Farms.

The animals provide the structure of her day, but people constantly fill the spaces between those responsibilities.

She talks to customers while feeding livestock, includes Kira and Milo in her hobbies, invites people to the farm, delivers treats, and looks for reasons to make ordinary days feel communal.

Her schedule should also reveal the flaw beneath that warmth.

Jasmine rarely asks whether she has enough energy for another person.

If someone needs cheering up, she simply finds more energy.

Her progression should therefore involve small moments where she stops performing happiness.

Early Jasmine sees someone hurting and immediately tries to make them smile.

Later she can sit beside them without saying much.

Early Jasmine hides her own difficult days behind jokes, food, and enthusiasm.

Later she occasionally admits that she is tired, worried, sad, or simply not feeling cheerful.

Her routine reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: being there for someone does not always mean making their sadness disappear, and the people Jasmine loves do not need her to smile every moment in order to love having her around.
