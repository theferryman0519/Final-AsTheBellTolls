# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Peace Sometimes Requires Difficult Conversations

Character Arc Start State: Jonas learns that honesty isn't the enemy of peace. By speaking openly about difficult feelings, he strengthens the trust his family already shares instead of protecting them through silence.

Character Arc End State: Jonas believes keeping the peace is the best way to protect the people he loves. Rather than confronting conflict directly, he quietly absorbs tension himself, hoping time and kindness will smooth things over.

Core Belief: The strongest harvest comes from people working together.

Core Misbelief: If I keep everyone happy, everything will work itself out.

Core Traits: Gentle, Optimistic, Patient

Defining Sentence: ”The land gives back what you give in.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To understand that avoiding conflict doesn't protect relationships—honest conversations do.

Favorite Location: At Mercer Farms with the entire family home

Fears: Jonas fears disappointing the people who depend on him. More than failure itself, he worries about letting his family carry burdens they shouldn't have to because of his own mistakes.

Goals: To leave Mercer Farms healthier than he found it and create a home where Kira and Milo always know they're loved, supported, and free to become whoever they choose to be.

Habit: Tips his farmer’s hat whenever greeting someone.

History: Jonas grew up on Mercer Farms, learning from an early age that good things take patience, consistency, and hard work. After marrying Claudia, they built a life rooted in mutual respect, raising Kira and Milo while working side by side to care for both their family and the farm. Jonas is known throughout Blackmere for his gentle nature and unwavering dependability, always willing to lend a hand without expecting anything in return. Though he prefers harmony over confrontation, his greatest lesson is discovering that some conversations are worth having because they're rooted in love.

Hobbies: Walking the fields at sunrise; repairing fences by hand; fishing with Milo; teaching Kira about gardening; carving small wooden toys; tending the orchard; sharing quiet evenings on the farmhouse porch with Claudia.

Motivation: Jonas wants Mercer Farms to remain a place where hard work, kindness, and family are passed from one generation to the next.

Personality Colors: Forest Green, Wheat, Sky Blue

Personality Summary: Jonas is patient, dependable, and quietly optimistic. He rarely seeks the spotlight, preferring to let his actions speak louder than his words. Friends know they can always rely on him, whether they need help repairing a fence or simply someone willing to listen without judgment. He approaches every challenge with calm determination and believes that most problems can be solved through cooperation and compassion. His greatest weakness is his reluctance to address conflict directly, often carrying worries alone in hopes of protecting the people he loves.

Public Persona: The people of Blackmere know Jonas as the steady heart of Mercer Farms. He's the kind of neighbor who notices when someone's fence needs fixing before they're able to ask for help, and the kind of father whose children never doubt they're loved. Quietly dependable, Jonas has earned the town's trust through years of consistent kindness and honest work.

Secret: Jonas keeps a wooden growth chart hidden inside the old barn where he marks Kira's and Milo's heights every year. He never tells them about it, hoping one day they'll discover decades of memories preserved in the wood and realize just how closely he treasured watching them grow.

Speaking Tone: Gentle

Strength: Steady

Weakness: Avoiding

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Jonas follows a steady and practical routine centered almost entirely around Mercer Farms, its fields, orchard, and his family.

As the farmer of Mercer Farms, Jonas spends most days tending crops, inspecting soil, maintaining irrigation, repairing fences, caring for the orchard, preparing fields, managing seed stock, and handling crop-seed sales.

Mercer Farms is open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM every day.

The farm has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Jonas lives at Mercer Farms with his wife Claudia, his sister Jasmine, his daughter Kira, and his son Milo.

Mercer Farms is also Jonas's favorite location.

He rarely feels a need to leave when the people he loves and the work he values are already around him.

Jonas manages crop-seed sales at Mercer Farms and therefore remains available somewhere on the farm for most operating hours.

He does not remain at a sales counter continuously.

Throughout the day, he moves between the Fields, orchard, residence, storage areas, fences, and customer area.

Jonas's hobbies include walking the fields at sunrise, repairing fences, fishing with Milo, teaching Kira about gardening, carving wooden toys, tending the orchard, and spending quiet evenings with Claudia.

His greatest difficulty is confronting problems directly.

Jonas often notices tension before anyone else but prefers to give people time, space, and kindness rather than initiating an uncomfortable conversation.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Jonas likes ordinary rain and generally follows his normal schedule.

Mercer Farms remains open.

Rain reduces the amount of watering and irrigation work required.

Jonas instead spends additional time:

- Checking drainage
- Inspecting soil
- Repairing fences
- Maintaining tools
- Working in the orchard
- Organizing seed stock
- Completing indoor woodworking projects

He enjoys seeing the fields receive a steady natural rainfall.

### Rainy (Severe)

Jonas dislikes severe rain.

Mercer Farms remains open.

Jonas follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Because protecting the farm and his family takes priority, he spends much of the day checking:

- Drainage
- Flood-prone areas
- Fences
- Fields
- Orchard trees
- Farm structures
- Irrigation channels

He avoids unnecessary travel away from Mercer Farms.

### Snowy

Jonas dislikes snowy weather.

Mercer Farms remains open.

Fieldwork is reduced and replaced with:

- Fence repair
- Tool maintenance
- Seed organization
- Woodworking
- Orchard inspections
- Family time

### Snowy (Severe)

Jonas dislikes severe snow.

Mercer Farms remains open.

He follows the Severe Weather Schedule and focuses on protecting the farm rather than ordinary crop work.

### Windy

Jonas generally follows his normal schedule.

He pays additional attention to:

- Orchard trees
- Fences
- Gates
- Loose farm equipment
- Damaged crops

## Routine Characterization

Jonas's routine is intentionally quiet and consistent.

Where Claudia balances the farm against mining and Jasmine fills the farm with social energy, Jonas is the person who keeps the land itself functioning.

His schedule revolves around crops, soil, irrigation, fences, trees, and the small repairs that keep Mercer Farms stable year after year.

His family is equally important.

Jonas does not work hard because he wants recognition or expansion. He wants Mercer Farms to remain a secure home for Claudia, Jasmine, Kira, and Milo.

His character growth should appear through conversations more than dramatic schedule changes.

Early Jonas notices conflict and finds another task to do.

Later he becomes more willing to stop working, sit down with someone, and say what actually needs to be said.

His routine reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: patience can help relationships grow, but sometimes caring for them also means having the difficult conversation.
