# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Wisdom Is Meant to Be Passed On

Character Arc Start State: Julian believes his greatest contribution to Blackmere is preserving knowledge. He devotes himself to his research, convinced that documenting discoveries is more important than teaching others how to make them.

Character Arc End State: Julian realizes that books alone cannot preserve knowledge. True wisdom survives only when it's entrusted to the next generation, inspiring him to become a genuine mentor rather than simply a scholar.

Core Belief: Understanding the world begins with careful observation.

Core Misbelief: Knowledge is safest in experienced hands.

Core Traits: Analytical, Meticulous, Patient

Defining Sentence: ”Understanding begins the moment we stop assuming.”

Dialogue Length: Excruciating

Emotional Need: To accept that teaching others is just as important as discovering something new.

Favorite Location: In Gloamwood Forest surrounding Rain & Hale

Fears: Julian fears that decades of accumulated knowledge will disappear after he's gone. More than death itself, he worries that valuable discoveries, remedies, and observations will simply be forgotten.

Goals: To document the medicinal properties of every plant, mineral, fungus, and natural phenomenon surrounding Blackmere while preparing someone capable of continuing his life's work.

Habit: Sketches designs in the air with his finger while walking through the forest.

History: Julian arrived in Blackmere during its earliest days alongside Agatha and Roland, helping establish one of the settlement's first apothecaries. Fascinated by medicine and natural science since childhood, he spent decades studying the forests, rivers, and mountains surrounding the town, carefully recording every discovery in an ever-growing collection of journals. Though respected as Blackmere's foremost expert on herbs and remedies, Julian has often become so absorbed in research that he forgets not everyone shares his endless curiosity. As the years pass, he begins to realize that preserving knowledge requires more than recording it—it requires someone willing to carry it forward.

Hobbies: Cataloging herbs; pressing flowers; sketching medicinal plants; maintaining decades of handwritten field journals; conducting harmless experiments; birdwatching; reading scientific texts; identifying fungi during long walks through Gloamwood Forest.

Motivation: Julian wants every generation of Blackmere to understand the natural world a little better than the one before it, believing that knowledge is humanity's greatest inheritance.

Personality Colors: Forest Green, Brass, Mahogany

Personality Summary: Julian is methodical, endlessly curious, and remarkably patient. Every question is an opportunity to teach, every conversation a chance to share another fascinating observation about the natural world. Though his formal demeanor and scientific precision can make him seem emotionally distant, his passion comes from a genuine desire to improve people's lives through knowledge. He believes nearly every mystery can be understood through careful study and evidence, yet his greatest lesson is discovering that wisdom grows strongest when shared rather than merely preserved.

Public Persona: The people of Blackmere know Julian as "Doc," the apothecary who always seems to know the answer to even the most obscure question. Whether someone needs a remedy, identifies an unusual plant, or simply asks an innocent question, they can expect an informative explanation that often lasts far longer than anticipated. Though his lectures are legendary, so is his expertise.

Secret: Julian has spent decades writing what he calls The Blackmere Compendium—an enormous collection of journals documenting every medicinal herb, seasonal change, local species, and scientific observation he's made throughout his life. He has quietly begun wondering who, if anyone, will inherit it when he's gone.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Calm

Weakness: Detached

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Julian follows a methodical and research-focused routine centered around Rain & Hale and the surrounding Gloamwood Forest.

As Blackmere's apothecary, Julian spends his days preparing remedies, cataloging herbs, assisting residents, maintaining botanical specimens, conducting harmless experiments, selling herbs, and recording his observations in an extensive collection of handwritten journals.

Rain & Hale is both Julian's residence and workplace.

Rain & Hale is available from 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM every day.

The apothecary has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Gloamwood Forest is also available from 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM every day and has no weather closures.

Because Rain & Hale is located within Gloamwood Forest, Julian regularly moves between the apothecary and nearby forest without treating those excursions as significant journeys.

Julian's favorite location is Gloamwood Forest surrounding Rain & Hale.

He regularly studies herbs, fungi, flowers, birds, seasonal changes, and other natural phenomena.

Julian manages herb sales at Rain & Hale.

Although he spends significant portions of the week conducting field research, he remains at Rain & Hale for predictable periods each day so residents and the player can reliably find him.

His hobbies include cataloging herbs, pressing flowers, sketching medicinal plants, maintaining field journals, conducting experiments, birdwatching, reading scientific texts, and identifying fungi.

Julian maintains The Blackmere Compendium, an enormous collection of scientific journals containing decades of observations about the natural world surrounding Blackmere.

His greatest difficulty is recognizing that recording knowledge is not the same as ensuring someone else will understand how to use it.

## Weather Overrides

### Rainy

Julian likes ordinary rain and generally follows his normal schedule.

Rain & Hale and Gloamwood Forest remain available.

Rain changes Julian's research priorities rather than preventing fieldwork.

During rainfall, he becomes particularly interested in:

- Fungi
- Moisture-loving plants
- Soil conditions
- Changes in herb appearance
- Water accumulation
- Bird behavior
- Plants that become easier to identify when wet

He carries appropriate field equipment and may deliberately spend additional time in Gloamwood Forest.

### Rainy (Severe)

Rain & Hale and Gloamwood Forest remain available.

Julian is neutral toward severe rain but limits unnecessary field travel for safety.

He spends more time inside Rain & Hale conducting:

- Specimen analysis
- Experiments
- Remedy preparation
- Journal work
- Botanical preservation
- Scientific reading

Short observations immediately surrounding Rain & Hale may still occur when conditions permit.

### Snowy

Julian generally follows his normal schedule with modified fieldwork.

He studies:

- Winter plants
- Dormancy
- Snow accumulation
- Animal tracks
- Cold-weather fungi
- Seasonal changes in tree and plant structure

Extended field expeditions are shortened when necessary.

### Snowy (Severe)

Julian remains primarily at Rain & Hale.

He focuses on preserved specimens, experiments, journals, and scientific reading.

### Windy

Julian generally follows his normal schedule.

He pays additional attention to:

- Seed dispersal
- Branch movement
- Wind damage
- Changes in bird behavior
- Fallen plant material

## Routine Characterization

Julian's routine is intentionally divided between observation in the field and documentation at Rain & Hale.

Unlike a conventional shopkeeper, he cannot perform his profession entirely from behind a counter. The herbs and phenomena he studies exist outside his laboratory, so regularly entering Gloamwood Forest is fundamental to his work.

The Blackmere Compendium is equally important.

Julian has spent decades ensuring his observations are preserved, but early in his arc almost all of that knowledge remains dependent on Julian himself being present to interpret it.

His progression should gradually make his routine less solitary.

Early Julian studies while others watch.

Later he begins handing someone else the notebook.

His routine reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: knowledge survives not when it is merely written down, but when someone else is taught how to understand, question, and continue it.
