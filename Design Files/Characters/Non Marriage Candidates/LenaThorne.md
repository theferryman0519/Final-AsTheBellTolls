# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Living Fully Honors Those We've Lost

Character Arc Start State: Lena quietly carries the weight of surviving the Great Flood while Ian did not. She dedicates herself to caring for Memorial Hill, believing that preserving the memories of others is more important than creating new ones for herself.

Character Arc End State: Lena realizes that Ian would never have wanted her life to end alongside his. By embracing joy, friendship, and new memories, she discovers that moving forward is another way of honoring the people she loved.

Core Belief: Every life deserves to be remembered with dignity.

Core Misbelief: If I'm happy again, it means I've forgotten Ian.

Core Traits: Compassion, Perseverance, Resilience

Defining Sentence: ”Love doesn’t even when someone is no longer there.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To forgive herself for surviving and accept that healing is not the same as leaving the past behind.

Favorite Location: On the grounds of Memorial Hill amongst the grave markers

Fears: Lena fears forgetting the small details that made Ian who he was—the sound of his laugh, the way he smiled, the ordinary moments that fade before the extraordinary ones. She also fears that the people buried in Memorial Hill will someday have no one left to remember them.

Goals: To preserve Memorial Hill as a place of peace, remembrance, and comfort where families can celebrate the lives of those they've lost rather than simply mourn them.

Habit: Pauses and leans down to admire flowers when walking.

History: Lena and Ian built a quiet, happy life together until the Great Flood forever changed Blackmere. Ian, then the town's sheriff, gave his life helping others escape the rising waters, leaving Lena widowed with their young daughter, Anya. Rather than allowing grief to harden her heart, Lena accepted responsibility for caring for Memorial Hill, dedicating herself to honoring every resident laid to rest there with the same love and respect she gives Ian. Though she still carries guilt for believing she could have done more that day, her compassion has quietly become a source of comfort for countless families throughout Blackmere.

Hobbies: Arranging fresh flowers on graves; pressing seasonal wildflowers; writing letters to Ian she never intends to send; tending quiet gardens; sketching birds that visit Memorial Hill; reading poetry beneath the oldest tree in the cemetery.

Motivation: Lena wants everyone who visits Memorial Hill to leave remembering the love they shared rather than only the loss they endured.

Personality Colors: Lavender, Evergreen, Stone Gray

Personality Summary: Lena is gentle, resilient, and deeply compassionate. She approaches every conversation with quiet empathy, offering comfort without ever forcing it upon others. Years spent tending Memorial Hill have taught her that grief looks different for everyone, making her an exceptional listener who rarely judges and never rushes another person's healing. Though she carries lingering guilt over Ian's death during the Great Flood, Lena chooses each day to transform that sorrow into kindness, believing the greatest tribute to those we've lost is caring well for those who remain.

Public Persona: Most residents know Lena as the kind-hearted gravekeeper who maintains Memorial Hill with remarkable care and dignity. Whether she's placing fresh flowers on an old headstone or quietly comforting a grieving family, she has become a reassuring presence whose compassion makes one of Blackmere's saddest places feel unexpectedly peaceful.

Secret: Every year on the anniversary of the Great Flood, Lena visits Ian's grave before sunrise and leaves a fresh bouquet of forget-me-nots. She sits with him until the first bell rings across Blackmere, sharing everything that has happened over the past year as though he were still there listening.

Speaking Tone: Compassionate

Strength: Empathy

Weakness: Guilt

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Lena follows a gentle and deliberate routine centered around Memorial Hill, her daughter Anya, and the residents whose memories she has chosen to care for.

As Blackmere's gravekeeper, Lena spends most days maintaining grave markers, tending flowers and gardens, cleaning pathways, assisting visitors, preserving memorials, and ensuring Memorial Hill remains peaceful and welcoming.

Memorial Hill is both Lena's residence and workplace.

Memorial Hill is available from 7:00 AM to 12:00 AM every day.

The location has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Lena lives at Memorial Hill with her daughter Anya.

Memorial Hill is also Lena's favorite location.

She feels most at peace among the grave markers, gardens, old trees, and flowers she tends.

Lena does not treat Memorial Hill as a place defined only by death.

She deliberately maintains it as somewhere residents can remember the people they loved without feeling that grief is the only emotion they are allowed to experience there.

Lena's hobbies include arranging fresh flowers on graves, pressing seasonal wildflowers, writing letters to Ian, tending quiet gardens, sketching birds, and reading poetry beneath the oldest tree at Memorial Hill.

She regularly pauses during her routine to admire flowers.

Lena is approachable while working and will stop what she is doing when a grieving visitor needs someone to listen.

Her greatest difficulty is allowing herself to create new memories without feeling that doing so diminishes the life she shared with Ian.

## Weather Overrides

### Clear

Lena likes clear weather and generally follows her normal schedule.

She spends additional time:

- Gardening
- Arranging flowers
- Reading outdoors
- Sketching birds
- Walking the grounds with Anya

Clear days make outdoor portions of her routine slightly longer when no other responsibilities require her attention.

### Cloudy

Lena follows her normal schedule.

She particularly enjoys the subdued atmosphere of Memorial Hill during cloudy weather.

### Rainy

Lena is neutral toward ordinary rain.

Memorial Hill remains available.

She continues essential gravekeeping work but shortens extended outdoor activities.

Reading, flower pressing, arranging indoor flowers, and writing letters may replace:

- Bird sketching
- Poetry beneath the oldest tree
- Long evening walks

### Rainy (Severe)

Memorial Hill remains available.

Lena focuses on protecting the grounds and checking for:

- Damaged flowers
- Soil erosion
- Fallen branches
- Flooded pathways
- Damaged memorial arrangements

She remains close to Anya and their residence when conditions become dangerous.

### Snowy

Lena likes snowy weather.

Memorial Hill remains available.

She performs essential maintenance but often takes additional time to appreciate the quiet appearance of the grounds.

She may clear snow from important grave markers and pathways.

### Snowy (Severe)

Lena dislikes severe snow.

She follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Optional outdoor activities are canceled.

### Windy

Lena generally follows her normal schedule.

She checks memorial arrangements and removes anything that could blow away or become damaged.

## Gravekeeping Override

Gravekeeping responsibilities always take priority over Lena's optional activities.

Possible responsibilities include:

- Cleaning grave markers
- Removing debris
- Replacing damaged flowers
- Maintaining pathways
- Tending memorial gardens
- Repairing simple memorial arrangements
- Preparing a grave for a memorial service
- Assisting visiting families

Lena treats every grave with equal dignity regardless of how frequently someone visits it.

Graves without living visitors receive particular attention.

Lena does not want any resident buried at Memorial Hill to appear forgotten.

---

## Visitor Comfort Override

Lena regularly encounters residents visiting loved ones at Memorial Hill.

When a visitor appears distressed, Lena may temporarily stop a low-priority task.

She does not immediately approach everyone who is grieving.

Instead, she remains nearby and allows the visitor to decide whether they want company.

If approached, Lena may:

- Listen quietly
- Help arrange flowers
- Share a memory when appropriate
- Sit beside the visitor
- Offer practical assistance
- Give them privacy afterward

Lena rarely attempts to tell another person how they should grieve.

Her years at Memorial Hill have taught her that different people need different things.

## Anya Override

Anya is Lena's daughter and always takes priority over routine gravekeeping when Lena believes she needs her.

Lena tries to share breakfast and dinner with Anya every day.

Possible activities together include:

- Gardening
- Walking Memorial Hill
- Pressing flowers
- Birdwatching
- Reading
- Preparing meals
- Arranging flowers
- Sitting quietly together

Lena does not expect Anya to participate in gravekeeping.

When Anya chooses to help, Lena gives her simple and respectful tasks.

Lena is careful not to make Memorial Hill or Ian's memory the entirety of Anya's childhood.

As Lena's character arc progresses, she becomes increasingly willing to create experiences with Anya that have nothing to do with grief or remembrance.

## Ian's Grave Override

Lena regularly visits Ian's grave.

These visits are usually private and brief.

She may:

- Replace flowers
- Clean the grave marker
- Sit beside him
- Tell him something Anya did
- Share something that happened in Blackmere
- Read part of a letter

Wednesday and Sunday evenings are her most consistent ordinary visits.

Lena does not treat Ian's grave differently when performing general gravekeeping duties.

Her personal visits occur after the work itself is finished.

Before the event, Lena helps:

- Clean pathways
- Prepare memorial areas
- Arrange flowers
- Organize gathering spaces
- Ensure grave markers are presentable
- Assist families preparing personal memorials

During the festival, Lena spends much of her time:

- Welcoming residents
- Helping visitors find graves
- Providing flowers
- Offering quiet support
- Participating in remembrance activities
- Spending time with Anya

Lena does not act as though River Remembrance is solely a day of sadness.

She encourages residents to tell stories, remember laughter, and celebrate the lives of those they lost.

## Great Flood Anniversary Override

On the anniversary of the Great Flood, Lena follows a unique private routine.

She wakes before the normal 6:00 AM schedule.

This is an intentional exception to the standard daily schedule because it is a scripted character event rather than an ordinary NPC routine.

Before sunrise, Lena prepares a bouquet of fresh Forget-Me-Nots.

She visits Ian's grave alone.

She remains there until the first bell rings across Blackmere.

During this time, Lena tells Ian about everything that has happened during the previous year.

Possible subjects include:

- Anya
- Blackmere's restoration
- Their friends
- Changes at Memorial Hill
- Difficult moments
- Happy moments
- Things Lena wishes he could have seen

After the first bell, Lena returns to her ordinary responsibilities.

This routine is private.

The player should not normally witness it unless a story or friendship event specifically reveals it.

## Memorial Hill Restoration Override

Lena's routine changes as Memorial Hill is restored.

### Weathered

Lena spends additional time:

- Clearing damaged pathways
- Repairing flower beds
- Cleaning weathered grave markers
- Removing flood debris
- Protecting damaged memorials

The condition of Memorial Hill weighs heavily on her.

### Rebuilding

Restoration work becomes part of Lena's daily routine.

She works around construction and helps ensure graves remain respected during repairs.

### Recovering

Improved pathways and repaired memorial areas reduce emergency maintenance.

Lena begins spending more time gardening and assisting visitors.

### Renewed

Memorial Hill once again feels peaceful and carefully maintained.

Lena has more time for flowers, poetry, visitors, and Anya.

### Growing

Expanded gardens and landscaping give Lena additional planting responsibilities.

She welcomes these changes because they make Memorial Hill feel alive rather than merely preserved.

### Prospering

Improved memorial gardens and gathering spaces allow families to spend more time remembering loved ones comfortably.

Lena increasingly focuses on helping visitors rather than repairing damage.

### Flourishing

Memorial Hill becomes one of Blackmere's most peaceful and beautiful locations.

Lena's routine reflects her character progression.

She spends less time desperately preserving what remains and more time creating a place where remembrance and continued life comfortably exist together.

## Character Progression Override

Lena's routine should gradually expand beyond Memorial Hill as her character arc progresses.

The changes should remain gentle.

Early Lena spends nearly all of her free time:

- Maintaining Memorial Hill
- Caring for Anya
- Visiting Ian
- Writing to Ian
- Performing quiet solitary hobbies

Later schedule variants may occasionally replace these blocks with:

- Visiting Jasmine
- Spending time with Madison
- Taking Anya somewhere purely for fun
- Eating at Winding Banks Inn
- Attending a community gathering without helping organize it
- Visiting a restored area of Blackmere
- Sitting somewhere simply because she enjoys being there
- Accepting an invitation without first inventing a reason she should stay home

Lena never stops visiting Ian.

The change is that those visits no longer prevent her from living the rest of her life.

Lena's existing Memorial Hill restoration override remains the primary influence on her work. Broader Blackmere restoration gradually gives her more reasons to leave Memorial Hill without diminishing its importance.

### Weathered / Rebuilding

Lena remains strongly centered around Memorial Hill, its residence, and immediate maintenance needs.

Selected free-time blocks may become General Store errands, Town Square walks, Inn visits, or time with Jasmine and Anya in restored community spaces.

## Routine Characterization

Lena's routine is intentionally peaceful and grounded in Memorial Hill.

She is not simply maintaining a cemetery.

She is maintaining a place where Blackmere's residents are allowed to remember people they loved.

That makes her work different from Irene's relationship with remembrance.

Irene preserves tradition and continuity through the Bell Tower.

Lena works directly with grief, memory, families, flowers, and the physical places where individual lives are remembered.

Her schedule should also prevent grief from becoming her only characterization.

She gardens.

She watches birds.

She reads poetry.

She spends time with Anya.

She has tea with Jasmine.

She notices flowers simply because they are beautiful.

Those small pieces of ordinary life are important because Lena's arc is not about forgetting Ian.

It is about realizing that loving Ian and loving the life she still has are not opposing choices.

Early Lena believes happiness risks leaving Ian behind.

Later Lena understands that every new memory she makes with Anya, Jasmine, Madison, and the rest of Blackmere becomes another part of the life Ian sacrificed himself to protect.

Her routine reinforces the central lesson of her character arc: living fully does not diminish the people we've lost—it carries their love forward with us.
