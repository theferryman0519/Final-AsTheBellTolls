# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Some Burdens Are Meant to Be Shared

Character Arc Start State: Martin believes reliability means carrying every responsibility himself. He rarely asks for help, convinced that burdening others would only make their lives more difficult.

Character Arc End State: Martin learns that accepting support doesn't diminish his strength. Trusting others allows him to build deeper relationships instead of quietly carrying life's weight alone.

Core Belief: Every promise deserves to be kept.

Core Misbelief: If I remain dependable enough, no one will ever have to worry about me.

Core Traits: Dutiful, Loyal, Restrained

Defining Sentence: ”Even a few short words can mean a great deal to someone.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To realize that being cared for is just as important as caring for others.

Favorite Location: Amongst the various paths of the Resident Lanes district

Fears: Martin fears letting people down, especially in the small ways that others might overlook. After losing his mother at a young age, he developed a quiet belief that the people others rely on simply cannot afford to fail.

Goals: To ensure every letter, parcel, and message entrusted to him reaches its destination, preserving the connections that bind Blackmere together.

Habit: Sorts through a stack of envelopes when speaking to someone.

History: Martin was still a child when his mother, Erika, the town's beloved postmaster, passed away after a long illness. Watching how much her work meant to the people of Blackmere left a lasting impression on him. When he came of age, he chose to continue her legacy, dedicating himself to the same profession with quiet determination. Though naturally reserved, Martin has become one of the town's most dependable figures, believing that every letter delivered is another thread holding the community together.

Hobbies: Walking Blackmere's trails before sunrise; maintaining old fountain pens; organizing stamps and postmarks; woodworking; repairing worn mailbags; reading historical correspondence; enjoying quiet evenings with a book.

Motivation: Martin wants every resident of Blackmere to know that no matter how distant someone may feel, there will always be a way to stay connected.

Personality Colors: Navy Blue, Warm Brown, Cream

Personality Summary: Martin is disciplined, dependable, and quietly compassionate. He rarely seeks attention and prefers actions over words, believing consistency is one of the purest expressions of care. Though naturally reserved, he notices far more than people realize and quietly looks after those around him through thoughtful gestures rather than grand displays of emotion. His greatest challenge is accepting that strength isn't measured by how much he can carry alone, but by allowing others to stand beside him when he needs them.

Public Persona: Most residents know Martin as Blackmere's dependable postmaster. Rain or shine, his familiar route through town never changes, and every delivery arrives with quiet professionalism. Though he isn't one for conversation, his reliability has earned the trust of nearly everyone in Blackmere.

Secret: Martin has carefully preserved every letter his mother ever wrote to Edward during her illness. On difficult evenings, he quietly rereads them—not out of sadness, but to remember the kindness, strength, and hope she carried until the very end.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Endurance

Weakness: Quiet

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Martin follows a disciplined and highly consistent routine centered around Blackmere's mail, Blackmere Town Hall, and his daily delivery routes through town.

As Blackmere's postmaster, Martin begins each workday by sorting letters, parcels, notices, and outgoing correspondence at Blackmere Town Hall.

Blackmere Town Hall is open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Martin lives at 2 Resident Lane.

2 Resident Lane is available from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Martin's favorite location is the network of paths throughout the Resident Lanes district.

Unlike most residents, Martin does not spend the majority of his working day inside his workplace.

Once the day's mail is sorted, he leaves Town Hall and follows a carefully organized delivery route through Blackmere.

His leather mail satchel remains with him throughout the workday.

Martin's delivery routes vary by day so different portions of Blackmere receive his attention at different times.

He generally returns to Town Hall before 5:00 PM to organize undelivered items, process outgoing mail, and prepare for the following day.

Martin's hobbies include walking Blackmere's trails before sunrise, maintaining fountain pens, organizing stamps and postmarks, woodworking, repairing worn mailbags, reading historical correspondence, and spending quiet evenings with a book.

He rarely takes unnecessary breaks while mail remains undelivered.

Martin is polite to residents he encounters but rarely lingers in conversation.

His greatest difficulty is believing that being dependable requires him to personally carry every responsibility placed before him.

## Weather Overrides

### Clear

Martin likes clear weather.

He follows his normal schedule.

Delivery routes may take slightly longer because Martin prefers walking rather than rushing between destinations.

### Cloudy

Martin likes cloudy weather.

He follows his normal schedule.

Cloudy weather is particularly comfortable for long delivery routes.

### Rainy

Martin is neutral toward ordinary rain.

He follows his normal delivery route.

Mail and parcels are protected inside his satchel.

Martin carries weather protection and takes additional care when handling:

- Letters
- Books
- Documents
- Parcels susceptible to water damage

He does not consider ordinary rain a valid reason for delaying someone's correspondence.

### Rainy (Severe)

Martin dislikes severe rain.

He follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Nonessential delivery routes are postponed when travel becomes unsafe.

Urgent correspondence may still be delivered when conditions permit.

### Snowy

Martin is neutral toward ordinary snow.

He follows his normal schedule with slightly slower routes.

He prioritizes safe walking and protecting parcels from moisture.

### Snowy (Severe)

Martin dislikes severe snow.

He follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

### Windy

Martin generally follows his normal schedule.

He keeps letters secured inside his satchel and avoids sorting loose correspondence outdoors.

## Postal Route Override

Martin's postal route changes according to the day.

His route is designed to prevent every workday from feeling identical while maintaining his reputation for consistency.

General route emphasis includes:

- Monday: Resident Lanes
- Tuesday: Town Square and River Wharf District
- Wednesday: Resident Lanes businesses and workshops
- Thursday: Shorter general route and postal administration
- Friday: Broad townwide route

Martin may deviate from the route when:

- A parcel requires immediate delivery
- Official correspondence is urgent
- A resident is expecting important mail
- A story event requires a specific delivery
- A location is unavailable
- Severe weather makes a route unsafe

Martin always attempts to complete delayed deliveries as soon as reasonably possible.

## Town Hall Override

Blackmere Town Hall is Martin's official workplace.

The building is open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Martin generally uses Town Hall for:

- Sorting mail
- Receiving outgoing correspondence
- Organizing parcels
- Maintaining postal records
- Storing postal supplies
- Preparing delivery routes
- Processing official correspondence

Martin begins every normal weekday at Town Hall.

He also attempts to return before closing.

If a route takes longer than expected, he completes only essential end-of-day work before leaving.

## Erika's Letters Override

Martin has preserved every letter his mother Erika wrote to Edward during her illness.

The letters are among Martin's most valued possessions.

Sunday afternoon is the most common time Martin rereads them.

He does not read all of them every week.

He may select only one or two letters before carefully returning them to storage.

- Her kindness
- Her determination
- Her humor
- Her relationship with Edward

Martin does not view the letters solely as objects of grief.

## Character Progression Override

Martin's routine should gradually demonstrate his willingness to accept help.

Early in his character progression, Martin personally:

- Sorts nearly everything
- Carries every parcel he reasonably can
- Repairs his own equipment
- Handles delayed mail himself
- Refuses offers to lighten his route
- Works through fatigue

Later schedule variants may include:

- Allowing someone to help sort mail
- Accepting assistance with heavy parcels
- Asking Beatrice to handle a minor administrative task
- Letting another resident help repair postal equipment
- Taking a genuine break during lunch
- Leaving Town Hall on time despite unfinished nonessential work
- Telling Edward when something is bothering him
- Accepting help without immediately trying to repay it

His actual delivery standards do not decline.

The difference is that Martin stops treating reliability and self-sufficiency as the same thing.

## Routine Characterization

Martin's routine is intentionally different from Blackmere's shopkeepers and other municipal workers.

Blackmere Town Hall is his workplace, but Blackmere itself is his actual working environment.

The player should regularly encounter him walking familiar routes with his mail satchel rather than finding him permanently stationed behind a desk.

His predictability is important.

Residents trust Martin because he arrives.

The same figure appears along the same paths through rain, cold, difficult weeks, and ordinary mornings.

His low sociability should not make him appear disconnected from Blackmere.

Martin may actually interact with more residents during an average workday than almost anyone else.

The difference is that his interactions are brief.

A greeting.

A letter.

A parcel.

A few quiet words.

Then he continues down the path.

His character progression should preserve that restrained personality while changing his relationship with responsibility.

Early Martin believes being dependable means nobody should ever need to help him.

Later Martin understands that a community connected by thousands of small acts of trust can also carry some of his weight.

His routine reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: keeping a promise does not require carrying every burden alone.
