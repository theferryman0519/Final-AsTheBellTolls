# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Letting Go Doesn't Mean Letting Go of Purpose

Character Arc Start State: Roland believes his role in shaping Blackmere has come to an end. Though proud of what the town has become, he quietly steps aside, hesitant to offer guidance unless someone explicitly asks for it.

Character Arc End State: Roland realizes experience is most valuable when it's shared. Rather than remaining on the sidelines, he embraces becoming a mentor whose quiet wisdom helps the next generation build an even brighter future.

Core Belief: A community is measured by how well its people care for one another.

Core Misbelief: The younger generation needs room to grow without my involvement.

Core Traits: Loyal, Patient, Reflective

Defining Sentence: ”A town isn’t built from wood and stone, but from the people within.”

Dialogue Length: Mid

Emotional Need: To understand that offering guidance isn't interfering—it's another way of serving the people he loves.

Favorite Location: At Thread & Thimble with Leo

Fears: Roland fears becoming a relic of Blackmere's past rather than a meaningful part of its future. More than growing older, he worries that the lessons learned through decades of experience will quietly disappear if left unshared.

Goals: To watch Blackmere flourish under the next generation's leadership while helping others discover the confidence to lead with kindness, integrity, and humility.

Habit: Twirls his cane while walking.

History: Roland helped establish Blackmere alongside his wife, Agatha, during the town's earliest years, dedicating his life to building a close-knit community where neighbors looked after one another. Though never one to seek recognition, his steady leadership and unwavering dependability earned him the respect of generations of residents. As the years passed, he gladly stepped aside so others—especially his daughter, Beatrice—could guide the town forward, believing every generation deserves the opportunity to shape Blackmere's future in its own way.

Hobbies: Visiting Leo's tailor shop; woodworking; carving small toys for local children; fishing at quiet ponds; tending fruit trees; reading history; walking through town at sunrise; sharing tea with Agatha.

Motivation: Roland wants the people of Blackmere to inherit not only a thriving town, but a culture of kindness, patience, and mutual support that will endure for generations.

Personality Colors: Oak Brown, Deep Blue, Gold

Personality Summary: Roland is patient, dependable, and quietly wise. Rather than commanding attention, he listens first, speaks thoughtfully, and offers advice only when it's truly needed. Years of helping build Blackmere have taught him that lasting communities are created through countless small acts of kindness rather than grand achievements. Though naturally reflective and content to remain in the background, Roland's greatest lesson is realizing his experience still has tremendous value for those finding their own paths.

Public Persona: To most of Blackmere, Roland is one of the town's founding pillars—a man whose calm demeanor, fairness, and unwavering kindness helped shape the community into what it is today. Though retired, many residents still seek his advice, trusting his perspective even when he rarely offers it unprompted.

Secret: Roland quietly keeps a hand-drawn map of Blackmere from its earliest days, adding small notes whenever a new family arrives, a child is born, or a meaningful place changes. To him, the map isn't about buildings—it's a living record of the people who transformed a settlement into a home.

Speaking Tone: Gentle

Strength: Steady

Weakness: Passive

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Roland follows a relaxed and deliberate routine centered around his family, old friendships, Blackmere's community, and the places that remind him how much the town has changed.

Roland is retired and has no formal workplace.

He lives at 1 Resident Lane with his wife Agatha and daughter Beatrice.

1 Resident Lane is available from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM every day.

Roland spends considerably more time outside his home than Agatha.

He enjoys walking through Blackmere, observing residents, visiting friends, tending fruit trees, fishing, woodworking, reading history, and spending quiet time with Agatha.

His favorite location is Thread & Thimble with Leo.

Thread & Thimble is available from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM Tuesday through Sunday.

Thread & Thimble is closed Monday and during severe rain or severe snow.

Thread & Thimble first becomes available on Day 23 of Summer, Year 1.

Roland's friendship with Leo is particularly important to his weekly routine.

He enjoys sitting in the tailor shop while Leo works, listening to him talk, offering occasional advice when asked, and watching someone from a younger generation build something of his own.

Roland's hobbies include visiting Thread & Thimble, woodworking, carving toys for children, fishing, tending fruit trees, reading history, walking through town at sunrise, and sharing tea with Agatha.

Although many residents respect Roland's experience, he rarely approaches someone specifically to give advice.

Early in his character progression, he assumes the younger generation should be allowed to solve its own problems unless someone explicitly asks for his perspective.

His greatest difficulty is recognizing that sharing experience does not mean taking control away from someone else.

## Weather Overrides

### Clear

Roland likes clear weather and generally follows his normal schedule.

He particularly enjoys:

- Sunrise walks
- Fishing
- Tending fruit trees
- Sitting outdoors
- Walking between districts

He may remain outdoors slightly longer when the weather is pleasant.

### Cloudy

Roland is neutral toward cloudy weather.

He follows his normal schedule.

### Rainy

Roland is neutral toward ordinary rain.

He continues most of his normal routine but shortens unnecessary outdoor walks.

Fishing is usually replaced with:

- Reading history
- Woodworking
- Visiting friends
- Spending time with Agatha

### Rainy (Severe)

Roland dislikes severe rain.

He follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Thread & Thimble and the A. Whitmore Public Library are closed during severe rain.

Optional travel is canceled.

### Snowy

Roland is neutral toward ordinary snow.

He shortens his sunrise walk and spends additional time:

- Woodworking
- Reading
- Sharing tea with Agatha
- Visiting accessible indoor locations

### Snowy (Severe)

Roland dislikes severe snow.

He follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Thread & Thimble and the A. Whitmore Public Library are closed.

### Windy

Roland is neutral toward windy weather.

He generally follows his normal schedule.

Fishing may be replaced when conditions make it unpleasant.

## Thread & Thimble Override

Thread & Thimble is Roland's favorite location.

Leo is Roland's best friend.

Thread & Thimble is available from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM Tuesday through Sunday.

The shop is closed Monday.

It is also closed during:

- Rain (Severe)
- Snow (Severe)

Thread & Thimble first becomes available on Day 23 of Summer, Year 1.

Roland regularly visits on Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday.

He does not visit because he needs clothing.

He enjoys:

- Watching Leo work
- Talking with Leo
- Listening to Leo's ideas
- Seeing new designs
- Watching customers interact with Leo's work
- Occasionally helping with a practical task
- Simply sitting somewhere he feels welcome

Roland does not dominate these conversations with advice.

Early in his character progression, he waits for Leo to ask before offering much guidance.

Leo may occasionally ask Roland's opinion about:

- A difficult decision
- A customer
- Blackmere
- Confidence
- Creativity
- Relationships
- Running a business
- Whether something is worth pursuing

Roland's friendship with Leo is one of the clearest places where his character arc can become visible.

## Leo Mentorship Override

Although Leo is Roland's best friend, their relationship also naturally develops into an informal mentorship.

Roland never treats Leo like a child.

He respects Leo's independence, creativity, and ability to make his own decisions.

Early Roland primarily:

- Listens
- Encourages
- Answers questions when asked
- Avoids volunteering advice

As Roland progresses through his character arc, he becomes more willing to say:

- What he has observed
- What he learned from his own mistakes
- What he believes Leo should consider

He still does not tell Leo what decision to make.

Roland learns that mentorship means giving someone more tools with which to make their own choice.

## Agatha Override

Agatha is Roland's wife.

Their marriage is one of the most stable relationships in Blackmere.

Roland tries to share breakfast, dinner, and evening tea with Agatha whenever possible.

Possible activities together include:

- Drinking tea
- Reading
- Discussing Blackmere
- Sitting quietly together
- Walking around their garden
- Talking about Beatrice
- Remembering Blackmere's earliest years

Roland and Agatha do not require constant conversation.

Long periods of comfortable silence are normal for them.

Agatha is also one of the few people who openly challenges Roland when she believes he is becoming too passive.

She may point out that refusing to offer advice can sometimes be just as consequential as giving too much of it.

## Beatrice Override

Beatrice is Roland and Agatha's daughter.

Roland deliberately avoids interfering with Beatrice's leadership as Mayor.

He is proud of her and believes Blackmere belongs to her generation to guide.

Early in his character progression, Roland rarely offers political or leadership advice unless Beatrice directly asks him.

Possible interactions include:

- Family meals
- Quiet conversations at home
- Discussing Blackmere's history
- Listening to Beatrice talk through a difficult decision
- Reassuring her without solving the problem for her

Roland's restraint is well-intentioned but sometimes excessive.

As his character progression develops, he becomes more comfortable offering relevant experience while still respecting Beatrice's authority.

## Founder's Map Override

Roland keeps a hand-drawn map of Blackmere dating back to the town's earliest years.

Sunday is his most consistent period for reviewing and updating it.

The map contains notes documenting:

- New families
- Births
- Important relationships
- New buildings
- Changed buildings
- Restored locations
- Places that disappeared
- Meaningful community spaces
- Major events

Roland does not treat the map as an architectural record.

Some physically important changes receive only a small notation.

A seemingly ordinary home may receive several notes because of the people who lived there.

As Blackmere is restored, the map should gradually accumulate additional markings.

The player may eventually discover the map through friendship or story progression.

## Town Restoration Routine Override

Roland's behavior changes as Blackmere moves from emergency recovery toward a town that can sustain itself without his generation directing every decision.

### Weathered / Rebuilding

Roland frequently pauses during walks to examine flood damage and restoration work. He remains watchful of civic decisions and is more likely to spend time near Town Hall or damaged public spaces.

### Recovering / Renewed / Growing

Roland begins revisiting restored locations, noting changes on his map, and spending more time speaking with younger residents rather than inspecting work. He becomes increasingly willing to offer advice only when asked.

### Prospering / Flourishing

Roland's routine shifts decisively toward leisure, mentorship, Agatha, Leo, Town Square, and ordinary community life. He takes pride in seeing a Blackmere that no longer depends upon his generation to sustain it.

He fishes.

He drinks tea with his wife.

Roland's problem is not that he has nothing left to contribute.

His knowledge is not valuable because the past was better.

It is valuable because he remembers how people navigated the difficult moments that came before.

His routine reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: stepping aside can give others room to grow, but experience only becomes a legacy when someone is willing to share it.
