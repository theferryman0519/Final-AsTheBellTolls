# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Loyalty to the Past Shouldn't Prevent a Future

Character Arc Start State: Walter faithfully maintains the life he and Opal built together, refusing to change more than necessary. He believes that moving forward too much would feel like leaving her behind.

Character Arc End State: Walter realizes that carrying Opal's memory forward means continuing to live the life they dreamed of together, not preserving it exactly as it was.

Core Belief: The people who depend on you deserve consistency.

Core Misbelief: If I let my life change, I'll lose the last part of Opal I have left.

Core Traits: Loyal, Pragmatic, Stubborn

Defining Sentence: ”Cherish everything and everyone you love as often as you can.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To accept that love isn't measured by how long you mourn, but by how you continue living with the memories you've been given.

Favorite Location: At Memorial Hill in front of Opal’s grave marker

Fears: Walter fears waking up one day and realizing he can no longer remember Opal's voice, her laugh, or the ordinary conversations they shared. More than being alone, he fears slowly forgetting the woman who made his life feel complete.

Goals: To keep the Blackmere General Store running as reliably as it always has, providing the town with the stability and familiarity people have relied upon for decades.

Habit: Grabs an opal from his pocket and admires it while thinking.

History: Walter and his wife, Opal, spent years building the Blackmere General Store into one of the town's most dependable gathering places. During the Great Flood, Opal lost her life helping others reach safety, leaving Walter to carry on alone. Though he rarely speaks about his grief, he pours himself into maintaining the store exactly as they once ran it together. His quiet routines, stubborn dedication, and unwavering reliability have become his way of honoring the life they built, even if it has left little room for a future beyond remembering her.

Hobbies: Repairing old shelves instead of replacing them; fishing before sunrise; sharpening hand tools; tending a small vegetable patch behind the store; carving simple wooden trinkets during quiet evenings; visiting Memorial Hill at sunset.

Motivation: Walter wants Blackmere to always have a place where people can find what they need, just as they always could when Opal stood beside him.

Personality Colors: Muted Brown, Slate Gray, Forest Green

Personality Summary: Walter is practical, dependable, and deeply loyal. He isn't expressive, but his actions consistently reveal how much he cares for the people around him. Years of quiet routine have made him seem distant, yet beneath his dry humor and stubborn exterior is a man who still measures every decision against the life he once shared with Opal. Though resistant to change, Walter's heart has never hardened—he simply struggles to imagine a future that looks different from his past.

Public Persona: Most residents know Walter as the no-nonsense owner of the Blackmere General Store. He speaks plainly, wastes few words, and expects people to do things properly the first time. While some mistake his reserved demeanor for coldness, longtime residents know he's remarkably dependable and quietly generous whenever someone truly needs help.

Secret: Walter still sets two cups on the kitchen table every morning as he once did with Opal. He quietly puts the second cup at the end of the day, never mentioning the habit to anyone.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Perseverance

Weakness: Isolation

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Walter follows a highly consistent and practical routine centered around Blackmere General Store, the small vegetable patch behind it, and Memorial Hill.

As the owner of Blackmere General Store, Walter spends most working days managing inventory, stocking shelves, assisting customers, repairing store fixtures, handling deliveries, and ensuring Blackmere's residents can reliably purchase everyday materials and flower seeds.

Blackmere General Store is both Walter's residence and workplace.

The store is open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Sunday through Friday.

The store is closed every Saturday.

The store has no weather closures.

Walter remains at the General Store for nearly all public operating hours.

He does not remain behind the counter continuously.

Throughout the day, he moves between the customer area, shelves, stockroom, vegetable patch, and other areas of the store.

Walter prefers repairing shelves, fixtures, tools, and storage equipment rather than replacing anything that can still serve its purpose.

Saturday is Walter's only regular day away from public business.

He uses it for fishing, household maintenance, woodworking, gardening, and visiting Memorial Hill.

Memorial Hill is Walter's favorite location because Opal is buried there.

Walter visits her grave regularly, particularly near sunset.

His hobbies include repairing old shelves, fishing before sunrise, sharpening hand tools, tending a small vegetable patch behind the store, carving simple wooden trinkets, and visiting Memorial Hill.

Walter's greatest difficulty is accepting changes that make his life look different from the one he shared with Opal.

## Weather Overrides

### Clear

Walter likes clear weather and generally follows his normal schedule.

He particularly enjoys:

- Fishing
- Tending the vegetable patch
- Walking to Memorial Hill
- Performing outdoor repairs

### Cloudy

Walter likes cloudy weather and follows his normal schedule.

He considers cloudy weather good working weather because it is cool and rarely interrupts his routine.

### Rainy

Walter is neutral toward ordinary rain.

Blackmere General Store remains open.

He follows his normal professional schedule.

Outdoor gardening and maintenance are shortened or replaced with:

- Shelf repairs
- Tool sharpening
- Inventory organization
- Wood carving
- Stockroom maintenance

Ordinary rain does not prevent Walter from visiting Memorial Hill unless conditions make the walk impractical.

### Rainy (Severe)

Walter dislikes severe rain.

Blackmere General Store remains open because it has no weather closures.

Walter follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Optional travel to Memorial Hill and fishing are canceled.

Severe rain puts Walter into a noticeably quieter mood because of the Great Flood and Opal's death.

### Snowy

Walter is neutral toward ordinary snow.

Blackmere General Store remains open.

He follows his normal store schedule but shortens outdoor activities.

### Snowy (Severe)

Walter dislikes severe snow.

Blackmere General Store remains open.

He follows the Severe Weather Schedule.

Optional travel is canceled.

### Windy

Walter is neutral toward windy weather.

He follows his normal schedule.

He checks:

- Exterior signs
- Flower-seed displays
- Loose crates
- Vegetable-patch supports
- Store fixtures

## Store Closure Override

Blackmere General Store is closed every Saturday.

Because Blackmere General Store is also Walter's residence, the closure prevents public shopping but does not prevent Walter from remaining inside privately.

Walter's normal Saturday schedule already functions as his weekly Store Closure Override.

Saturday is Walter's primary personal day.

He uses it for:

- Fishing
- Vegetable gardening
- Store repairs
- Tool sharpening
- Woodworking
- Visiting Memorial Hill
- Spending time at Opal's grave

Walter does not use Saturday as a normal inventory or customer-service day.

He may repair something inside the shop because repairing things is genuinely one of his hobbies.

If severe weather occurs on Saturday, the Severe Weather Schedule takes priority.

He notices:

- Dirt on the stone
- Wilted flowers
- Weeds
- Fallen leaves
- Weather damage
- Anything that looks out of place

Early in his character progression, Walter sometimes behaves as though maintaining the grave perfectly is another way of maintaining their marriage.

He begins telling Opal more about new things happening in his life rather than only remembering their old life together.

## Two Cups Override

Walter sets two cups on the kitchen table every morning.

One is his.

The other was Opal's.

He performs the routine almost automatically.

The second cup remains untouched throughout the day.

Walter quietly puts it away during the evening.

He never discusses the habit.

Early in his character progression, this occurs every day.

As his arc progresses, the behavior may gradually change.

Possible progression states include:

- The second cup remains on the table for less time
- Walter occasionally pauses before setting it out
- He sometimes places the cup back in the cupboard unused
- Eventually, he prepares only one cup on an ordinary morning

The change should not be treated as Walter forgetting Opal.

It represents his realization that remembering her does not require recreating their old morning exactly.

## Victor Friendship Override

Their friendship can occasionally create useful contrast.

- Bring Walter unfamiliar goods

- Tell him about stores elsewhere

These interactions can support Walter's character arc by showing that change does not automatically erase what already works.

## General Store Restoration Override

Walter's routine changes as Blackmere General Store is restored.

### Weathered

Walter spends additional time:

- Repairing shelves
- Protecting surviving inventory
- Patching damaged fixtures
- Working around empty displays
- Maintaining the store with limited resources

Walter resists replacing anything he believes can still be repaired.

### Rebuilding

Structural repairs occur throughout the store.

Walter is protective of old fixtures and occasionally argues against replacing them.

### Recovering

More shelves become stocked and the store begins feeling familiar again.

Walter's mood improves subtly.

### Renewed

The General Store becomes fully functional.

Some new fixtures replace pieces that could no longer be saved.

Walter initially struggles with these changes.

### Growing

Decorative displays and flower boxes make the shop visibly different from the one he and Opal originally ran.

Walter gradually begins acknowledging that the changes are good.

### Prospering

Expanded displays and improved furnishings allow the store to serve Blackmere better.

Walter becomes increasingly willing to adopt practical improvements.

### Flourishing

The General Store becomes one of Blackmere's busiest centers of daily commerce.

Walter finally accepts that the store can remain the place he and Opal built while also becoming something they never had the opportunity to see.

## Character Progression Override

Walter's routine should change gradually rather than dramatically.

He does not become outgoing.

He does not stop visiting Opal.

He does not suddenly renovate everything he owns.

Instead, small signs of willingness to move forward begin appearing.

Possible progression changes include:

- Replacing a shelf that truly cannot be repaired
- Trying one of Victor's new product suggestions

## Routine Characterization

Walter's routine is intentionally predictable.

He opens the same store at the same time, maintains the same shelves, tends the same vegetable patch, and follows habits he developed over decades with Opal.

That reliability makes him valuable to Blackmere.

The problem is that Walter has gradually stopped distinguishing between consistency and preservation.

The store has become both his livelihood and a monument to the life he lost.

His visits to Memorial Hill make that conflict visible, but the two-cup ritual communicates it even more quietly.

Walter does not need to stop loving Opal in order to change.

His progression should instead show him becoming willing to create new routines alongside the old memories.

His routine reinforces the central lesson of his character arc: carrying someone with you does not require standing still where they left you.
