# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Loyalty to the Past Shouldn't Prevent a Future

Character Arc Start State: Walter faithfully maintains the life he and Opal built together, refusing to change more than necessary. He believes that moving forward too much would feel like leaving her behind.

Character Arc End State: Walter realizes that carrying Opal's memory forward means continuing to live the life they dreamed of together, not preserving it exactly as it was.

Core Belief: The people who depend on you deserve consistency.

Core Misbelief: If I let my life change, I'll lose the last part of Opal I have left.

Core Traits: Loyal, Pragmatic, Stubborn

Defining Sentence: ”Cherish everything and everyone you love as often as you can.”

Dialogue Length: Short

Emotional Need: To accept that love isn't measured by how long you mourn, but by how you continue living with the memories you've been given.

Favorite Location: At Memorial Hill in front of Opal’s grave marker

Fears: Walter fears waking up one day and realizing he can no longer remember Opal's voice, her laugh, or the ordinary conversations they shared. More than being alone, he fears slowly forgetting the woman who made his life feel complete.

Goals: To keep the Blackmere General Store running as reliably as it always has, providing the town with the stability and familiarity people have relied upon for decades.

Habit: Grabs an opal from his pocket and admires it while thinking.

History: Walter and his wife, Opal, spent years building the Blackmere General Store into one of the town's most dependable gathering places. During the Great Flood, Opal lost her life helping others reach safety, leaving Walter to carry on alone. Though he rarely speaks about his grief, he pours himself into maintaining the store exactly as they once ran it together. His quiet routines, stubborn dedication, and unwavering reliability have become his way of honoring the life they built, even if it has left little room for a future beyond remembering her.

Hobbies: Repairing old shelves instead of replacing them; fishing before sunrise; sharpening hand tools; tending a small vegetable patch behind the store; carving simple wooden trinkets during quiet evenings; visiting Memorial Hill at sunset.

Motivation: Walter wants Blackmere to always have a place where people can find what they need, just as they always could when Opal stood beside him.

Personality Colors: Muted Brown, Slate Gray, Forest Green

Personality Summary: Walter is practical, dependable, and deeply loyal. He isn't expressive, but his actions consistently reveal how much he cares for the people around him. Years of quiet routine have made him seem distant, yet beneath his dry humor and stubborn exterior is a man who still measures every decision against the life he once shared with Opal. Though resistant to change, Walter's heart has never hardened—he simply struggles to imagine a future that looks different from his past.

Public Persona: Most residents know Walter as the no-nonsense owner of the Blackmere General Store. He speaks plainly, wastes few words, and expects people to do things properly the first time. While some mistake his reserved demeanor for coldness, longtime residents know he's remarkably dependable and quietly generous whenever someone truly needs help.

Secret: Walter still sets two cups on the kitchen table every morning as he once did with Opal. He quietly puts the second cup at the end of the day, never mentioning the habit to anyone.

Speaking Tone: Dry

Strength: Perseverance

Weakness: Isolation
