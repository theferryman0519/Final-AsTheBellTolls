# Personality Information

Dialogue Length: Long

Personality Colors: Charcoal, Crimson, Steel Gray

Personality Summary: Rupert is an experienced politician who believes progress is achieved through decisive leadership rather than public consensus. Intelligent, articulate, and highly strategic, he carefully weighs every decision against its long-term political and economic consequences. While rarely raising his voice or losing his composure, Rupert possesses an unwavering confidence that often borders on arrogance. He respects competence above all else and has little patience for sentimentality, believing difficult decisions are simply the price of effective governance.

Public Persona: To the citizens of Morvanya, Rupert presents himself as a calm, capable statesman committed to economic growth and governmental stability. His speeches are polished, his manners impeccable, and his confidence reassuring. Beneath that respectable image, however, Rupert views communities as assets to be managed rather than homes to be cherished, quietly maneuvering to bring Blackmere under greater state control whenever an opportunity presents itself.

Speaking Tone: Dry
