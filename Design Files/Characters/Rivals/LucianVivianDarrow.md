# Personality Information

Character Arc: Learning That Trust Is Stronger Than Control

Character Arc Start State: Lucian/Vivian believes discipline, preparation, and respecting the natural passage of time are the only responsible ways to protect people. They view the grandfather clock's power as a dangerous temptation, no matter how noble the intention.

Character Arc End State: Lucian/Vivian comes to understand that time itself isn't inherently dangerous—it's the heart of the person wielding it that determines whether it becomes a blessing or a curse. Though they never fully embrace time manipulation, they learn to trust the player rather than fear the power.

Core Belief: Time should be respected, never controlled.

Core Misbelief: If people are given the power to change time, they'll eventually believe they're entitled to control everything.

Core Traits: Disciplined, Perceptive, Reliable

Defining Sentence: ”Time deserves our respect before it becomes lost.”

Dialogue Length: Long

Emotional Need: To accept that trust sometimes requires surrendering certainty and that not every outcome can—or should—be controlled.

Favorite Location: Around the base of the Bell Tower in the Town Square

Fears: Lucian/Vivian fears watching history repeat itself because someone believed they knew better than time itself. More personally, they fear betraying Myrtle's teachings by allowing the grandfather clock's power to be used irresponsibly.

Goals: To preserve Myrtle's legacy as a master clockmaker while proving that craftsmanship, patience, and perseverance can improve lives without altering the natural flow of time.

Habit: Checks their pocket watch whenever the Bell Tower tolls.

History: Lucian/Vivian apprenticed under Myrtle for several years, quickly earning a reputation for exceptional precision and discipline. Shortly before the Great Flood, Myrtle began treating them less like an apprentice and more like a fellow clockmaker, entrusting them with increasingly complex restorations. During that time, they also learned fragments of the Pendrelle family's closely guarded secret surrounding the manor's grandfather clock and its ability to manipulate time. Witnessing the devastation left by the flood only strengthened their conviction that such power should never be used, believing Myrtle intended the clock to remain protected rather than relied upon. Since then, Lucian/Vivian has dedicated themselves to preserving traditional clockmaking while quietly watching the player's growing connection to the manor with equal parts concern, respect, and reluctant curiosity.

Hobbies: Restoring antique pocket watches; sketching gear mechanisms; studying historical timepieces; maintaining the Bell Tower clock; journaling observations about mechanical designs; taking long evening walks through Blackmere to listen for clocks striking the hour.

Motivation: Lucian/Vivian wants to prove that patience, craftsmanship, and honest effort are enough to rebuild Blackmere without depending on powers that could ultimately do more harm than good.

Personality Colors: Steel Blue, Brass, Ivory

Personality Summary: Lucian/Vivian is disciplined, perceptive, and deeply principled. Every decision is weighed carefully, and every piece of craftsmanship reflects their belief that excellence comes through patience rather than shortcuts. Though often reserved and formal, they possess a quiet compassion that reveals itself through actions more than words. Their skepticism toward time manipulation isn't born from arrogance, but from a genuine fear that even the best intentions can lead people astray when they believe they can rewrite what should simply be lived.

Public Persona: Most residents know Lucian/Vivian as Blackmere's talented young clockmaker—a meticulous craftsperson whose standards are as exacting as Myrtle's once were. Professional, dependable, and somewhat reserved, they have earned widespread respect for preserving the town's clocks and mechanical traditions with remarkable care.

Secret: Lucian/Vivian still carries the first pocket watch they ever attempted to build completely on their own. It never kept accurate time, and despite possessing the skill to repair it now, they've intentionally left it broken as a reminder that perfection isn't achieved by never failing.

Speaking Tone: Formal

Strength: Steadfastness

Weakness: Fear of Losing Control

# Schedule & Routine

## General Routine

Lucian/Vivian follows a disciplined and highly structured routine centered around traditional clockmaking, mechanical study, 6 Resident Lane, and the Blackmere Bell Tower.

Lucian/Vivian has no formal public workplace.

They perform most private clockmaking, restoration, drafting, calibration, and mechanical research from 6 Resident Lane.

6 Resident Lane is available from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

The Blackmere Bell Tower is one of Lucian/Vivian's most important recurring work locations.

The Bell Tower is available from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

The Bell Tower has no regular days closed and no weather closures.

Lucian/Vivian regularly assists Irene with maintenance, inspections, calibration, and mechanical issues involving the tower.

The area around the base of the Bell Tower is also Lucian/Vivian's favorite location.

They frequently remain in Town Square after completing mechanical work to observe the tower, listen to its tolls, sketch ideas, or simply sit somewhere surrounded by Blackmere's clocks and routines.

Lucian/Vivian's hobbies include restoring antique pocket watches, sketching gear mechanisms, studying historical timepieces, maintaining the Bell Tower clock, journaling mechanical observations, and taking long evening walks through Blackmere to listen for clocks striking the hour.

They check their pocket watch whenever the Bell Tower tolls.

Their routine is intentionally organized around predictable blocks of work.

Unexpected changes frustrate them, particularly when those changes affect something mechanical or time-sensitive.

Their greatest difficulty is accepting uncertainty when careful planning and preparation cannot guarantee an outcome.

## Weather Overrides

### Clear

Lucian/Vivian dislikes clear weather.

They still follow their normal schedule.

Clear weather does not interfere with their work, but they find bright, open conditions less comfortable than subdued weather.

They are somewhat more likely to remain inside the workshop during optional outdoor periods.

### Cloudy

Lucian/Vivian likes cloudy weather.

They follow their normal schedule.

Cloudy conditions are their preferred weather for:

- Walking
- Bell Tower inspections
- Mechanical sketching outdoors
- Sitting in Town Square

### Rainy

Lucian/Vivian likes ordinary rain.

They generally follow their normal schedule.

Rain creates a quiet atmosphere they find conducive to:

- Clock restoration
- Journaling
- Technical drafting
- Bell Tower maintenance
- Listening carefully to mechanical sounds

Outdoor evening walks are shortened if necessary.

### Rainy (Severe)

Lucian/Vivian is neutral toward severe rain.

The Blackmere Bell Tower remains available.

They follow the Severe Weather Schedule.

They may assist Irene with additional structural and mechanical inspections when conditions warrant.

### Snowy

Lucian/Vivian is neutral toward ordinary snow.

They generally follow their normal schedule with shorter outdoor walks.

### Snowy (Severe)

Lucian/Vivian is neutral toward severe snow.

They follow the Severe Weather Schedule.

### Windy

Lucian/Vivian is neutral toward windy weather.

They generally follow their normal schedule.

They listen for unusual vibration, movement, or resonance around mechanical structures exposed to the wind.

## Routine Characterization

Lucian/Vivian's routine is intentionally precise.

Their life revolves around mechanisms that function because every component moves when and where it is supposed to.

That worldview extends beyond clockmaking.

They plan carefully, prepare thoroughly, and believe uncertainty should be reduced whenever possible.

The Bell Tower is therefore more than their favorite location.

It represents their ideal relationship with time.

The bell does not hurry.

It does not go backward.

It reaches the next hour exactly when it should.

Their conflict with the grandfather clock comes directly from that worldview.

Time Manipulation represents the possibility that someone can decide the natural sequence is insufficient.

Lucian/Vivian's progression should not end with them deciding they were completely wrong.

Their caution has value.

The change is that they eventually recognize control has limits too.

They cannot guarantee what the player will do.

They cannot guarantee every invention will work.

They cannot preserve Myrtle's legacy by making every decision she might have made.

At some point, they have to trust.

Their broken first pocket watch reinforces the same lesson.

It is imperfect.

It failed.

And Lucian/Vivian deliberately kept it that way.

Their routine reinforces the central lesson of their character arc: responsibility does not mean controlling every possible outcome—it means preparing carefully, choosing wisely, and trusting the people who have earned that trust.
