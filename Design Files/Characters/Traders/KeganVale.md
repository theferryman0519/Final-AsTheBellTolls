# Personality Information

Dialogue Length: Mid

Personality Colors: Sapphire Blue, Forest Green, Amber

Personality Summary: The Vale siblings are seasoned international traders who have spent their lives traveling between distant towns and cultures. Friendly, observant, and endlessly curious, they have a gift for making strangers feel like old friends within moments of meeting them. Their calm confidence comes from years on the road, and they take genuine joy in introducing people to goods, stories, and traditions from beyond Blackmere. Though each sibling oversees a different season's trade route, they all share the same easygoing charm, professionalism, and unwavering belief that every exchange is an opportunity to build lasting relationships.

Public Persona: The Vale siblings are welcomed visitors whose arrival marks the beginning of a new season. Always courteous and well-traveled, they bring unique goods from across Morvanya and beyond while eagerly sharing stories of the places they've visited. To most residents, they represent the wider world beyond Blackmere and serve as a reminder that even a quiet town remains connected to something much larger.

Speaking Tone: Gentle
