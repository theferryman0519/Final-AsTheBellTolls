## Special Events

- Day 3 at 7:00pm: Residents remain gathered around Town Square later than usual for the first informal evening of summer. Shops leave their doors open, residents linger outside, and musicians play near the fountains until dusk.

- Day 11 at 8:00pm: Lanterns are hung along the bridges and waterways throughout Blackmere. They remain lit until midnight as part of an old summer tradition welcoming travelers arriving after sunset.

- Day 19 at 6:00pm: Several households and businesses place tables outside and eat their evening meals outdoors. NPC schedules temporarily shift so residents gather in small groups throughout town rather than returning directly home.

- Day 28 at 9:00pm: Town Square remains unusually active after nightfall for the final long evening of summer. Residents gather around the fountains and remain outdoors later than their normal schedules.
