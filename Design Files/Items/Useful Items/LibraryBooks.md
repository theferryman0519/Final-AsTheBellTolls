## Book 1

Author: Edith Greenwell

Category: Gameplay Tip

Display Name: A Beginner's Guide to Soil

ID: item_useful_beginner-guide-to-soil

Short Description: A practical guide on maintaining healthy farmland.

Text: New gardeners often mistake healthy soil for endless soil. They plant season after season without allowing the ground a chance to recover. Leave a tired plot alone for a few days, and it will reward your patience. Rush it, and the earth will remind you that even gardens need to rest.

Title: A Beginner's Guide to Soil
