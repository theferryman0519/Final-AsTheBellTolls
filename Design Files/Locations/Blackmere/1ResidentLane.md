## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Whitmore home is occupied but still visibly flood-worn and improvised. Agatha and Roland spend extra time maintaining the property, while Beatrice sometimes carries civic work home because the shared rooms are not yet fully dependable.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: Exterior masonry and carpentry are secure, laundry lines and household fixtures are usable, and the most important shared areas look intentionally repaired while still modest.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Agatha, Beatrice, and Roland spend less time compensating for damaged household space and return to more dependable home routines.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The porch, garden edge, and primary shared rooms become fully usable and visibly maintained.
    2) Updated Audio: Normal household ambience includes porch movement, dishes, garden activity, and quiet conversation.
    3) Updated NPC Routines: Agatha and Roland begin using the porch/garden while Beatrice moves more civic work back to Town Hall.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The home gains resident-specific furnishings and a stable family dining/living area rather than improvised shared space.
    2) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Recurring family meals and evening porch conversations become ambient household scenes.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The front garden and porch gain comfortable visitor seating and a more welcoming neighborhood-facing appearance.
    2) Updated Audio: Clear evenings add porch conversation, tea service, and garden ambience.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Occasional residents stop by for Agatha/Roland advice or informal conversations with Beatrice.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Mature beds, seasonal flowers, and household details reflect all three Whitmore residents.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: Flowers or produce from the household garden can visibly contribute to selected communal meals, memorials, or civic events.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The property gains refined seasonal dressing and a permanent neighborhood gathering setup without becoming a public building.
    2) Updated Audio: Seasonal tea/storytelling gatherings add small crowd ambience while normal days retain quieter residential sound.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: A small seasonal tea/storytelling gathering becomes a recurring Resident Lanes activity.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Neighbors can reference the Whitmore home as a trusted place for advice, stories, and informal community connection.
