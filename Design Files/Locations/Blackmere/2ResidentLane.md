## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - Martin Ashcroft lives here while only part of the house is comfortably usable. Mail sacks and parcels are kept inside because dry storage is limited, causing his home and postal responsibilities to overlap visibly.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The roof and entry are secure, dry parcel storage is available, and Martin has a small organized area that separates postal materials from normal living space.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Martin spends less of his off-hours protecting or relocating mail and returns to a more normal postal and home routine.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: A dry front sorting room, labeled parcel shelves, and an organized correspondence area become usable.
    2) Updated Audio: Paper sorting, parcel handling, and door activity become the defining sounds during Martin's home-work periods.
    3) Additional Function or Service: After the relevant postal progression, selected parcel pickup or non-critical mail retrieval can occur from the residence.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Postal overflow is reduced and Ashcroft family objects/furnishings return to the living spaces.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Martin gains regular evening home anchors instead of extending work-related sorting into most off-hours.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A covered delivery nook and weather-protected message box appear outside.
    2) Updated Audio: Daytime parcel drop-offs, paper handling, and brief doorstep interactions add activity to the lane.
    3) Additional Function or Service: The delivery nook can hold selected non-critical player mail or overflow parcels as a convenience point.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The porch becomes an orderly neighborhood exchange point with baskets, labels, and seasonal notice ribbons.
    2) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Nearby residents occasionally use the porch for small handoffs, messages, or brief conversations with Martin.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The property becomes a recognizable Resident Lanes message hub with refined correspondence displays and rotating seasonal notices.
    2) Updated Audio: Occasional doorstep greetings, parcel exchanges, and paper/box activity make the property audibly active without becoming a shop.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Town Hall notices, Inn guest correspondence, and Caravan/trader messages can generate occasional mail activity routed through Martin.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Residents can acknowledge messages, parcels, or news that reached them through Martin's restored home-post network.
