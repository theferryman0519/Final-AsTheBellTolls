## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - Adrian Lockwood and Sabrina Fairchild share cramped usable space. Books and school materials are stacked wherever they can remain dry, and both residents often stay at the Library or School longer because home is not yet comfortable for extended work or study.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: A dependable shared study area, stable shelving, a lesson-preparation surface, and separated book/school storage make the home comfortably usable for evening work.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Adrian and Sabrina can spend selected evening work periods at home instead of remaining at the Library or School solely because the residence is uncomfortable.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The reading desk and lesson-prep table become fully functional while work materials are organized into resident-specific areas.
    2) Updated Audio: Page turning, writing, quiet study, and normal household ambience define the residence during evening work periods.
    3) Updated NPC Routines: Both residents gain dependable home study/preparation blocks, making the house visibly occupied after work.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Personal shelves, teaching supplies, books, and shared furnishings turn the space into a stable home rather than overflow storage.
    2) Updated NPC Dialogue: Home-specific dialogue can reference reading together, lesson preparation, rest, and recovered comfort.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A front reading bench and small neighborhood reading area become usable.
    2) Updated Audio: Clear evenings add page turning, soft conversation, and child/adult passersby near the bench.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Children and selected adults can briefly stop at the reading bench or visit Adrian/Sabrina outside work hours.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The home gains a prepared small-group table, shared reading materials, and rotating school/library projects.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: School and Library restoration can unlock a small book/lesson club hosted here on selected evenings.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Donated/recovered texts and education-related keepsakes rotate through the home, visually reflecting Blackmere's learning culture.
    2) Updated Audio: Rare club nights add small-group reading, discussion, and child voices while ordinary evenings remain quiet.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Rare education-centered cross-NPC scenes can occur here among children, teachers, readers, and family/friends.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Adrian, Sabrina, and visiting NPCs can reference shared lessons, books, or community learning that now extends beyond their workplaces.=
