## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - Irene Calder keeps the residence sparse and practical while prioritizing her Bell Tower duties. Damaged clock- and bell-maintenance tools occupy the safest usable room, and the home feels functional rather than settled.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: Irene has a proper bell-record and mechanism-maintenance corner with organized tools, ledgers, and secure storage.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Irene gains a predictable at-home maintenance block and spends less off-duty time improvising around damaged storage.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Repaired exterior chimes and small metalwork details return to the property.
    2) Updated Audio: Controlled ticking, light mechanism work, and occasional exterior chimes give the residence a deliberate timekeeping ambience.
    3) Updated NPC Routines: Irene occasionally performs minor clock/bell mechanism maintenance at home.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Windows, lighting, furnishings, and organized records make the home feel calm and deliberately ordered.
    2) Updated NPC Dialogue: Irene can reference the house returning to a dependable rhythm synchronized with her Bell Tower responsibilities.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A small historic bell/memorial display appears outside after appropriate story progress.
    2) Updated Audio: Passersby and light chime ambience create a subtle point of interest without competing with the Bell Tower.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Residents occasionally stop at the display or speak with Irene about bellkeeping/history.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The work corner expands with teaching notes, restored instruments, and examples of Blackmere bellkeeping practice.
    2) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Irene can occasionally mentor or consult another NPC on timekeeping, maintenance, or local tradition.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The residence becomes a polished living extension of Blackmere's timekeeping heritage with festival-responsive details.
    2) Updated Audio: Unique restrained chime responses can occur on festival days or significant bell events.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Bell Tower festival/status changes can trigger matching home ambience or display behavior here.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Irene and visitors can connect the restored home, Bell Tower, and Blackmere's recovered sense of rhythm.
