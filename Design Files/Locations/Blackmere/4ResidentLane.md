## Restoration Progression

* Weathered:
    - Irene keeps the home sparse and practical, prioritizing Bell Tower duty; damaged clock/bell-related tools sit in the safe room.

* Rebuilding:
    - Repairs create a proper work corner for bell records and maintenance tools; Irene spends less time improvising storage.

* Recovering:
    - Exterior chimes/metalwork return; Irene can occasionally be seen maintaining small mechanisms at home.

* Renewed:
    - The house gains a calm, precise routine synced to the town bell; lighting/windows make her presence visible at predictable hours.

* Growing:
    - A small memorial or historic bell display appears outside after relevant story progress, giving passersby a reason to stop.

* Prospering:
    - Irene begins mentoring/consulting others on bellkeeping traditions; occasional NPC visits create social motion.

* Flourishing:
    - The home becomes a living extension of Blackmere's timekeeping heritage, with special ambient chime details that subtly respond to festival days.
