## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Library remains open enough for the player to read available Library Books, but damaged shelves and closed sections restrict circulation. Adrian works around the unusable areas, and few residents stay longer than necessary.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: A dependable reading zone, stable repaired shelving, book carts, and a clear circulation path reopen a meaningful portion of the Library while later-stage sections remain visibly modest or unavailable.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Adrian gains a reliable public-service area, and selected residents begin making brief regular visits to the reopened reading zone.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Additional shelves, circulation space, reading tables, and the donation area become orderly and usable.
    2) Updated Audio: Quiet footsteps, page turning, shelving, and low reading-room ambience make the Library feel actively used.
    3) Updated NPC Routines: Children and adults begin scheduled reading/library visits during normal hours.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Restored collections and the Preservation Display Case become prominent public/story anchors.
    2) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Predictable small reading groups, school visits, or paired NPC reading periods begin appearing.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Reading nooks and/or garden seating create additional indoor/outdoor reading anchors.
    2) Updated Audio: Occupied nooks add soft conversation, page turning, outdoor birds/foliage, and occasional child voices.
    3) Additional Function or Service: Adrian/library research can provide broad discovery hints for selected books, artifacts, memories, or inventions.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: An expanded collection/display area accommodates visiting research materials and rotating themed exhibits.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: School, Hall of Wonder, and Archive/memory progression can generate visiting scholars, research prompts, or display content here.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Rotating Blackmere-history displays and recovered texts turn the Library into a visible memory institution.
    2) Updated Audio: Special lecture/research days add restrained crowd and discussion ambience while normal days stay quiet.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Scholars, students, and residents can attend occasional lectures/research gatherings tied to completed content.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Adrian and visitors can reference specific recovered histories, donated works, and the Library's role in preserving Blackmere.
