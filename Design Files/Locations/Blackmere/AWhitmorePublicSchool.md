## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The School is still operating, but classes are compressed into limited usable space and the grounds are visibly flood-damaged. Sabrina and the children maintain a basic school routine, while unusable play areas and damaged facilities make the school day feel constrained.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: One dependable classroom, a safe access path, and a functional basic teaching area allow the School to operate without relying entirely on improvised space.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Sabrina and the children use the restored classroom as their dependable school-day anchor while the remaining school facilities are still limited.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Remaining core classroom space and portions of the grounds/play area become safely usable.
    2) Updated Audio: Full class ambience includes chalk and writing sounds, footsteps, and children before and after lessons.
    3) Updated NPC Routines: Full class-day schedules resume and children begin using the grounds before and after lessons.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Student projects, lesson displays, repaired furnishings, and school identity return throughout the building.
    2) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Parent/community visits, school-project moments, and small class-related gatherings become available.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Garden/play improvements create dedicated after-school activity anchors.
    2) Updated Audio: After-school play, garden activity, and small-group conversation extend the school's soundscape beyond class hours.
    3) Additional Function or Service: Occasional after-school activities or small student/community requests become available.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Workshop/demo resources and expanded learning displays reflect a school connected to Blackmere's trades and professions.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: Restored workshops, Library, Farms, Fishery, or other professions can provide scheduled guest lessons or demonstrations.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Seasonal student work and future-focused projects become polished enough to appear both at the School and around Blackmere.
    2) Updated Audio: Special project/exhibition days add parents, visiting professionals, and children presenting work.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Student projects can visibly decorate Town Hall, festivals, Library, or other civic locations during relevant seasons.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Children and adults begin discussing education, apprenticeships, and futures in Blackmere rather than only recovery.
