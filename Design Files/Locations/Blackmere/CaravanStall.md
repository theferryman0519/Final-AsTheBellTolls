## Information

- Location for player to shop for random goods.
- All goods sold are 18 random items, no matter the season.
- Located in Town Square District.

---

## Availability

- First Available: Day 1 of Spring, Year 1
- Hours: 10am to 6pm
- Days Closed: Sunday
- Weather Closures: Rain (Severe), Snow (Severe)

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - When the Caravan is present, the player can shop from its current random selection of goods, but the trading pitch is little more than a rough temporary stop. Shelter is limited, traders pack quickly, and few Blackmere residents linger.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Caravan pitch has level ground, secure tie-down points, and a dependable awning while still retaining its temporary traveling-market identity.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Traders gain orderly setup and teardown routines, and a small number of locals begin stopping by during visits.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Secure storage, signage, and organized crates make the stop dependable enough for recurring trade.
    2) Updated Audio: Setup sounds, wagon/crate handling, bargaining, and light browsing ambience replace a mostly empty stall.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Locals begin scheduled browsing alongside the player during caravan visits.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Seating/signage and repeated-trader details make the Caravan visit feel like a recognized Blackmere routine.
    2) Updated NPC Dialogue: Traders can reference specific improvements, restored businesses, or goods they now recognize from Blackmere.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A local-export display area appears alongside imported/random goods.
    2) Updated Audio: Busier trade days add local producer handoffs, browsing, and crate movement.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Eligible restored farms/trades can contribute one rotating export good or special Blackmere order to the Caravan.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Specialty-order markers, additional trade crates, and small crowd anchors signal higher-value exchange.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Specialty requests or Blackmere-linked exchange opportunities become available during selected visits.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Rotating regional flags, recognizable imported/export crates, and visitor staging make the stall a true exchange point.
    2) Updated Audio: Full trade-day ambience includes bargaining, wagon handling, local shoppers, and visiting voices.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Inn guests, Bank/trade health, and local producers can influence visiting trader types or special opportunities.
    4) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Traders, producers, and townspeople can form small exchange scenes that visibly reconnect Blackmere to the region.
