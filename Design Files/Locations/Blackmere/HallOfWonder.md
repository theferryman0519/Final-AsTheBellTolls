## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The player can donate gemstones and artifacts, but damaged cases and closed museum sections make donations feel closer to rescue and preservation than public exhibition. Dante focuses primarily on keeping surviving pieces safe.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: One gallery is safe and presentable, with repaired display cases and protected exhibit space while the unopened sections remain visibly unavailable.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Dante shifts from pure preservation work toward cataloguing, arranging, and maintaining the first public gallery.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Additional cases and collection sections open, allowing donations to appear as organized public exhibits.
    2) Updated Audio: Museum footsteps, case handling, quiet visitor ambience, and Dante's public-facing activity create a functioning gallery soundscape.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Regular local visitors begin appearing, with visitation increasing as collection sections fill.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Hall gains complete visitor pathways, school/library visit areas, and stronger exhibit presentation.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: School and Library schedules can include museum visits, lessons, or research tied to displayed collections.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A rotating exhibit/demonstration zone adds seasonal themes beyond permanent donation cases.
    2) Updated Audio: Demonstration days add small crowds, presentation sounds, and more active gallery ambience.
    3) Additional Function or Service: Rotating themed exhibits or gemstone/artifact demonstrations provide reasons to revisit after basic donations.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Visiting-scholar workspace, appraisal materials, and richer exhibit interpretation show the Hall's growing reputation.
    2) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Outside scholars, collectors, or traders can visit for lore, appraisal, or special requests.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Completed collections, Blackmere-history material, and signature galleries turn the Hall into a major cultural landmark.
    2) Updated Audio: Busy exhibit days and special presentations create a mature museum soundscape while quiet days remain contemplative.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Library/Archive, School, Inn visitors, Rupert/civic pride, and regional guests can all feed activity into the Hall.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Dante, residents, and visitors can reference specific collections and the Hall as proof Blackmere has a past worth preserving and a future worth visiting.
