## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The player can still upgrade tools with Marcus Rowan, but the Forge operates around patched equipment and a limited number of safe work stations. Work is slower and the building feels like a surviving workshop rather than a dependable town trade.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The core forge, ventilation, primary work surface, and order racks are dependable enough for normal smithing work, though the shop remains simple.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Marcus spends less time keeping damaged equipment operational and more time at a stable forging station.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Organized tool racks, component storage, and safe forging stations make normal work dependable.
    2) Updated Audio: Regular hammering, bellows/fire, quenching, and tool handling create the dependable rhythm of normal forge work.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Residents begin scheduled tool drop-offs/pickups, showing the Forge serves Blackmere as well as the player.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Finished tools and components appear on racks tied to active upgrades or town work.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Selected higher-quality/advanced tool-upgrade handling becomes available according to the existing tool progression.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A demonstration/commission area and visible civic components appear beside normal forging work.
    2) Updated Audio: Commissioned work adds component assembly and occasional visitor/workshop conversation to the forge soundscape.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Craftspeople or residents visit for civic components, demonstrations, or scheduled collaboration.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Marcus-made fixtures/components visibly appear both in the Forge and on restored Blackmere projects.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: Woodworks, Design Studio, civic inventions, and restoration projects can require or display Forge-made specialized parts.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Masterwork displays, Showcase components, and refined workstations mark the Forge as a regional craft landmark.
    2) Updated Audio: Full-production days layer confident forging, assembly, visitors, and occasional Showcase preparation.
    3) Additional Function or Service: Rare masterwork/Showcase commission interactions become available at the highest stage.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Marcus and visiting NPCs can reference specific town fixtures, inventions, or Showcase work produced by the restored Forge.
