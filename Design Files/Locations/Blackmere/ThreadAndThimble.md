## Information

- Location for player to shop for clothing and alter their apparance and clothes.
- Residence for Leo Finch.
- Located in Trades Quarter District.
- All clothing items sold are 8 random items, no matter the season.
    * 1 Outfit
    * 1 Headwear
    * 1 Footwear
    * 1 Accessory
    * 2 Bottoms
    * 2 Tops

---

## Availability

- First Available: Day 23 of Summer, Year 1
- Hours: 10am to 7pm
- Days Closed: Monday
- Weather Closures: Rain (Severe), Snow (Severe)

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The player can purchase the available clothing rotation and alter their appearance, but Leo sells from limited racks and much of his work is still practical mending. Clothing around Blackmere remains visibly patched and repaired, reinforcing the town's Weathered state.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The sewing floor, fitting area, racks, and mending stations are dependable again while the available clothing remains practical and visibly rooted in Blackmere's recovery.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Leo gains predictable sewing and fitting work blocks and can visibly complete occasional resident commissions.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Dependable fitting space, organized fabric storage, and fuller racks restore normal tailoring service.
    2) Updated Audio: Sewing and needle work, fabric handling, fitting-room movement, and light customer ambience make the shop feel routinely active.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Residents begin scheduled fittings/pickups, making tailoring visible beyond the player's purchases.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Rotating window displays, color coordination, and resident-specific commissions make the shop expressive rather than merely functional.
    2) Updated NPC Dialogue: Leo and browsing NPCs can comment on seasonal style, repairs, and restored confidence in personal presentation.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A dye/alteration work area and small style-consultation space become visible.
    2) Updated Audio: Fittings, fabric cutting, dye work, and browsing create a more active boutique soundscape.
    3) Additional Function or Service: Custom-dye/alteration or small style-request interactions unlock without changing the core clothing inventory model.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: NPC mannequins/displays and town clothing visibly shift toward better-maintained seasonal garments.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: Festivals, weddings, prosperity, and selected NPC progression can cause Thread & Thimble work to appear on NPC outfits around town.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Signature Blackmere styles, festival garments, and wedding/formal pieces turn the shop into a cultural marker.
    2) Updated Audio: Busy commission periods and event preparation add richer customer/fabric/sewing ambience.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Festivals, Town Hall weddings, and community traditions can commission visible clothing sets from the shop.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Residents can recognize Leo's work on one another and discuss having room for beauty, celebration, and personal style again.
