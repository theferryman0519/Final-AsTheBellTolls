## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The player can use the Workbench to craft items and inventions, but Clara drafts around damaged tables, salvaged plans, and incomplete models. Most design work is focused on immediate recovery rather than long-term planning.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: Stable drafting tables, organized tools, and a proper restoration-plan area give Clara a dependable workspace while models and displays remain modest.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Clara gains regular drafting and Workbench blocks instead of working around damaged stations.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Organized model shelves, workbench space, and client-plan areas restore normal design/invention collaboration.
    2) Updated Audio: Drafting, paper and model handling, measuring, and light Workbench sounds create a focused design-studio ambience.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Clients/contractors occasionally visit Clara for restoration plans or invention collaboration.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Completed district models/maps and finished project plans turn the Studio into a visible record of Blackmere's restoration.
    2) Updated NPC Dialogue: Clara and visitors can reference specific completed projects and how the town's needs are changing.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Planning tables gain variant boards/material samples for selected civic projects.
    2) Updated Audio: Planning sessions add group discussion, model handling, and material/sample sounds.
    3) Additional Function or Service: Selected civic projects can offer curated cosmetic/function variants through Clara.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Staged Forge/Woodworks components and multi-shop project boards show Clara coordinating larger work.
    2) Cross-Location Synergy: Forge + Woodworks + Studio restoration can unlock or accelerate selected civic/invention projects.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Emergency-repair boards are replaced by future-facing plans, regional maps, and completed Blackmere models.
    2) Updated Audio: Occasional planning sessions create a confident collaborative studio sound rather than constant repair urgency.
    3) Additional Function or Service: The Studio becomes a Future Planning/design consultation location for post-repossession growth projects.
    4) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Clara can host multi-profession planning meetings for civic, beautification, or regional projects.
