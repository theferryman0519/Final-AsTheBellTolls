## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - Memorial Hill is home to Anya Thorne and Lena Thorne and remains the site of River Remembrance. The Hill is still solemn and usable, but damaged paths, weathered markers, sparse plantings, and flood-worn edges make everyday visits feel careful and subdued.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: Essential paths, retaining edges, damaged markers, and basic memorial plantings are carefully restored, making everyday visitation safer without making the site feel newly built.
    2) Updated NPC Routines: Lena and Anya gain safer, more predictable home and memorial routes, while selected residents begin resuming brief remembrance visits.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Repaired paths, cleaned markers, benches, and maintained flower areas make regular visitation possible.
    2) Updated Audio: Wind, birds, footsteps, soft foliage, and restrained visitor sounds create a cared-for but contemplative memorial ambience.
    3) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Small individual/family remembrance visits begin occurring outside the River Remembrance festival.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Recovered names, offerings, flowers, and quiet seating create a cared-for memorial rather than a damaged burial ground.
    2) Updated NPC Dialogue: Anya, Lena, and visiting residents can reference specific acts of remembrance and the difference between neglect and care.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A small oral-history/story marker or remembrance garden creates a deliberate reflection anchor.
    2) Updated Audio: Quiet visitor conversation, wind chimes/bells if appropriate, and seasonal nature sounds deepen the contemplative atmosphere.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Recovered memories/Archive content can add names, flowers, or contextual details to the Hill without changing the past.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Mature memorial plantings and festival-ready paths show sustained care rather than increasing spectacle.
    2) Additional NPC Gatherings at Location: Selected families/friends can gather here on anniversaries or quiet remembrance days.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Memorial Hill becomes a fully maintained living memorial with mature plantings, preserved markers, and subtle community offerings.
    2) Updated Audio: Seasonal wind, birds, footsteps, soft voices, and River Remembrance-specific layers distinguish everyday reflection from festival observance.
    3) Cross-Location Synergy: Hall of Wonder and River Remembrance content can add contextual memorial elements or visiting routines.
    4) Updated NPC Dialogue: Residents can frame the Hill as a place where Blackmere remembers while continuing to live, rather than only a symbol of the flood.
