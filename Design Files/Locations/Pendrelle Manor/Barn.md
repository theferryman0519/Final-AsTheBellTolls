## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks
|-------------------|---|
| Weathered         | n/a
| Rebuilding        | 2 Max Barn Animals

| Renewed           | 6 Max Barn Animals
| Growing           | 8 Max Barn Animals
| Prospering        | 10 Max Barn Animals
| Flourishing       | 15 Max Barn Animals
