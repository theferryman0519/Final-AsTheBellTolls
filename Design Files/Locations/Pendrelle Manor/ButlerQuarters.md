## Information

- Living quarters for Edward.
- Butler Help is available starting at the Weathered stage (Edward will give tips on what to do next or aid on a quest).
- Daily Advice is available starting at the Rebuilding stage (Edward gives a tip or advice at the start of the day).
- Weather Forecast is available starting at the Recovering stage (Calendar will feature a weather forecast for the next 3 days).
- Manor Report is available starting at the Renewed stage (Animals and crops will have informational panels available to show growth details).
- Lost & Found is available starting at the Growing stage (Edward will gift the player a different non-food ingredient item at the start of each day).
- Groundskeeper is available starting at the Prospering stage (Edward will harvest any ready crop or flower, placing them in the Pantry or Storage Chest).
- Butler Tunnels are available starting at the Flourishing stage (Edward unlocks fast-travel locations across Blackmere).

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - Edward's quarters are serviceable from the beginning, but they are austere and visibly affected by the Manor's condition. His personal space functions more as a practical refuge than a fully restored living and administrative room. Butler Help: Edward can give the player guidance about what to do next or assist with a quest.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The room is fully repaired into a dependable personal office and bedroom, with an organized writing desk, restored shelving, and a clearer place for Edward's daily notes and Manor records.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Daily Advice: Edward provides a tip or piece of advice at the start of the day.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Traditional Study — orderly desk, dark wood, neatly stacked correspondence.
        - Variant B: Practical Steward — maps, ledgers, task lists, estate-management materials.
        - Variant C: Quiet Gentleman — reading chair, lamp, books, understated personal belongings.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Weather instruments, charts, almanacs, and observation notes become part of the room's fixed visual language, reinforcing Edward's new forecasting service.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Weather Forecast: the Calendar displays a forecast for the next three days.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Weather Scholar — brass instruments, charts, barometer, forecast notes.
        - Variant B: Window Observer — telescope/binoculars, window-side charts, weather journal.
        - Variant C: Estate Meteorology — old Pendrelle instruments restored alongside newer equipment.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: Ledgers, organized reports, estate diagrams, and recordkeeping materials make the room feel like the Manor's operational office as well as Edward's living quarters.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Manor Report: informational panels become available for animals and crops to show growth details.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Ledger Room — bound reports, organized shelves, formal recordkeeping.
        - Variant B: Planning Room — wall maps, pins, task boards, estate schedules.
        - Variant C: Correspondence Room — writing desk, letters, town notices, carefully filed records.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A dedicated recovered-items shelf or cabinet appears for Lost & Found, while the room gains more personal belongings and signs that Edward is no longer living provisionally.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Lost & Found: Edward gives the player a different nonfood ingredient item at the start of each day.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Curator's Shelf — recovered objects arranged like small historical finds.
        - Variant B: Lost & Found Cabinet — labeled drawers, baskets, categorized belongings.
        - Variant C: Memory Shelf — recovered Manor objects mixed with photographs and keepsakes.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Groundskeeping tools, baskets, estate maps, and neatly maintained work equipment show Edward's expanded role across the restored Manor grounds.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Groundskeeper: Edward harvests ready crops or flowers and places them in the Pantry or Storage Chest.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Groundskeeper's Quarters — baskets, gloves, pruning tools, garden notes.
        - Variant B: Estate Steward — formal Manor-management tools, maps, keys, schedules.
        - Variant C: Gentleman Gardener — botanical books, pressed plants, polished gardening equipment.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Hidden service-door details, tunnel maps, keys, and restored estate-routing references visually reveal the old Butler Tunnel network as part of the Manor's fully recovered infrastructure.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Butler Tunnels: Edward unlocks fast-travel locations across Blackmere.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Pendrelle Steward — elegant traditional quarters honoring Edward’s long service.
        - Variant B: Keeper of Blackmere — town maps, recovered artifacts, community correspondence.
        - Variant C: Master of the Manor — tunnels, keys, ledgers, maps, and every aspect of the estate neatly integrated.
