## Information

- Holds the Grandfather Clock.
- Time Manipulation is available starting at the Rebuilding stage, starting with the Advance mechanic.
- The Preserve mechanic is available starting at the Recovering stage.
- The Synchronize mechanic is available starting at the Renewed stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Central Room begins as the damaged heart of Pendrelle Manor. The broken grandfather clock dominates the room, and the surrounding space feels incomplete and quiet because the Manor's defining mechanism is not yet functioning.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The grandfather clock is operational again and becomes the restored focal point of the room. Basic seating, flooring, walls, and lighting are repaired enough for the Central Room to feel usable.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Time Manipulation becomes available with a daily maximum of 5 Chimes, with the use of the Advance mechanic.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Pendrelle Heritage — dark wood clock surround, traditional rug, warm lamps.
        - Variant B: Blackmere Restoration — reclaimed local wood, stone accents, hand-restored furnishings.
        - Variant C: Inventor's Revival — brass details, exposed clockwork accents, practical work surfaces.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The clock housing, pendulum area, surrounding woodwork, and room lighting become more stable and refined, making the space feel intentionally centered around timekeeping rather than emergency repair.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Daily Chime maximum increases to 6, with the added use of the Preserve mechanic.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Preserved Parlor — carefully restored antique seating and family objects.
        - Variant B: Botanical Hall — restrained plants, floral textiles, natural motifs.
        - Variant C: Clockmaker's Hall — horological diagrams, brass instruments, old clock components.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Central Room regains the appearance of a proper Manor gathering and planning space, with more complete trim, furnishings, and presentation around the Grandfather Clock.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Daily Chime maximum increases to 8, with the added use of the Sychronize mechanic.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Grand Hall — symmetrical furnishings, formal rug, restored chandelier.
        - Variant B: Community Hall — warmer seating clusters, local crafts, welcoming gathering space.
        - Variant C: Workshop Salon — elegant furnishings mixed with drafting tools and subtle mechanisms.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The room becomes more established as the Manor's central planning hub, with richer furnishings and more deliberate visual connections between the clock, estate records, and the rest of the home.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Daily Chime maximum increases to 10.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Legacy Gallery — family portraits, Manor artifacts, Pendrelle history.
        - Variant B: Blackmere Gallery — maps, town memorabilia, local artwork.
        - Variant C: Invention Gallery — selected completed inventions and technical sketches.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Higher-quality finishes, more polished clockwork presentation, and refined lighting make the room feel prestigious without losing the practical warmth of an inhabited home.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Daily Chime maximum increases to 12.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Regency Revival — refined historical elegance and rich finishes.
        - Variant B: Prosperous Blackmere — sophisticated local materials and artisan craftsmanship.
        - Variant C: Temporal Study — subtle clockwork motifs, brass inlays, time-themed decorative elements.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Central Room reaches its complete legacy presentation: the Grandfather Clock, architecture, furnishings, and surrounding keepsakes read together as the fully restored emotional and mechanical heart of Pendrelle Manor.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Daily Chime maximum increases to 15.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Myrtle's Legacy — restored close to the Manor’s remembered family character.
        - Variant B: Heart of Blackmere — community gifts, town accomplishments, local craftsmanship.
        - Variant C: Master Inventor's Hall — tasteful synthesis of Manor heritage and the player's inventions.
