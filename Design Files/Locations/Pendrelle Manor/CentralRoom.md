## Information

- Holds the Grandfather Clock.
- Time Manipulation is available starting at the Rebuilding stage, starting with the Advance mechanic.
- The Preserve mechanic is available starting at the Recovering stage.
- The Synchronize mechanic is available starting at the Renewed stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks
|-------------------|---|
| Weathered         | n/a
| Rebuilding        | Time Manipulation / Advance Mechanic / 5 Chimes
| Recovering        | Preserve Mechanic / 6 Chimes
| Renewed           | Synchronize Mechanic / 8 Chimes
| Growing           | 10 Chimes
| Prospering        | 12 Chimes
| Flourishing       | 15 Chimes

* Weathered:
    - The broken grandfather clock is a focal wound in the house; no Chimes, irregular ticks or silence.

* Rebuilding:
    - Clock runs again; Advance mechanic and 5 Chimes introduce targeted intervention with obvious clock-face feedback.

* Recovering:
    - Clock case/pendulum stabilizes; Preserve mechanic unlock is represented by a second ring/hand that can “hold” a state.

* Renewed:
    - Full face mechanism returns; Synchronize mechanic unlock uses paired hands/markers to align two processes.

* Growing:
    - Room becomes a planning hub with visible time-linked project markers; Chime capacity increase feels like mastery, not only a larger bar.

* Prospering:
    - Clock interfaces with key manor/town inventions; advanced temporal effects visibly resonate through nearby mechanisms.

* Flourishing:
    - At Flourishing, the clock is Myrtle's legacy made whole: 15 Chimes plus rare civic resonance uses. The room should host story/family/community scenes so the mechanical heart is also the emotional heart.
