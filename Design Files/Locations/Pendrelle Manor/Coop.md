## Information

- Holds all Coop animals (Chicken, Duck, and Goose)
- Coop animals can start being purchased at the Rebuilding stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks
|-------------------|---|
| Weathered         | n/a
| Rebuilding        | 2 Max Coop Animals
| Recovering        | 4 Max Coop Animals
| Renewed           | 6 Max Coop Animals
| Growing           | 8 Max Coop Animals
| Prospering        | 10 Max Coop Animals
| Flourishing       | 15 Max Coop Animals
