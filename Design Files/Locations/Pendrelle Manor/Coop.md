## Information

- Holds all Coop animals (Chicken, Duck, and Goose)
- Coop animals can start being purchased at the Rebuilding stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Coop begins empty, damaged, and unsuitable for birds. Nesting areas are unsafe and the yard lacks the organization needed for animal care. Coop animals cannot yet be purchased or housed.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The first usable nesting and feeding spaces are restored, basic fencing is repaired, and the Coop is visibly safe enough to house a small flock.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 2 Max Coop Animals.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Simple Timber Nests — plain nesting boxes and natural wood.
        - Variant B: Whitewashed Coop — pale boards, clean trim, traditional farm appearance.
        - Variant C: Stone & Timber Coop — reclaimed stone base with darker wooden nesting areas.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Additional nesting areas, cleaner flooring, improved perches, and more organized feed/water placement make the space feel actively maintained.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 4 Max Coop Animals.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Country Nesting — woven baskets, straw storage, simple textiles.
        - Variant B: Garden Coop — small plants, climbing greenery, floral accents.
        - Variant C: Heritage Coop — restored old feeders, brass labels, Pendrelle-era details.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Coop becomes clean and dependable, with complete nesting, collection, feeding, and access areas that visually support routine animal care.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 6 Max Coop Animals.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Clean Farmhouse — organized nests, matching feed storage, light finishes.
        - Variant B: Blackmere Rustic — handmade local fixtures and darker natural materials.
        - Variant C: Estate Poultry House — polished wood, orderly brass hardware, traditional design.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The structure and yard become fuller and more organized for a larger mixed flock, with more distinct bird-use areas and stronger seasonal life around the Coop.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 8 Max Coop Animals.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Springyard — colorful seasonal accents and flower boxes.
        - Variant B: Harvestyard — baskets, grain bundles, autumnal agricultural accents.
        - Variant C: Waterfowl Yard — pond-inspired blues/greens and details suited to ducks/geese.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: More durable finishes, refined storage, improved fixtures, and a mature yard presentation make the Coop feel like an established part of the Manor's food-production system.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 10 Max Coop Animals.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Artisan Coop — hand-crafted local fittings and decorative nest fronts.
        - Variant B: Estate Coop — refined traditional detailing and polished fixtures.
        - Variant C: Inventor Coop — subtle automated feeders and clockwork-inspired indicators.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Coop reaches a fully developed, orderly flock-house presentation with complete nesting, perching, storage, yard, and care zones suitable for its maximum capacity.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 15 Max Coop Animals.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Country Menagerie — warm, lively, varied farmhouse decoration.
        - Variant B: Pendrelle Poultry House — elegant historic estate appearance.
        - Variant C: Modern Flock House — clean, efficient styling with tasteful invention integration.
