## Information

- Holds all Coop animals (Chicken, Duck, and Goose)
- Coop animals can start being purchased at the Rebuilding stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks
|-------------------|---|
| Weathered         | n/a
| Rebuilding        | 2 Max Coop Animals
| Recovering        | 4 Max Coop Animals
| Renewed           | 6 Max Coop Animals
| Growing           | 8 Max Coop Animals
| Prospering        | 10 Max Coop Animals
| Flourishing       | 15 Max Coop Animals

* Weathered:
    - Unsafe nesting space remains empty and quiet.

* Rebuilding:
    - Two birds return; eggs/nests and small morning routines make the yard lively.

* Recovering:
    - Mixed flock support increases; ducks/geese/chickens use differentiated areas where possible.

* Renewed:
    - Clean nesting/feeding flow makes byproduct collection visually understandable and less cluttered.

* Growing:
    - Expanded flock adds yard social behavior and seasonal touches; family/NPCs may stop to watch/feed birds.

* Prospering:
    - Coop products visibly enter kitchen/market loops; quality storage or invention synergy is represented physically.

* Flourishing:
    - Fifteen-bird capacity culminates in a bustling but orderly coop with species-specific behaviors and a signature flock-management convenience beyond the number cap.
