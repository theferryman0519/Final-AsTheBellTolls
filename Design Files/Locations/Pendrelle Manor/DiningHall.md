## Information

- Hosts the Toll of Hearths festival on Day 25 of Winter. If still in Weathered stage, festival is skipped for that year. Only player, Edward, and Martin celebrate.
- Guests are available starting at the Rebuilding stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Dining Hall begins largely unusable as a gathering space. Its scale hints at the Manor's former communal life, but damaged or covered furnishings leave it unable to host guests. Guests cannot yet be hosted in the Dining Hall.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: A functional dining arrangement, restored lighting, repaired flooring, and usable seating return the Hall to small-group use without making it feel fully grand.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 9 Max Guests.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Family Table — warm wood, simple chairs, modest tableware.
        - Variant B: Formal Pendrelle — darker furniture, traditional place settings, restrained elegance.
        - Variant C: Blackmere Gathering — handcrafted local tableware, mixed woods, welcoming atmosphere.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Additional seating and table space, restored wall details, and a more complete service area make larger meals visually believable.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 12 Max Guests.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Candlelit Hall — candles, warm fabrics, intimate lighting.
        - Variant B: Garden Hall — floral arrangements, botanical textiles, lighter finishes.
        - Variant C: Heritage Hall — restored portraits, antique serving pieces, traditional décor.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The room regains the warmth and identity of a true Manor dining space, with complete table settings, stronger lighting, textiles, and seasonal/event presentation.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 15 Max Guests.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Celebration Hall — flexible seasonal decorations and brighter textiles.
        - Variant B: Community Hall — local crafts, Blackmere banners, communal seating character.
        - Variant C: Refined Dining Room — coordinated furnishings, polished service pieces, elegant presentation.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Hall expands visually into a flexible gathering venue, with more seating zones and enough finished space for larger meals and different guest arrangements.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 18 Max Guests.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Harvest Table — produce displays, seasonal wreaths, rustic abundance.
        - Variant B: Garden Banquet — flowers, greenery, softer fabrics, botanical centerpieces.
        - Variant C: Inventor's Supper — brass serving accessories, unusual lighting, subtle mechanical curiosities.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Dining Hall becomes a polished civic-scale gathering room, with refined fixtures, complete service presentation, and enough visual richness to support major communal events.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 24 Max Guests.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Grand Estate Dining — formal linens, chandelier, elegant table settings.
        - Variant B: Blackmere Banquet Hall — local craftsmanship and town gifts displayed proudly.
        - Variant C: Warm Family Hall — comfortable furnishings, photographs, keepsakes, lived-in richness.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Hall reaches its full ceremonial and communal presentation, capable of seating all guests and visually supporting the largest shared meals and Toll of Hearths at full scale.
    2) Additional Function or Service: All Guests can be hosted.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Pendrelle Grand Hall — fully restored historic Manor elegance.
        - Variant B: Hall of Blackmere — town banners, community gifts, civic identity.
        - Variant C: Toll of Hearths Hall — warm lights, symbolic hearth motifs, permanent traces of its role as Blackmere's gathering place.
