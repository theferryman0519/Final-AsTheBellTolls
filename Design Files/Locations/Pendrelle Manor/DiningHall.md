## Information

- Hosts the Toll of Hearths festival on Day 25 of Winter.
- Guests are available starting at the Rebuilding stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks
|-------------------|---|
| Weathered         | n/a
| Rebuilding        | 9 Max Guests
| Recovering        | 12 Max Guests
| Renewed           | 15 Max Guests
| Growing           | 18 Max Guests
| Prospering        | 24 Max Guests
| Flourishing       | All Guests

* Weathered:
    - Large room is mostly closed/off-limits; furniture is covered and the scale of past gatherings is obvious.

* Rebuilding:
    - Nine guests can be hosted; first table/lighting returns and small dinners become possible.

* Recovering:
    - Twelve guests fill more of the room; NPC seating patterns and meal ambience make gatherings feel social rather than a cutscene backdrop.

* Renewed:
    - Fifteen guests allow meaningful community dinners and relationship scenes; decorations reflect season/events.

* Growing:
    - Eighteen guests support rotating themed meals or visiting groups; guest composition can react to relationships and town progress.

* Prospering:
    - Twenty-four guests make the hall a genuine civic venue; pre/post festival setup activity appears.

* Flourishing:
    - All guests culminates in Toll of Hearths at full scale, plus occasional late-game communal meals that show the Manor has become a shared home for Blackmere rather than private luxury.
