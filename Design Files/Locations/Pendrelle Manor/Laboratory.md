## Information

- Holds the Workbench and Tonic Kit for crafting items and inventions as well as crafting tonics.
- Also holds the Storage Chest for storing non-food items.
- Storage Chest is available starting at the Weathered stage.
- Workbench is available starting at the Rebuilding stage.
- Tonic Kit is available starting at the Renewed stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Laboratory begins with only the Storage Chest accessible. Myrtle's former work areas remain damaged, leaving the room more like protected storage than an operational workshop. Storage Chest with 16 Storage Slots.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Workbench area becomes fully usable with restored work surfaces, tool storage, shelving, and enough repaired room space to support invention crafting.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Workbench becomes available. Storage expands to 32 Storage Slots.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Myrtle's Workshop — restored old tools, wooden benches, inherited apparatus.
        - Variant B: Practical Workshop — organized tools, clean benches, efficient storage.
        - Variant C: Clockwork Workshop — exposed gears, brass fittings, visible mechanical experiments.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Additional benches, shelving, drawers, and organized material storage make the Laboratory feel capable of supporting multiple active projects.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 48 Storage Slots.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Drafting Laboratory — blueprints, drawing tables, measuring instruments.
        - Variant B: Prototype Laboratory — half-built devices, parts bins, test stands.
        - Variant C: Collector's Workshop — samples, minerals, components, labeled storage.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Tonic Kit gains a dedicated restored preparation area, visually separating herbal/tonic work from clockwork fabrication while keeping both within the same laboratory.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Tonic Kit becomes available. Storage expands to 64 Storage Slots.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Apothecary Laboratory — herbs, glassware, tonic equipment prominently integrated.
        - Variant B: Mechanical Laboratory — gears, metalworking tools, mechanical apparatus.
        - Variant C: Hybrid Laboratory — equal visual emphasis on tonics and invention crafting.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The room gains more display and staging space for components, prototypes, notes, and completed work, making the player's growing invention history visible.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 80 Storage Slots.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Gallery Workshop — completed inventions displayed neatly on shelves.
        - Variant B: Working Chaos — controlled clutter, active experiments, parts everywhere.
        - Variant C: Academic Laboratory — categorized displays, diagrams, carefully labeled specimens.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: More advanced work surfaces, higher-quality storage, refined instruments, and better organized testing areas give the room the appearance of a mature invention laboratory.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 96 Storage Slots.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Precision Laboratory — polished instruments, measurement equipment, refined test rigs.
        - Variant B: Temporal Laboratory — Chime-linked instruments and clock-inspired test apparatus.
        - Variant C: Industrial Laboratory — stronger machinery, reinforced benches, advanced fabrication equipment.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Laboratory reaches its full clockmaker-inventor presentation, with complete work zones, extensive storage, finished display areas, and a visually accumulated record of the player's craft.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 112 Storage Slots.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Myrtle's Legacy Laboratory — inherited workshop perfected and preserved.
        - Variant B: Master Inventor's Laboratory — distinctly the player's own mature workshop.
        - Variant C: Blackmere Institute — collaborative feeling with community gifts, inventions, and local materials.
