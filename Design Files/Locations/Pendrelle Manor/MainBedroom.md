## Information

- Contains the bed and dresser of the player, where they go to sleep at night and can change their clothes and appearance.
- Bed and Dresser are available starting at the Weathered stage.
- Consumer Bin is available starting at the Rebuilding stage.
- Dogs and Cats are available for purchase starting at the Recovering stage.
- Player stamina also increases with each restoration stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks                                   |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Weathered         | Bed / Dresser / 100 Max Stamina           |
| Rebuilding        | Consumer Bin / 120 Max Stamina            |
| Recovering        | 1 Max Dog / 1 Max Cat / 140 Max Stamina   |
| Renewed           | 1 Max Dog / 1 Max Cat / 160 Max Stamina   |
| Growing           | 2 Max Dogs / 2 Max Cats / 180 Max Stamina |
| Prospering        | 2 Max Dogs / 2 Max Cats / 200 Max Stamina |
| Flourishing       | 3 Max Dogs / 3 Max Cats / 220 Max Stamina |

* Weathered:
    - Bed/dresser are usable but the room feels temporary; the player is sleeping in inherited ruins, not yet living in a home.

* Rebuilding:
    - Consumer Bin + 120 stamina coincides with warmer lighting, repaired personal storage and the first sense of ownership.

* Recovering:
    - Pets unlock + 140 stamina; pet beds/bowls and animal movement immediately change the emotional life of the room.

* Renewed:
    - 160 stamina should add personal-display/customization anchors so the room reflects the player's history, clothing and relationship state.

* Growing:
    - Two dogs/cats + 180 stamina can add spouse/pet routines and morning/evening ambient scenes if married.

* Prospering:
    - 200 stamina is represented by comfort rather than luxury alone — better bed, insulation, quiet, and a stronger “well rested” home feeling.

* Flourishing:
    - 220 stamina + three pets should culminate in the player being visibly rooted: family photos/keepsakes, pet routines, spouse/child traces, and a morning reflection bonus or cosmetic ritual rather than another inventory function.
