## Information

- Contains the bed and dresser of the player, where they go to sleep at night and can change their clothes and appearance.
- Bed and Dresser are available starting at the Weathered stage.
- Consumer Bin is available starting at the Rebuilding stage.
- Dogs and Cats are available for purchase starting at the Recovering stage.
- Player stamina also increases with each restoration stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Main Bedroom is one of the few immediately usable personal spaces. The Bed and Dresser function, but the room still feels temporary and weathered rather than like a settled home. Bed and Dressera are available, along with the player having 100 Max Stamina.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: The room receives its first complete structural and comfort restoration, with repaired walls/floors, improved lighting, better personal storage presentation, and a fixed place for the Consumer Bin.
    2) Additional Function or Service: Consumer Bin. 120 Max Stamina.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Inherited Bedroom — restored Pendrelle furniture and traditional fabrics.
        - Variant B: Blackmere Bedroom — locally crafted furniture, warmer rustic materials.
        - Variant C: Personal Retreat — simpler furniture, lighter fabrics, player-focused cozy atmosphere.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Pet beds, bowls, and appropriate pet-use areas are incorporated into the restored room while the player's sleeping and dressing spaces become more comfortable.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 1 Max Dog and 1 Max Cat. 140 Max Stamina.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Pet-Friendly Room — pet beds, baskets, durable fabrics, playful details.
        - Variant B: Elegant Companion Room — coordinated pet furniture matching the bedroom.
        - Variant C: Country Companion Room — woven pet beds, natural materials, casual warmth.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The bedroom gains a more complete personal-home appearance, with stronger display space, richer textiles, improved lighting, and room for the player's collected belongings to feel intentional.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 1 Max Dog and 1 Max Cat. 160 Max Stamina.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Memory Wall — photographs, letters, relationship keepsakes.
        - Variant B: Achievement Display — awards, discoveries, showcase memorabilia.
        - Variant C: Personal Collection — favorite objects, books, flowers, clothing/accessory displays.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The room expands visually to accommodate more pets and, where applicable, spouse/household traces without making marriage a requirement for the stage to feel complete.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 2 Max Dogs and 2 Max Cats. 180 Max Stamina.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Family Bedroom — warm shared furnishings and spouse/family details.
        - Variant B: Romantic Bedroom — richer fabrics, softer lighting, intimate accents.
        - Variant C: Independent Retreat — reading/work corner, personal collections, individual identity.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: Higher-quality bedding, insulation, flooring, storage, lighting, and furnishings make the room feel restful and established rather than simply luxurious.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 2 Max Dogs and 2 Max Cats. 200 Max Stamina.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Grand Pendrelle Suite — refined historic furniture and luxurious traditional textiles.
        - Variant B: Blackmere Comfort — handcrafted furniture, layered fabrics, deeply cozy atmosphere.
        - Variant C: Inventor's Suite — clever storage, brass lighting, tasteful practical mechanisms.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Main Bedroom becomes the clearest visual statement that the player is rooted in Pendrelle Manor, with the room's final architecture, furnishings, keepsakes, pet accommodations, and personal history reading as one cohesive home.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 3 Max Dogs and 3 Max Cats. 220 Max Stamina.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Legacy Suite — family history, keepsakes, heritage furnishings.
        - Variant B: Rooted in Blackmere — community gifts, relationship memories, local craftsmanship.
        - Variant C: Life Well Lived — balanced collection of pets, family, achievements, inventions, and personal history.
