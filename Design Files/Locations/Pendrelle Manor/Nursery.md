## Information

- Holds any children that the player and their spouse have.
- Children are available to have and live in the Nursery starting at the Recovering stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks
|-------------------|---|
| Weathered         | n/a
| Rebuilding        | n/a
| Recovering        | 1 Max Child
| Renewed           | 1 Max Child
| Growing           | 2 Max Children
| Prospering        | 3 Max Children
| Flourishing       | 4 Max Children
