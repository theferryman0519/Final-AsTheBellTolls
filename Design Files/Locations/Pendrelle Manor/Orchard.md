## Information

- Holds soil squares for growing Orchard crops.
- Planting and harvesting crops is available starting at the Rebuilding stage.
- Greenhouse effect with roof is available starting at the Prospering stage (ability to plant crops from any season).

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks
|-------------------|---|
| Weathered         | n/a
| Rebuilding        | Farming / 6 Soil Squares
| Recovering        | 9 Soil Squares
| Renewed           | 12 Soil Squares
| Growing           | 15 Soil Squares
| Prospering        | Greenhouse Effect / 18 Soil Squares
| Flourishing       | 24 Soil Squares
