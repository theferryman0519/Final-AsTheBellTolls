## Information

- Holds all Stables animals (Horse)
- Horses can start being purchased at the Rebuilding stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

| Restoration Stage | Unlocks      |
|-------------------|--------------|
| Weathered         | n/a          |
| Rebuilding        | 1 Max Horse  |
| Recovering        | 1 Max Horse  |
| Renewed           | 1 Max Horse  |
| Growing           | 2 Max Horses |
| Prospering        | 2 Max Horses |
| Flourishing       | 3 Max Horses |

* Weathered:
    - Empty, damaged stalls emphasize lost mobility and estate decline.

* Rebuilding:
    - One horse returns, changing traversal immediately; grooming/tack props make ownership visible.

* Recovering:
    - Same one-horse capacity but better stable condition should improve life: shelter, grooming interaction, horse comfort animation or reduced care friction.

* Renewed:
    - Still one horse; Renewed should add a meaningful stable function such as saddle/loadout storage or a named horse bond milestone rather than no mechanical change.

* Growing:
    - Two horses permit choice between mounts or household use; spouse/NPC stable visits can appear.

* Prospering:
    - Two horses plus improved tack/storage can support travel convenience or mounted inventory, if scope allows.

* Flourishing:
    - Three horses culminates in a small working stable with distinct stalls and travel identity; a signature route/ride perk is more meaningful than capacity alone.
