## Information

- Holds all Stables animals (Horse)
- Horses can start being purchased at the Rebuilding stage.

---

## Restoration Progression

* Initial Weathered Stage:
    - The Stables begin empty and damaged, with unsafe stalls and worn tack/storage areas. The state of the building reflects the Manor's lost mobility and estate infrastructure. Horses cannot yet be purchased or housed.
* Weathered to Rebuilding:
    1) Updated Visuals: One stall becomes completely safe and usable, with restored feed/water areas, basic tack storage, repaired flooring, and a functional stable entrance.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 1 Max Horse.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Rough Estate Stable — repaired timber, simple tack, iron fittings.
        - Variant B: Whitewashed Stable — pale stall walls, dark beams, clean traditional look.
        - Variant C: Blackmere Stable — stone bases, reclaimed wood, locally forged hardware.
* Rebuilding to Recovering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The one-horse stable becomes more comfortable and organized through improved shelter, cleaner surfaces, better grooming space, and more complete storage.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 1 Max Horse.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Grooming Stable — brushes, blankets, wash equipment, care-focused details.
        - Variant B: Rider's Stable — saddle stands, bridles, travel equipment.
        - Variant C: Comfort Stable — softer bedding, warmer lighting, horse-comfort details.
* Recovering to Renewed:
    1) Updated Visuals: The Stables regain a polished, dependable one-horse presentation with complete stall fittings, organized tack, improved lighting, and a stronger estate-stable identity.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 1 Max Horse.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Traditional Tack Room — leather equipment, wooden cabinets, estate styling.
        - Variant B: Traveler's Tack Room — saddlebags, maps, route equipment.
        - Variant C: Organized Tack Room — labeled storage, clean equipment stations, functional design.
* Renewed to Growing:
    1) Updated Visuals: A second usable stall and expanded common care space make the building visibly capable of supporting two horses without feeling cramped.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 2 Max Horses.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Country Stable — warm rustic woods, woven blankets, relaxed character.
        - Variant B: Pendrelle Stable — polished stalls, brass fittings, traditional estate elegance.
        - Variant C: Blackmere Stable — local craftsmanship, regional textiles, hand-forged fixtures.
* Growing to Prospering:
    1) Updated Visuals: The two-horse stable receives higher-quality fixtures, refined tack/storage areas, stronger flooring and lighting, and a more established travel-and-riding presentation.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 2 Max Horses.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Traveler's Stable — route maps, saddlebags, mounted storage, expedition feel.
        - Variant B: Equestrian Stable — polished tack, grooming stations, refined riding equipment.
        - Variant C: Inventor-Assisted Stable — practical mechanical storage, brass indicators, subtle travel conveniences.
* Prospering to Flourishing:
    1) Updated Visuals: Three complete stalls, organized tack and care zones, finished circulation space, and the Stables' highest-quality architectural treatment create a small but fully realized working estate stable.
    2) Additional Function or Service: 3 Max Horses.
    3) Player Customization:
        - Variant A: Pendrelle Equestrian House — fully restored historic estate stable with elegant tack and fittings.
        - Variant B: Blackmere Riding Stable — warm local craftsmanship, regional textiles, and community-made details.
        - Variant C: Master Traveler's Stable — refined travel equipment, organized route gear, and subtle invention-assisted mobility.
