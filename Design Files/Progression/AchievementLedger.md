## Information

- Ledger holds the progress for all data in the game if the player is looking to 100% complete it.
- Ledger can be found in the Player Menu when pausing the game.
- Ledger matches the Achievement Quests:
  - Catching all fish
  - Cooking all meals and drinks
  - Crafting all tonics
  - Crafting all fabrications
  - Crafting all inventions
  - Maxing out all NPC Friendship and Connection Bonds
  - Fully restoring every room in Pendrelle Manor
  - Fully restoring all buildings in Blackmere
  - Fully funishing the Hall of Wonder museum
  - Fully furnishing the A. Whitmore Public Library
