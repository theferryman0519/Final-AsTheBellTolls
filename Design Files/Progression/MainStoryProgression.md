## Pre-Game (Before Day 1 of Spring, Year 1)

- Player starts in a carriage with the camera in a first-person view.
- Player looks down at a map of the region, which has the crest of the Office of the Vice Regent of Morvanya in the bottom corner.
- Several red Xs are crossing out many buildings within Blackmere, including Pendrelle Manor.
- Driver: "Blackmere? You sure? Flood hit this place harder than most last year."
- Player looks out the window as the carriage passes Town Hall, seeing the weathered facade.
- Driver: "Ah...that's right. You're Ms. Pendrelle's grandchild. Here about the manor."
- Player looks on the seat next to them and sees an envelope marked "To the Heir of Pendrelle Manor".
- Carriage starts to slow down and stop.
- Driver: "Well...this is you. Good luck."
- Carriage door opens and fades to the Avatar Creation Menu.
- Player creates their avatar, including name and appearance.
- Once created, the Avatar Creation Menu fades away.
- Player walks north on a dirt road leading to Edward and the front of Pendrelle Manor.
- Edward: "Forgive me. For a moment there...There's a bit of Myrtle in you."
- Edward: "I'm Edward Ashcroft. I served your grandmother as butler here at Pendrelle Manor."
- Edward: "I hope your journey here wasn't too uncomfortable. Did you open the envelope?"
- First letter from the envelope reads: "Last will and testament of Ms. Myrtle Pendrelle. I hereby name [Player] Pendrelle sole heir to Pendrelle Manor and its estate."
- Second letter from the envelope reads: "Office of the Vice Regent of Morvanya: Notice of Conditional Repossession"
- Edward: "The entire town received one. Pendrelle Manor is hardly unique in that regard."
- Edward: "After the flood, we waited for help. None came. Eventually, those notices did."
- Edward: "Three years to rebuild what was lost. Anything deemed beyond recovery will be repossessed and put to other use."
- Edward: "Blackmere is far from ruined, no matter what those markings on that map might suggest."
- Edward: "There are good people here. People who've spent the last year trying to find some sense of normalcy after seeing what the river took."
- Edward: "Your grandmother was among those it took...She cared deeply for this place."
- Camera pans up to the manor, then back down toward Edward and the player.
- Edward: "The manor is yours, as is a place for you here in Blackmere. What happens next is your decision."
- Player: "Where do we start?" / "I'm not sure if I'm ready for this..."
- Edward: "With the people who live here." / "Nor should you be. You've only just arrived."
- Edward: "For now, meet the people who live here. Once you've gotten your bearings, there's plenty that needs doing around the manor. I've managed to recover some usable equipment that should help."
- Player receives Axe, Hammer, Herb Knife, Pickaxe, and Scythe.
- Edward: "They're hardly heirlooms, but they'll serve you well enough. You'll find no shortage of work for them around Blackmere."
- Edward: "I'll be inside once you've acquainted yourself with some of the residents. Your room is ready whenever you decide to turn in."
- Edward: "Welcome to Blackmere, [Player]."
- Edward walks toward the manor and inside.
- Camera returns to the player's main view.
- HUD fades in and game starts at 10:00am on Day 1 of Spring, Year 1.

## Early Game (Years 1 and 2)

- Player meets all NPCs, starting to build up their Friendship levels.
- Player explores all of Blackmere and surrounding landscape.
- Player learns how to make tonics with Julian.
- Player learns how to cook with Helen and Theo.
- Player acquires a Fishing Rod and learns how to fish from Nora.
- Player starts to restore various rooms in the manor and various buildings in Blackmere.
- Player learns how to use Time Manipulation.

---

## Mid-Game (Years 2 and 3)

- Player continues to grow Friendship levels and starts to grow Connection levels with NPCs.
- Player continues to restore rooms in the manor and buildings in Blackmere.
- Player joins in on all festivals, including the Grand Showcase.
- Player has crafted about half of the Blueprint Book inventions.
- NPCs have grown in their arcs due to completed quests and Connection Bonds.
- Rupert (regional politician) has visited and seen the town being restored and thriving at the start of Year 3.

---

## End Game (Years 4 and Beyond)

- Player has gotten married and started a family with an NPC.
- NPCs have their Friendship and Connection levels at or near maximum.
- Player has fully or close to fully restored all rooms in the manor and all buildings in Blackmere.
- Player has crafted all or most inventions from their Blueprint Book.
- Player has completed majority of achievements in Achievement Ledger.
- Rupert has come back at the start of Year 4 for a follow-up if the player did not pass the first assessment.
