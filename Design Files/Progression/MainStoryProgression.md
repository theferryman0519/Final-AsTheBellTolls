## Pre-Game (Before Day 1 of Spring, Year 1)

- Player arrives to town via carriage.
- Player rides through Blackmere Town Square north to Pendrelle Manor.
- Player exits the carriage to the Avatar Creation Menu.
- After creating avatar, player arrives at Pendrelle Manor near Edward.
- Edward tells player about the inheritance, the flood, and restoring both Blackmere and the manor.
- Edward tells player about the warning from the regional government about taking the land after 2 years.
- Edward gives the player starting Tools: Axe, Hammer, Herb Knife, Pickaxe, and Scythe.

---

## Early Game (Years 1 and 2)

- Player meets all NPCs, starting to build up their Friendship levels.
- Player explores all of Blackmere and surrounding landscape.
- Player learns how to make tonics with Julian.
- Player learns how to cook with Helen and Theo.
- Player acquires a Fishing Rod and learns how to fish from Nora.
- Player starts to restore various rooms in the manor and various buildings in Blackmere.
- Player learns how to use Time Manipulation.

---

## Mid-Game (Years 2 and 3)

- Player continues to grow Friendship levels and starts to grow Connection levels with NPCs.
- Player continues to restore rooms in the manor and buildings in Blackmere.
- Player joins in on all festivals, including the Grand Showcase.
- Player has crafted about half of the Blueprint Book inventions.
- NPCs have grown in their arcs due to completed quests and Connection Bonds.
- Rupert (regional politician) has visited and seen the town being restored and thriving.

---

## End Game (Years 4 and Beyond)

- Player has gotten married and started a family with an NPC.
- NPCs have their Friendship and Connection levels at or near maximum.
- Player has fully or close to fully restored all rooms in the manor and all buildings in Blackmere.
- Player has crafted all or most inventions from their Blueprint Book.
- Player has completed majority of achievements in Achievement Ledger.
