## Anya Thorne

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Forgotten Things

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player notices Anya carefully organizing a small collection of pressed flowers inside her room.

**<u>Event:</u>** Anya admits she’s afraid that if she stops looking at her keepsakes, she’ll forget the people connected to them. She quietly wonders whether memories disappear the same way flowers eventually fade. For the first time, the player understands that Anya isn’t collecting flowers; she’s trying to hold on to the people she loves.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** The Memory Box

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Anya reveals that her small wooden memory box disappeared during the Great Flood. Inside were pressed flowers, sketches, tiny notes, and little keepsakes connected to moments she shared with Ian. She has never told anyone how much she misses it because she worries they’ll think they’re “just old things.”

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Memorial Hill to recover Anya’s Memory Box, then return it to Anya.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 2,500 Bellnotes

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Carrying Tomorrow

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering the Memory Box, Anya asks the player to meet her beneath the oldest tree in Memorial Hill.

**<u>Event:</u>** Sitting together, Anya slowly places each keepsake back into the box before adding one more item: a freshly picked forget-me-not from that morning. She looks at the player and quietly says, “I thought if I made new memories...the old ones would disappear.” She pauses, smiling for the first time without sadness. “But maybe...they’re happier when they have new friends.” Closing the box, Anya tucks it safely into her satchel instead of hiding it away. For the first time, she chooses to keep making memories instead of only preserving old ones, realizing that remembering and living have always belonged together.

**<u>Reward:</u>** Gifts given to NPCs now add 5 more Friendship Points.
