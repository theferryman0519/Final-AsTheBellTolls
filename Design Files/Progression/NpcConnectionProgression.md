## Adrian Lockwood

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Unwritten Conclusions

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Adrian staring at several pages of historical notes.

**<u>Event:</u>** He admits he has spent years researching Blackmere, yet hesitates every time he reaches a conclusion. "What if I'm wrong? What if someone relies on something I've written?" For the first time, the player sees how deeply Adrian fears making irreversible mistakes.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** The Missing Chapter

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Adrian reluctantly confesses that something precious disappeared after the flood. Not a rare book. Not an artifact. His own manuscript. Years of research documenting Blackmere’s history vanished while he was rescuing library materials. He admits he’s almost relieved no one can read it because he never believed it was good enough anyway.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover 5 scattered manuscript pages. Return all pages to Adrian.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Manuscript Archive decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** The Final Draft

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Weeks after recovering the manuscript, Adrian asks the player to visit the library after closing.

**<u>Event:</u>** He has finally finished revising his history of Blackmere. But this time, he stops revising. Instead of asking whether it’s perfect, he places the finished manuscript on the library display shelf. He thanks the player for teaching him something books never could: "Knowledge matters...but eventually, someone has to believe they know enough." He no longer seeks certainty. He trusts his own judgment. His character arc reaches its conclusion as he begins sharing his research publicly and announces plans to publish *The History of Blackmere*.

**<u>Reward:</u>** History of Blackmere Library Room unlocks

## Clara Weiss

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** One More Revision

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Clara alone in Weiss Design Studio long after everyone else has gone home.

**<u>Event:</u>** Surrounded by discarded blueprints, Clara admits she has spent days making tiny revisions to a project that was already ready to begin. She quietly wonders whether every overlooked detail could become the flaw people remember forever. For the first time, the player sees how exhausting her pursuit of perfection has become.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** The Locked Portfolio

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Clara reluctantly admits that something deeply personal disappeared while moving supplies after the flood—a locked portfolio containing years of unfinished architectural designs. Although she’d never shown them to anyone, she can’t bear the thought of losing them forever.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover 5 scattered portfolio sketches, then return them to Clara.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Draft Portfolio decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Ready to Build

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering her portfolio, Clara invites the player back to Weiss Design Studio to show them something.

**<u>Event:</u>** Instead of revising another blueprint, Clara signs her name across a finished design and carefully rolls it closed. She smiles and says, “There will always be something I could improve. But if I keep waiting for perfection...nothing will ever stand.” She decides to archive her unfinished portfolio—not because she’s giving up on improvement, but because she’s finally learned that completed work changes lives. For the first time, Clara is excited to begin building instead of endlessly revising.

**<u>Reward:</u>** All future Blackmere building restorations will only take 1 day to restore.

## Dante Menici

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Standing in the Shadows

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Dante quietly studying a display case long after the museum has closed.

**<u>Event:</u>** Dante admits he often feels more comfortable celebrating the achievements of others than pursuing his own. He confesses that whenever inspiration strikes, he convinces himself it could never compare to the people whose work fills the museum. The player begins to understand how deeply he fears being remembered only as someone who preserved greatness, never created it.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** An Unopened Exhibition

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Dante reluctantly reveals that a portfolio containing plans for a permanent exhibition about Blackmere’s recovery disappeared during the chaos following the Great Flood. He never searched very hard for it, believing perhaps it wasn’t worthy of being displayed anyway.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover 5 scattered exhibition plans, then return them to Dante.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Museum Exhibition Poster decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** The First Exhibit

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering his exhibition plans, Dante asks the player to visit the Hall of Wonder before opening hours.

**<u>Event:</u>** Instead of filing his plans away once again, Dante unveils Blackmere’s newest permanent exhibit: “The Great Flood and the Rebirth of Blackmere.” Standing beside it, he admits that history isn’t finished simply because it’s been preserved. It continues through every person willing to contribute something meaningful. Looking at the exhibit filled with artifacts from the town’s recovery, he smiles and says, “Perhaps the greatest privilege isn’t preserving history… but helping write its next chapter.” For the first time, Dante sees himself not only as a curator of the past, but as someone helping shape Blackmere’s future.

**<u>Reward:</u>** Hall of Wonder Museum Donations now provide double Bellnotes when new artifacts are donated.

## Leo Finch

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Behind the Smile

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Leo quietly putting away several clothing designs after the shop has closed.

He confesses that sometimes he wonders if people dislike his clothes because they secretly dislike the person who made them. The player begins to see the vulnerable heart hidden beneath Leo’s cheerful personality.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Leo reluctantly reveals that a trunk containing his most personal clothing designs disappeared while reorganizing Thread & Thimble after the Great Flood. They were never intended for customers—they were designs created purely for himself. Losing them feels like losing a part of who he is.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover 5 scattered clothing sketches, then return them to Leo.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Designer’s Sketchbook decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Wear It Proudly

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering his sketchbook, Leo invites the player to Thread & Thimble for the unveiling of his newest clothing collection.

**<u>Event:</u>** Instead of displaying safe, fashionable designs, Leo proudly presents the colorful, expressive outfits he once hid away. Looking at the collection, he smiles and says, “Not everyone will love them...and that’s alright. They’re finally honest.” For the first time, Leo creates without worrying whether everyone will approve. He has learned that authenticity is worth far more than universal acceptance.

**<u>Reward:</u>** All clothing purchased from Thread & Thimble is permanently 25% less Bellnotes.

## Madison Remington

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** The Weight of the Badge

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player notices Madison lingering alone in her office after Town Hall has closed.

**<u>Event:</u>** Madison quietly admits that every difficult decision reminds her of Sheriff Ian Thorne. She worries that one mistake could prove Blackmere chose the wrong person to replace him. For the first time, she confesses that beneath her confidence is a constant fear of letting the town down.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** Ian’s Legacy

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Madison reveals that Ian Thorne’s original sheriff’s badge disappeared while Blackmere was being repaired after the Great Flood. Though she now wears her own badge, losing Ian’s feels like losing the promise she made to honor his legacy.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover Ian Thorne’s Sheriff Badge, then return it to Madison.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Sheriff Badge decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** A Sheriff of Her Own

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering Ian’s badge, Madison asks the player to meet her outside Town Hall before sunrise.

**<u>Event:</u>** Holding Ian’s badge one final time, Madison thanks him for inspiring her before carefully placing it inside a display case rather than back in her desk drawer. She smiles and says, "I spent so long trying to become Ian Thorne...I forgot Blackmere asked for Madison Remington.” She finally accepts that honoring Ian doesn’t mean becoming him. By trusting others and embracing her own strengths, she has become the sheriff Blackmere truly needs.

**<u>Reward:</u>** Using the Emergency Supply Stations permanently restore the player's Stamina to maximum with each use.

## Mara Klein

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Carrying the Weight

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Mara working alone late into the evening despite several unfinished projects surrounding her.

**<u>Event:</u>** Mara admits she rarely asks anyone for help because it’s easier to trust her own hands than risk someone else making a mistake. She quietly wonders if depending on others is simply asking to be disappointed. The player begins to understand that her independence has become a wall between herself and the people who care about her.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** The First Carving

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Mara reveals that the first wooden sign she ever carved as a child has gone missing after being misplaced during renovations at Klein Woodworks. Although crude and full of mistakes, it’s the one project she’s never been able to throw away because it reminds her where she started.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover Mara’s First Wooden Sign, then return it to Mara.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / First Wooden Sign decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Built to Last

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering the wooden sign, Mara asks the player to stop by Klein Woodworks before opening for the day.

**<u>Event:</u>** Holding the crooked little sign in her hands, Mara laughs at how imperfect it is. Instead of hiding it away again, she hangs it proudly on the workshop wall where everyone can see it. She smiles and says, “Turns out...the strongest things I’ve ever built weren’t made by one person.” She admits that Blackmere’s restoration succeeded because everyone contributed—not because she tried to carry the work alone. For the first time, she fully embraces that trusting others doesn’t weaken her craftsmanship; it strengthens everything she builds.

**<u>Reward:</u>** All future Wood gathered from trees permanently yields 50% more.

## Marcus Rowan

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** The Last One to Rest

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Marcus alone in the forge well after midnight, finishing work no one asked him to complete that day.

**<u>Event:</u>** Marcus admits he always puts his own needs aside because someone else is waiting on a repaired tool, a replacement part, or a new piece of equipment. He quietly wonders if taking time for himself would make him selfish. The player begins to understand that Marcus measures his worth almost entirely by what he provides for others.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** An Unchosen Path

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Marcus reluctantly reveals that an old letter from a renowned master blacksmith disappeared after the Great Flood. It contained an invitation to apprentice outside Morvanya; an opportunity he quietly turned down because he couldn’t bear leaving Blackmere when the town needed him most.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover Marcus’s Apprenticeship Letter, then return it to Marcus.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Master Blacksmith’s Letter decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** More Than the Forge

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering the letter, Marcus asks the player to meet him outside Ironveil Forge after closing.

**<u>Event:</u>** Holding the letter in his hands, Marcus admits he doesn’t regret staying in Blackmere—but he does regret believing that his own dreams stopped mattering the day everyone else needed him. He carefully places the letter into a keepsake frame instead of hiding it away and says, “Helping people has always been my purpose, but maybe I’m allowed to have a life worth building too.” For the first time, Marcus begins making time for himself, understanding that his happiness isn’t a distraction from his responsibilities; it’s part of becoming the person Blackmere has always believed he is.

**<u>Reward:</u>** Pickaxe permanently yields 50% more Ore, Gemstones, Clay, Coal, and Stone gathered.

## Nora Reed

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Between the Waves

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player notices Nora unusually quiet after finishing work for the day.

**<u>Event:</u>** Away from the crowds, Nora admits she sometimes worries that if she ever stops smiling, everyone will realize she’s still carrying pain from the Great Flood. She confesses that making other people laugh has become easier than talking about the things she’d rather forget. For the first time, the player sees that her optimism has also become a shield.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** The Quiet Shore

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Nora finally tells the player about a stretch of the Graythorne River she hasn’t visited since the Great Flood. While helping others during the disaster, she lost a small keepsake there that she has never been able to bring herself to search for again.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Travel to the forgotten riverbank, recover Nora’s Keepsake Lure, then return it to Nora.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Weathered Fishing Lure decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Weathering the Storm

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering the keepsake lure, Nora asks the player to meet her at the quiet stretch of river she has avoided for so long.

**<u>Event:</u>** Standing at the water’s edge, Nora finally allows herself to speak openly about the flood and everything she has spent years trying to outrun. She admits that pretending she was always alright never actually helped her heal. Holding the old lure in her hand, she smiles through tears and says, “Guess storms don’t end because you ignore them...they end because you keep sailing anyway.” She leaves the riverbank not because the memories are gone, but because she no longer has to face them alone. Her laughter remains every bit as genuine as before; only now it comes from someone who has learned that vulnerability and hope can exist together.

**<u>Reward:</u>** Fish permanently sell for 50% more Bellnotes.

## Sabrina Fairchild

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Carrying Every Burden

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Sabrina alone in her classroom long after the students have gone home.

**<u>Event:</u>** Sabrina admits that whenever a student struggles, she immediately wonders what she could have done differently. She quietly confesses that every setback feels like a personal failure, even when it’s beyond her control. The player begins to understand that her greatest kindness is also the source of her deepest self-doubt.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** Little Reminders

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Sabrina reveals that a small keepsake box containing years of handwritten notes, drawings, and thank-you letters from her students was misplaced during repairs to the school after the Great Flood. Whenever she doubts herself, those letters remind her why she became a teacher.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover 5 Student Letters, then return them to Sabrina.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Keepsake Letter Box decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** One Life Is Enough

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering the keepsake box, Sabrina invites the player back to her classroom after school has ended.

**<u>Event:</u>** As she rereads the letters, Sabrina realizes that none of them praise her for being a perfect teacher. Instead, they thank her for listening, encouraging, believing, and simply being there when someone needed her. Smiling through tears, she closes the box and says, “Maybe changing the whole world was never my job...maybe changing one person’s world is enough.” For the first time, Sabrina lets go of the impossible expectation that she must save everyone. She embraces the idea that genuine compassion is measured one life at a time.

**<u>Reward:</u>** All crafted Fabricated items have a 25% chance to produce an additional copy.

## Victor Cross

### 2 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** One More Road

**<u>Backstory:</u>** The player finds Victor sitting beside his caravan after closing, quietly watching the road leading out of Blackmere.

**<u>Event:</u>** Victor admits he’s never stayed anywhere long enough to truly unpack his life. He worries that choosing one place means giving up countless opportunities waiting beyond the next horizon. Beneath his cheerful confidence, the player begins to see someone who mistakes keeping every option open for genuine freedom.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 4 Key Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** Ready to Leave

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Victor reluctantly reveals that his old travel bag disappeared while unloading supplies after the Great Flood. Though it contains little monetary value, it’s always remained packed beneath his bed; a quiet reminder that he could leave whenever he wanted.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Search several locations around Blackmere to recover Victor’s Travel Bag, then return it to Victor.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 1,500 Bellnotes / Traveler’s Satchel decor

### 6 Key Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Unpacking

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After recovering the travel bag, Victor asks the player to meet him at his home after the market has closed.

**<u>Event:</u>** Victor sets the worn travel bag on the floor and slowly begins unpacking it for the first time in years. One by one, he places maps, keepsakes, and souvenirs onto shelves around his home before finally tucking the now-empty bag into a closet. He smiles and says, “Guess I always thought leaving meant I was still free..but maybe choosing where you belong is the bravest journey of all.” For the first time, Victor allows himself to stop living as though tomorrow will always be another departure. Blackmere is no longer just another stop along the road; it’s home.

**<u>Reward:</u>** All items sold at the Caravan Stall permanently sell for 25% less Bellnotes.
