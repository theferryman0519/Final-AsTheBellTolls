## Adrian Lockwood

### 3 Heart Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** A Chapter Begins

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Adrian has always wanted to make the library feel welcoming instead of intimidating. Recently, several damaged books recovered after the flood have arrived, but he hasn’t had time to properly restore and organize them.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Collect and gather 5 books around the Library, then return to Adrian.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 500 Bellnotes

### 6 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** The Stories Between the Shelves

**<u>Backstory:</u>** While closing the library for the evening, Adrian notices an old family photograph tucked inside a weathered journal. Seeing it reminds him how many memories nearly disappeared after the flood. He quietly shares stories about growing up in Blackmere, spending afternoons in the library, and how its shelves became a second home during difficult times.

**<u>Event:</u>** The player simply listens while Adrian reflects on his childhood, his parents encouraging his love of books, and how the flood strengthened his desire to preserve history.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 9 Heart Invention Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** A Library Worth Sharing

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Adrian dreams of making Blackmere’s history available to everyone, but the library has no practical way to safely display fragile historical documents. He asks if the player could invent something that would protect them while still allowing people to appreciate them.

**<u>Craft Invention:</u>** Preservation Display Case

**<u>Reward:</u>** 2,000 Bellnotes / Preservation Display Case permanently appears inside the library

### 12 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Every Story Belongs Here

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Adrian invites the player to walk along the Graythorne River where he often reads.

**<u>Event:</u>** He explains that Blackmere isn’t important because of its buildings. It’s important because of its people. He points out locations where residents made memories long before the flood and admits that preserving those stories is what truly motivates him. The player realizes Adrian doesn’t simply love books. He loves what books preserve.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 15 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Our Next Chapter

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Adrian quietly leaves a handwritten journal beside the player’s bed.

**<u>Event:</u>** Inside is a chronicle of the player’s life in Blackmere. The first page simply reads: "Every book remembers someone. I wanted ours to remember us." He reveals that for the first time in his life, he wrote without worrying whether every detail was perfect. Because some stories are worth telling simply because they happened.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

## Clara Weiss

### 3 Heart Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** Building Foundations

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Clara is preparing plans for Blackmere’s continued restoration, but she refuses to finalize them until she can test a few construction ideas herself. She asks the player to help fabricate several prototype building components.

**<u>Objective:</u>** Craft 5 Fabrications using the Workbench, then return to Clara.

**<u>Reward:</u>** 500 Bellnotes / Keep crafted Fabrications / Workbench at Weiss Design Studio is permanently available for the player to use

### 6 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Blueprints of Yesterday

**<u>Backstory:</u>** While organizing old architectural drawings inside Weiss Design Studio, Clara uncovers several sketches her mentor encouraged her to keep from her earliest years studying architecture.

**<u>Event:</u>** Clara shares how she became fascinated with architecture as a child, explaining that every building tells a story about the people who built it. She admits that she still compares every design she creates to the impossible standards she imagined for herself years ago.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 9 Heart Invention Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** Built to Last

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Clara dreams of helping rebuild Blackmere more efficiently, but repeatedly redesigning every project slows progress. She asks the player to create a machine that allows her to quickly test architectural concepts before committing them to full construction.

**<u>Craft Invention:</u>** Architectural Drafting Table

**<u>Reward:</u>** 2,000 Bellnotes / Architectural Drafting Table permanently appears inside Weiss Design Studio

### 12 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** A Town Worth Building

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Clara invites the player to walk through several of Blackmere’s restored districts during sunset.

**<u>Event:</u>** She explains that architecture isn’t about creating impressive buildings; it’s about creating places where people laugh, celebrate, and build memories together. She admits that every restored home and business reminds her why rebuilding Blackmere has always mattered more than building her reputation.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 15 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Home Is Never Perfect

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After marrying the player, Clara quietly asks them to meet her outside Pendrelle Manor one evening.

**<u>Event:</u>** She reveals she has spent weeks sketching improvements for the Manor before finally putting the drawings away. Smiling, she admits it’s the first home she’s ever chosen not to redesign because she already loves it exactly as it is; not because of the building itself, but because of the life the two of you have built together.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

## Dante Menici

### 3 Heart Quest

**<u>Backstory:</u>** While cataloging artifacts after closing time, Dante discovers an old exhibition journal written by the museum’s original curator.

**<u>Event:</u>** Dante shares how reading the journal inspired him to become a curator. He explains that every artifact tells the story of an ordinary person, yet he has never believed his own life could become part of history. For the first time, he wonders if preserving the past is enough.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 9 Heart Invention Quest

**<u>Title:</u>** History in the Making

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Dante dreams of creating an exhibit dedicated to Blackmere’s recovery after the Great Flood, proving that history is still being written every day. He asks the player to build something worthy of displaying the town’s continuing story.

**<u>Craft Invention:</u>** Interactive Exhibition Display

**<u>Reward:</u>** 2,000 Bellnotes / Interactive Exhibition Display permanently appears inside the Hall of Wonder

### 12 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** Tomorrow’s Museum

**<u>Backstory:</u>** Dante invites the player to walk through the Hall of Wonder after everyone has gone home.

**<u>Event:</u>** He stops beside the museum’s newest exhibit and quietly remarks that every artifact inside was once simply someone’s everyday possession. Smiling, he realizes that Blackmere’s greatest exhibit may not be its distant past, but the people rebuilding it today. He thanks the player for reminding him that history is something everyone has the chance to create.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None

### 15 Heart Event

**<u>Title:</u>** A Life Worth Remembering

**<u>Backstory:</u>** After marrying the player, Dante invites them to the Hall of Wonder before it opens for the day.

**<u>Event:</u>** Inside a newly completed gallery, the player discovers a small display dedicated not to ancient relics, but to the life the two of them have built together in Blackmere. Dante explains that history is not reserved for kings, inventors, or heroes; it belongs to anyone whose life leaves the world kinder than they found it. He says their greatest contribution isn’t on display in the museum; it’s the future they’re building together.

**<u>Reward:</u>** None
