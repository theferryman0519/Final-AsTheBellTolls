## Every Current & Catch

Backstory: Nora has spent years learning the rhythms of the Graythorne River and the waters surrounding Blackmere. She challenges the player to discover every fish that can be caught, from familiar river staples to the rarest catches hidden by season, weather, and time of day.

ID: quest_every-current-and-catch

Objective: Catch every Fish at least once

Owner: Nora Reed

Prerequisite: Cast Into the Current Quest Complete

Reward: Keeper of the Current Statue

Title: Every Current & Catch

Type: Achievement Quest

## A Table for All Seasons

Backstory: Helen and Theo believe that Blackmere's recipes preserve the character of the town just as surely as its buildings and traditions. Together, they challenge the player to prepare every Meal and Drink, creating a complete collection of dishes from every season.

ID: quest_a-table-for-all-seasons

Objective: Cook every Meal and Drink at least once

Owner: Helen Holt and Theo Bennett

Prerequisite: Never Go Hungry Quest Complete

Reward: Eternal Hearth Statue

Title: A Table for All Seasons

Type: Achievement Quest

## Pendrelle Restored

Backstory: Edward has watched Pendrelle Manor endure loss, flood damage, and years of decline. He asks the player to restore every room until the entire Manor has reached its Flourishing state and once again feels like a true home.

ID: quest_pendrelle-restored

Objective: Fully restore every room in Pendrelle Manor to Flourishing stage

Owner: Edward Ashcroft

Prerequisite: Home Is Where the Heart Is Quest Complete

Reward: Heart of Pendrelle Statue

Title: Pendrelle Restored

Type: Achievement Quest

## A Library Made Whole

Backstory: Adrian wants the Public Library to become more than a repaired building. He dreams of shelves filled with books, comfortable places to read, and carefully chosen furnishings that make every visitor feel welcome. He asks the player to complete every furnishing project within the Library.

ID: quest_a-library-made-whole

Objective: Collect all Library Books

Owner: Adrian Lockwood

Prerequisite: Have at least 3 Hearts with Adrian Lockwood

Reward: Endless Shelf Statue

Title: A Library Made Whole

Type: Achievement Quest
