## A Friendly Face

Backstory: Edward suggests that the best way to settle into Blackmere is by getting to know its people. Every friendship begins with a simple conversation.

ID: quest_a-friendly-face

Objective: Speak to 10 residents of Blackmere

Owner: Edward Ashcroft

Prerequisite: None

Reward: 200 Bellnotes

Title: A Friendly Face

Type: Tutorial Quest

---

## Thoughtful Gifts

Backstory: Edward explains that a well-chosen gift can brighten someone's day and strengthen friendships. He encourages the player to offer a gift to one of Blackmere's residents.

ID: quest_thoughtful-gifts

Objective: Give a gift to any resident

Owner: Edward Ashcroft

Prerequisite: A Friendly Face Quest Complete

Reward: 400 Bellnotes

Title: Thoughtful Gifts

Type: Tutorial Quest

Owner: Nora Reed

Prerequisite: None

Reward: Fishing Rod

Title: Cast Into the Current

## Never Go Hungry

Backstory: Helen explains that good food brings people together. They invite the player to prepare their first meal using the Manor Kitchen.

ID: quest_never-go-hungry

Objective: Cook a Meal

Owner: Helen Holt

Prerequisite: Kitchen in Pendrelle Manor at Rebuilding stage 

Reward: Baked Potato, Creamy Mushroom Soup, Scrambled Eggs, Trout & Veggies

Title: Never Go Hungry

Type: Tutorial Quest

## Home Is Where the Heart Is

Backstory: Edward has cared for Pendrelle Manor through years of decline. He asks the player to complete their first restoration project and begin bringing the estate back to life.

ID: quest_home-is-where-the-heart-is

Objective: Restore the Main Bedroom to Rebuilding stage

Owner: Edward Ashcroft

Prerequisite: Thoughtful Gifts Quest Complete

Reward: 750 Bellnotes

Title: Home Is Where the Heart Is

Type: Tutorial Quest
