## Information

- During testing and protyping, the Admin Panel can be viewed to manually control and manipulate different pieces of data.
- These data pieces include Friendship and Connection Bond levels with different NPCs, amount of Bellnotes, current time and date, current weather, and the possession of different items and inventions.
- As an admin during testing, this feature is used for quickly testing specific events, actions, and interactions within the game.
