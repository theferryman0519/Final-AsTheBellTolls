## Information

- There are six different types of audio for the game: Music, Ambiance, Sound Effects, Weather, Footsteps, and Speech.
- Music are the songs that are played in the background during the game.
- Ambiance are the sound effects that play consistently, such as crickets, the forge bellows, and birds.
- Sound Effects are sound effects that are triggered, such as cutting a tree, using the hammer, and opening a door.
- Weather are sound effects caused by the weather, such as the wind, thunder, and rain.
- Footsteps are sound effects made by the player when they are moving.
- Speech are sound effects made by each of the characters when they are speaking to the player.
