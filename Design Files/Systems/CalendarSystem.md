## Base Information

- 60 Minutes in 1 Hour
- 18 Hours in 1 Day (6am to 12am)
- 7 Days in 1 Week
- 31 Days in 1 Season
- 4 Seasons in 1 Year (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter)

Day 1 of Spring, Year 1 starts on a Monday.
