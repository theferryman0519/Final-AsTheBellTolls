## Information

- During gameplay, the camera follows the player in a third-person view, sitting above and behind the player, angled down in a 2.5D fashion.
- During the first portion of the Intro cinematic, the camera is in a first-person view at the vantage point of the player within the carriage.
- During dialogue interactions, the camera pans to center on the NPC the player is currently speaking with, allowing the dialogue panel to not cover the NPC.
- During cinematic scenes (aside from the Intro), the camera free-flows in a third-person view throughout the interaction and event.
- Within the Settings navigated from the Player Menu, the player will be able to adjust the zoom of the camera between Near, Middle, and Far. The default setting is Middle.
