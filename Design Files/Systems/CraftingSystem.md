## Information

**<u>Activating Mechanics:</u>** While the Manor's Laboratory is in its Weathered restoration stage, the Workbench is not available. It only becomes available once the Laboratory has been restored to its Rebuilding stage. Once available, the player will need to walk up to the Workbench and interact with it. While at Weiss Design Studio, the player will be able to have access to the Workbench with Clara once reaching at least 3 Hearts with her.

**<u>Mechanic:</u>** After interacting with the Workbench, the Crafting Menu will pop up. Here, the player will be able to choose from an available crafting recipe. Once that recipe has been selected, the Crafting Menu will show the player's stored crafting ingredient items (from their Inventory and any Storage Units). The player will be able to choose the appropriate ingredients available for the selected recipe to add to the Workbench. Once all ingredients have been added, the Fabricated item will be added to the player's Inventory. If the player does not have room in their Inventory, the item will be mailed to the player the next morning.

**<u>Other Information:</u>** Crafting does not take any amount of in-game time. Time pauses when the Crafting Menu is open and does not restart until after the craft has completed. Recipes are only shown as available if they are both discovered and if the player has all necessary ingredient items. The player cannot cancel once confirming all ingredients added to the Workbench.

**<u>Note:</u>** The Workbench is also used for crafting Inventions. The mechanics are the same between crafting Fabricated items and crafting Inventions, it just depends on which recipe the player selects.

## Example Flows

### Laboratory in Weathered Stage

- Player enters Laboratory in Pendrelle Manor.
- Laboratory is at Weathered restoration stage.
- Workbench is not available to be used.

### Less than 3 Hearts with Clara Weiss

- Player enters Weiss Design Studio.
- Player interacts with Clara Weiss.
- The option to Craft With Clara is not available.

### Crafting Small Storage Unit in Manor

- Player enters Laboratory in Pendrelle Manor.
- Laboratory is at or above Rebuilding restoration stage.
- Player walks to end adjacent to Workbench.
- Player interacts with Workbench.
- Crafting Menu opens with available recipes sorted to top of scroll view.
- Player selects Small Storage Unit recipe.
- Crafting Menu opens with available crafting ingredient items.
- Player selects Wood Beam and toggles amount to 4, then selects Hinges and toggles to 2, then selects Nuts & Bolts and toggles to 4.
- Small Storage Unit is successfully created and added to the player's Inventory or mailed the next morning (if Inventory is full).

### Crafting Small Storage Unit with Clara Weiss

- Player enters Winding Banks Inn and navigates to Clara Weiss.
- Player speaks to Clara and is at or above 3 Hearts with her.
- After speaking, player selects Craft With Clara option.
- Crafting Menu opens with available recipes sorted to top of scroll view.
- Player selects Small Storage Unit recipe.
- Crafting Menu opens with available crafting ingredient items.
- Player selects Wood Beam and toggles amount to 4, then selects Hinges and toggles to 2, then selects Nuts & Bolts and toggles to 4.
- Small Storage Unit is successfully created and added to the player's Inventory or mailed the next morning (if Inventory is full).
- Player gains Friendship Points with Clara.
