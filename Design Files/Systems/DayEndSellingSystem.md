## Information

- One of the pages of the Day End System is the End of Day Selling.
- This highlights all the items sold from the Consumer Bin before the day ended.
- The page breaks down the items into five different categories, totaling the Bellnotes for each category before showing the total: Consumables, Non-Consumables, Meals & Drinks, Fabrications, and Tonics.
- The player will be able to select each of the categories to see the exact amount of Bellnotes earned for each individual item.
