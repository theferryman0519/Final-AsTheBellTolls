## Information

- The player must go to their bed in the Main Bedroom to sleep for the night.
- If the player does not go to sleep in their bed by 12:00am (midnight), the player will faint from exhaustion and the day will automatically end.
- Once the day ends, the player will be able to view four pieces of data related to the day that just ended and the next day: Updated Relationships, Timing Progress, End of Day Selling, and Tomorrow's Events.

---

## End of Day Data

- Updated Relationships: This will show the progression with various NPC Friendship and Connection Bonds. The page will show the day's progression for only the NPCs who had their Bonds changed (both positively and negatively).
- Timing Progress: This will show the various updated timings of objects and actions, including crop and flower growth, building and room restoration, and invention crafting and testing timings.
- End of Day Selling: This will show the amount of Bellnotes gained from items that were placed in the Consumer Bin before the day ended.
- Tomorrow's Events: This will show the date of the next day, the weather for the next day, and any events for the next day (birthdays, festivals, mini events, or ongoing events).
