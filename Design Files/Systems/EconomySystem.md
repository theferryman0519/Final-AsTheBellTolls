## Information

- The currency for the game is called Bellnotes.
- When starting the game, the player starts with 500 Bellnotes.
- When shopping, the player spends Bellnotes, but can only spend the amount of Bellnotes their currently have.
- The only time a player can have a negative amount is when paying back loans and the player does not have enough money.
- When a player has negative Bellnotes, they are unable to purchase anything until they return to a positive amount.
