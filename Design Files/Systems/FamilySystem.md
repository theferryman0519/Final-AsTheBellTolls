## Information

- When a player is married to an NPC, that NPC will be able to earn a maximum of 15 Friendship Hearts instead of the normal 12.
- Once that NPC has completed their 15 Heart Event, the player will receive an event during the next End of Day about possibly starting a family.
- The player will be able to choose whether to start a family or not. If they choose to not start a family, the player will be asked again in one year (at the End of Day on the same date).
- If the player chooses to start a family, then the Nursery will unlock in the manor.
- Once the Nursery is unlocked, the player will need to restore it to the Recovering stage.
- The next morning after restoring the Nursery to the Recovering stage, the player will see a dialogue welcoming their newborn. There is a 1% chance for twins. Each child born is randomized between girl or boy.
- Once the child is born, the player will be able to customize their name.
- After a year, the child moves from being a baby to a toddler (able to walk).
- Once able to walk, the child will be able to help out around the manor (giving the player a single ingredient item each morning).
