## Information

- Farming takes place in both the Garden and the Orchard in Pendrelle Manor.
- Farming also takes place on the Mercer Farms Grounds.
- Farming is unlocked in the Garden after reaching Rebuilding restoration stage.
- Farming is unlocked in the Orchard after reaching Rebuilding restoration stage.
- Once the Garden reaches Prospering restoration stage, crops or flowers can be planted from any season.
- Once the Orchard reaches Prospering restoration stage, crops can be planted from any season.
- Once a soil square has been harvested, it needs 3 days to refuel due to damage from the flood.
- Once a soil square has been refueled, it can be tilled and used for planting.
- Crops or flowers that are harvested are all at a count of 1, unless increased through quest rewards (only at the Manor).

## Process Flow

### Farming at Mercer Farms

- Player arrives at Mercer Farms.
- There is always 1 3x3 plot (or 9 plots total) on Mercer Farms Grounds.
- Crops or flowers will always be ready for harvest in the morning.
- Each crop or flower will be random (available for the current season), but all 9 plots are the same.
- Player walks up to a plot.
- The Interact panel appears to interact with the plot, harvesting it.
- Harvested crops will go into the player's Inventory, or into the Pantry (if there is no room in the Inventory).
- Harvested flowers will go into the player's Inventory, or be mailed to the player the next morning (if there is no room in the Inventory).
- All harvested crops or flowers are at Base quality.

### Farming at Pendrelle Manor in Garden

- Player enters the Garden after reaching Rebuilding restoration stage.
- Player changes current Tool to Hoe.
- Player walks up to a soil plot and interacts with soil to till (use the Hoe).
- Once tilled, the player interacts with soil to open the Plant Crop Menu.
- Plant Crop Menu shows all available crop or flower seeds for the current season available for the Garden.
- Player chooses an available seed and selects to Plant Seed.
- Player changes current Tool to Watering Can.
- Player interacts with planted seed to water the tilled soil.
- Each day, player can walk up to a tilled, planted soil and interact with it, showing the Seed Progress Panel.
- Seed Progress Panel shows the type of crop or flower, if it needs to be watered, and how many days left until harvest.
- Once ready to harvest, player interacts with the crop or flower, harvesting it.
- Harvested crops will go into the player's Inventory, or into the Pantry (if there is no room in the Inventory).
- Harvested flowers will go into the player's Inventory, or be mailed to the player the next morning (if there is no room in the Inventory).
- All harvested crops or flowers are at the quality set through inventions within the Blueprint Book.

### Farming at Pendrelle Manor in Orchard

- Player enters the Orchard after reaching Rebuilding restoration stage.
- Player changes current Tool to Hoe.
- Player walks up to a soil plot and interacts with soil to till (use the Hoe).
- Once tilled, the player interacts with soil to open the Plant Crop Menu.
- Plant Crop Menu shows all available crop seeds for the current season available for the Orchard.
- Player chooses an available seed and selects to Plant Seed.
- Player changes current Tool to Watering Can.
- Player interacts with planted seed to water the tilled soil.
- Each day, player can walk up to a tilled, planted soil and interact with it, showing the Seed Progress Panel.
- Seed Progress Panel shows the type of crop, if it needs to be watered, and how many days left until harvest.
- Once ready to harvest, player interacts with the crop, harvesting it.
- Harvested crops will go into the player's Inventory, or into the Pantry (if there is no room in the Inventory).
- All harvested crops are at the quality set through inventions within the Blueprint Book.

## Time Manipulation Integration

* Advance reduces crop growth duration.
* Advance reduces soil recovery duration.
* Preserve can hold mature crops/flowers.
* Synchronize can align two growing crops/flowers.
* Chimes cannot create harvest yield or quality directly.
