## Information

- Festivals and recurring community events occur on specific dates or days according to the Calendar System.
- Festival information is displayed on the Calendar and may appear within Tomorrow's Events during the Day End System.
- Festivals may temporarily change NPC routines, available interactions, dialogue, music, decorations, shops, and activities.
- The player will travel to the appropriate location to participate in a festival.
- NPCs participating in a festival will use festival-specific routines instead of their normal routines.
- Festival-specific behavior ends when the festival concludes.

---

## Event Types

| Event Type    | Information                                                                                                       |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Main Festival | Major seasonal or community festival with unique activities, dialogue, NPC placement, and other festival content. |
| Mini Festival | Smaller competitive or activity-focused festival.                                                                 |
| Ongoing Event | Recurring community event that occurs throughout the year.                                                        |

---

## Festival Activities

Festivals may contain:

- Unique NPC Dialogue
- Cinematic Game Events
- Competitions
- Minigames
- Shops
- Unique Items
- Rewards
- Decorations
- Festival Music
- Festival Activities
- Relationship interactions

---

## Festival NPCs

- NPCs participating in a festival will follow festival-specific routines.
- NPCs will generally idle within assigned areas during festivals.
- NPCs may have unique festival dialogue.
- NPC shops and normal work routines may be unavailable while the NPC is attending a festival.
- NPCs who do not participate in a festival may continue following their normal routines.
