## Information

- Throughout Blackmere, there are various instances that may occur that create a visual interest.
- These instances are not interactable, just visual points that add ambiance and intrigue to the game.
- They are broken into four categories: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Extremely Rare.
- Each occurrence has the potential to change NPC routines or could be small enough to just be a visual addition.
