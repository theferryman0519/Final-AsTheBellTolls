## Information

- HUD shows a variety of features that are necessary during the player's gameplay.
- Upper right corner: Shows the current time, current date, and current amount of Bellnotes.
- Bottom right corner: Shows the current tool icon from the player Tool Belt. If no tool is selected, it shows the open hands.
- Bottom center: Shows the interaction with nearby objects or characters.
- Bottom left corner: Shows notification popups when the player collects or acquires items.
- Upper left corner: Shows the current stamina and time manipulation bars, as well as any current buffs.
