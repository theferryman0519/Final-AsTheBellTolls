## Information

- Players will be able to interact with several objects within during gameplay.
- These interactions include NPCs, growing crops and flowers, soil, trees, rock deposits, bushes, tall grass, water, and other interactable objects.
- Some interactions will require a tool while others do not require the player to be holding any tool.
- When the player is adjacent and facing an object that can be interacted with, the Interact Panel will appear in the HUD at the bottom of the screen.
- The Interact Panel will state whether a tool is needed or that the player may press the Interact Input Key to trigger the interaction.
