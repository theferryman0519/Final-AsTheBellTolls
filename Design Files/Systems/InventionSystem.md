## Information

**<u>Activating Mechanics:</u>** While the Manor's Laboratory is in its Weathered restoration stage, the Workbench is not available. It only becomes available once the Laboratory has been restored to its Rebuilding stage. Once available, the player will need to walk up to the Workbench and interact with it. While at Weiss Design Studio, the player will be able to have access to the Workbench with Clara once reaching at least 3 Hearts with her.

**<u>Mechanic:</u>** After interacting with the Workbench, the Crafting Menu will pop up. Here, the player will be able to choose from an available crafting recipe. Once that recipe has been selected, the Crafting Menu will show the player's stored crafting ingredient items (from their Inventory and any Storage Units). The player will be able to choose the appropriate ingredients available for the selected recipe to add to the Workbench. Once all ingredients have been added, the Invention will start its Crafting & Testing Time. Once that time has completed, the Invention's ability will be available for the player.

## Example Flows

### Laboratory in Weathered Stage

- Player enters Laboratory in Pendrelle Manor.
- Laboratory is at Weathered restoration stage.
- Workbench is not available to be used.

### Less than 3 Hearts with Clara Weiss

- Player enters Weiss Design Studio.
- Player interacts with Clara Weiss.
- The option to Craft With Clara is not available.

### Crafting Seasoning Regulator in Manor

- Player enters Laboratory in Pendrelle Manor.
- Laboratory is at or above Rebuilding restoration stage.
- Player walks to end adjacent to Workbench.
- Player interacts with Workbench.
- Crafting Menu opens with available recipes sorted to top of scroll view.
- Player selects Seasoning Regulator recipe.
- Crafting Menu opens with available crafting ingredient items.
- Player selects Clay and toggles the amount to 5, selects Wood and toggles to 15, selects Stone and toggles to 10, and then selects Jasper as the required gemstone.
- The Crafting & Testing Time for the Seasoning Regulator starts its countdown.
- Once the time has reached 0:00, the Seasoning Regulator's ability becomes available for the player.
