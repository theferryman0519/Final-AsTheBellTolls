## Information

- Inventory is split between Tool Belt and Satchel.

### Tool Belt

- Tool Belt is only for the 8 tools: Axe, Fishing Rod, Hammer, Herb Knife, Hoe, Pickaxe, Scythe, Watering Can.
- Tool Belt items are automatically sorted into their specified slots.
- Tool Belt items will show a banner to denote their Quality.

### Satchel

- Satchel starts with 16 item slots.
- After crafting the Satchel Extender invention, the Satchel will hold 24 item slots.
- After crafting the Satchel Enlarger invention, the Satchel will hold 32 item slots.
- Satchel items can be sorted automatically or manually (sorting can be by Quality, name, or count).
- Satchel items will show a banner to denote their Quality.
- Satchel items can be stacked as long as the item is of a similar type and similar Quality.
- Satchel items can have a maximum stack count of 999, then will automatically start a new stack.
