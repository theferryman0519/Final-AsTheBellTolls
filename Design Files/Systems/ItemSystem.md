## Information

- Items are split between Artisanal, Ingredient, Interactable, Quest, Recipe, and Useful items.
- All items except Interactable can be placed in the player Inventory.
- Artisanal items include Clothing, Decor, and Furniture.
- Ingredient items include Byproducts, Flowers (and Seeds), Forageables, Crops (and Seeds), Gemstones, Herbs, Fish, and Ore.
- Interactable items include Man-made and Natural.
- Quest items include Recovery items.
- Recipe items include Drinks, Fabrications, Meals, and Tonics.
- Useful items include Library Books, Museum Artifacts, Record Discs, and Tools.
- All items are one-use except for Tools.

---

## Quality Types

- Items that can be different qualities are Base, Copper, Iron, Silver, Gold, or Cobalt.
- Base Quality items earn 1.0x their data points (Value Price and Replenishment Amount).
- Copper Quality items earn 1.1x their data points (Value Price and Replenishment Amount).
- Iron Quality items earn 1.25x their data points (Value Price and Replenishment Amount).
- Silver Quality items earn 1.5x their data points (Value Price and Replenishment Amount).
- Gold Quality items earn 1.9x their data points (Value Price and Replenishment Amount).
- Cobalt Quality items earn 2.5x their data points (Value Price and Replenishment Amount).
