## Information

- The library includes the Library Donation Counter, which is unlocked after completing Adrian's A Chapter Begins quest.
- Library books can be found throughout the overworld.
- There are a total of 50 library books that are scattered throughout the region.
- Once collected, books need to be returned to the Library Donation Counter.
- Once returned, the player may revisit the library to reread the excerpts for tips, tricks, or fun notes.
