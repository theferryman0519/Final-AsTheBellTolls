## Base Information

- Loans are taken out with Elise from the Blackmere Bank & Exchange.
- Loans can be taken out starting after Elise is at 3 Hearts in her Friendship Bond.
- Loans that are taken out can be repaid back at any time.
- If the loan is not repaid by the due date, the amount is deducted from the player's Bellnotes at the end of that day, even if that goes into a negative amount.
- Players can only take one loan out at a given time.
- Tracking the loan will appear as a quest.
- Paying back loans early would need to be paid in full.
