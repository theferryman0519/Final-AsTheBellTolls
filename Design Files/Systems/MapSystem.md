## Information

- One of the pages the player may view from the Player Wheel is the Map.
- This page shows an aerial, overhead view of a stylized version of Blackmere and its surrounding regions.
- The player will be able to select the different buildings around the area.
- Once selected, a small panel will pop up to show the building's name, hours, who runs the building, and a short description of what is sold at the building.
- The map also shows avatar icons of each NPC taken at a snapshot of when the map was opened. The player will be able to see the general location of each NPC at a quick glance.
