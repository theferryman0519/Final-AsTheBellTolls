## Information

- Players have the ability to marry one of ten NPCs within the game: Adrian, Clara, Dante, Leo, Madison, Mara, Marcus, Nora, Sabrina, or Victor.
- Once a player has reached 12 Friendship Hearts with one of the available NPCs, they are eligible for a proposal.
- In order to propose to one of the available NPCs, the player will need to purchase the Dove Timepiece from the Blackmere General Store.
- The player will need to gift the Dove Timepiece to the appropriate NPC.
- If that NPC is also at 6 Connection Keys, the gift will be accepted and the wedding will be in five days.
- If that NPC is below 6 Connection Keys, the gift will not be accepted and will remain with the player.
- The player will not need to do anything for the wedding.
- Once the wedding occurs, the ceremony is that morning in the Blackmere Town Hall.
- After the wedding, the player is now married to that NPC.
- That NPC will now live with the player at Pendrelle Manor.
- That NPC will now have a maximum of 15 Friendship Hearts.
- Once that NPC is at 15 Friendship Hearts, then the player and NPC have the ability to start a family.
- If the player wishes to divorce the NPC, they would need to interact with the Marriage Records in the Blackmere Town Hall.
- Once divorced, that NPC will stay at 12 Friendship Hearts and 6 Connection Keys, available to be proposed to once again.
