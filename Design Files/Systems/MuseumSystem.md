## Information

- The museum includes the Hall of Wonder Donation Counter, which is unlocked after completing Dante's Curator’s Collection quest.
- Museum artifacts can be found in the overworld while in Gloamwood Depths.
- Museum gemstones can be found through gemstone deposits in Ashfall Mines.
- There are a total of 80 artifacts and gemstones.
- Once collected, artifacts need to be returned to the Donation Counter.
- Once collected, gemstones can be returned to the Donation Center.
