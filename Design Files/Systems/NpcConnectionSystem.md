## Information

- Each NPC has a Connection Bond.
- The Connection Bond is denoted by key icons, showing the progression of their intimacy and trust level with the player.
- The Connection Bond only becomes available after the NPC's Friendship Bond reaches 4 Hearts.
- Each NPC starts with 0 keys, and can have a maximum of 6 keys.
- Each NPC has a Connection Event or Connection Quest every 2 keys (2, 4, 6).
- An NPC can accept a marriage proposal after 4 keys.
- An NPC married to the player can start a family at 6 keys.
- Once an NPC reaches a new Key level, they cannot lose those number of Keys, even if losing Connection Points.

## Points Breakdown

(After Friendship Bond reaches 4 Hearts)

- Sharing a meal event with an NPC: +30 Connection Points
- Completing an NPC-specific quest: +75 Connection Points
- Completing an NPC-specific Daily Quest: +20 Friendship Points
- Inviting an NPC out to Aurora Watch: +100 Connection Points (an invitation is accepted only if the NPC has unlocked their Connection Bond)
