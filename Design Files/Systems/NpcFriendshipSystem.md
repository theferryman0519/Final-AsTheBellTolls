## Information

- Each NPC has a Friendship Bond.
- The Friendship Bond is denoted by heart icons, showing the progression of their friendship and relationship level with the player.
- Each NPC starts with 0 hearts, and can have a maximum of 12 hearts.
- An NPC that is married to the player inceases their maximum hearts 15.
- Each NPC has a Friendship Event or Friendship Quest every 3 hearts (3, 6, 9, 12, 15).
- An NPC can accept a marriage proposal at 12 hearts.
- An NPC married to the player can start a family at 15 hearts.
- Once an NPC reaches a new Heart level, they cannot lose those number of Hearts, even if losing Friendship Points.

## Points Breakdown

- First conversation each day with an NPC: +5 Friendship Points
- Giving an NPC a Loved gift: +40 Friendship Points (+100 if on birthday)
- Giving an NPC a Liked gift: +20 Friendship Points (+50 if on birthday)
- Giving an NPC a Tolerated gift: +5 Friendship Points (+15 if on birthday)
- Giving an NPC a Disliked gift: -20 Friendship Points (-50 if on birthday)
- Giving an NPC a Hated gift: -40 Friendship Points (-100 if on birthday)
- Sharing a meal event with an NPC: +30 Friendship Points (if Connection Bond is not unlocked)
- Completing an NPC-specific quest: +75 Friendship Points (if Connection Bond is not unlocked)
- Completing an NPC-specific Daily Quest: +20 Friendship Points (if Connection Bond is not unlocked)
