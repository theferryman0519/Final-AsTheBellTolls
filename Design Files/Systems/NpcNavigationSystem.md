## Information

- NPCs are going to move on a NavMesh.
- Navigatable NavMesh includes dirt paths in town, cobblestone area in town, wooden plank area in town, each of the regions surrounding Blackmere, and each building interior.
- The player will be able to traverse anywhere on the ground.
- The player and NPCs will not be able to walk through objects that have colliders.
- NPCs will navigate following their specific routine.
- When idle, NPCs will both walk around and stand still in a specified area.
- During festivals, NPCs will be idling.
- When speaking to the player, NPCs will not be moving.
- When running a shop behind a counter, NPCs will not be moving.
