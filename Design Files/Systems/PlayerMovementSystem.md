## Information

- The player uses the Movement Input Keys to move around the scene.
- Movement speed for the player is constant throughout the game, so there is no running or slow-walking mechanic.
- After consuming certain tonics, the player gains 25% movement speed for the duration of the tonic.
- After completing the 6 Key Event for Kira, the player permanently gains 25% movement speed.
- After restoring the Stables at the manor to at least the Rebuilding stage and purchasing a Horse from Jasmine, the player will be able to ride the horse.
- Riding a horse gains 250% movement speed for as long as the player is still on the horse.
- After restoring the Butler Quarters at the manor to the Flourishing stage, the player will be able to use the Butler Tunnels. These tunnels are fast-travel locations throughout Blackmere for the player to quickly and instantly travel from one area to another.
