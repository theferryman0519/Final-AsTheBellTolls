## Information

- Achivement Quests are ones that will progress over the course of the entire gameplay. They include:
  - Catching all fish
  - Cooking all meals and drinks
  - Crafting all tonics
  - Crafting all fabrications
  - Crafting all inventions
  - Maxing out all NPC Friendship and Connection Bonds
  - Fully restoring every room in Pendrelle Manor
  - Fully restoring all buildings in Blackmere
  - Fully funishing the Hall of Wonder museum
  - Fully furnishing the A. Whitmore Public Library
- Connection Quests are ones that are specific to each NPC while progressing their Connection Bonds.
- Friendship Quests are ones that are specific to each NPC while progressing their Friendship Bonds.
- Tutorial Quests are ones that aid the player on specific actions during a first time learning method.
- Daily Quests are ones that are small asks by NPCs in order to earn Relationship Points and reward money.
