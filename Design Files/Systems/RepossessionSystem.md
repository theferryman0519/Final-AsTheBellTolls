## Information

- The player learns in the introduction about the deadline for a potential repossession of Blackmere by the government.
- On the first day of the third year (Day 1 of Spring, Year 3), Rupert visits the town for an event.
    - He explains that he is in town to examine its progress and looks forward to repossessing the town.
    - The town will be graded based on the restoration and usage of the town for a maximum of 200 points.
    - If the assessment passes, then Rupert leaves the town for good.
    - If not, then new events will occur over the course of the third year.

## Recover Assessment Breakdown

| Action                                 | Allotted Points
|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Town Hall restoration                  | 2 points per stage (max of 12 points)
| Public Library restoration             | 2 points per stage (max of 12 points)
| Hall of Wonder restoration             | 2 points per stage (max of 12 points)
| General Store restoration              | 2 points per stage (max of 12 points)
| Winding Banks Inn restoration          | 2 points per stage (max of 12 points)
| NPCs at 8 or more Hearts               | 1 point per NPC (max of 30 points)
| Earned 1st Place at Grand Showcase     | 10 points (earned only once)
| Completed Hall of Wonder donations     | 20 points (earned only once)
| Completed Library donations            | 20 points (earned only once)
| Did not take out any loans             | 10 points (earned only once)
| Completed Blueprint Book inventions    | 10 points per tier (max of 50 points)
| TOTAL                                  | 200 points

## Conditional Statements on First Assessment

- If the assessment score is 140 or higher:
    - The town has passed the assessment.
    - Rupert concedes and leaves Blackmere to be autonomous.
    - There is no second return date.
- If the assessment score is between 90 and 139:
    - The town conditionally passed the assessment.
    - Rupert reluctantly passes the town, but he wants to visit every now and then to keep an eye on things.
    - Rupert visits Town Hall every non-festival Monday until Day 1 of Spring, Year 4.
    - There is no second return date.
- If the assessment score is below 90:
    - The town failed the assessment.
    - Rupert is feeling generous and gives the town a final chance.
    - Rupert visits Town Hall every non-festival Monday through Friday until Day 1 of Spring, Year 4.
    - Rupert has an event on Day 1 of Spring, Year 4 as a second assessment.

---

## Conditional Statements on Second Assessment

- If the second assessment score is 120 or higher:
    - The town has passed the assessment.
    - Rupert concedes and leaves Blackmere to be autonomous.
- If the second assessment score is below 120:
    - Rupert decides to leave the town to the people, but create the Regency Tax.
    - Each shop that sells goods or products increases each price by 35% more Bellnotes.
    - Each shop has this Regency Tax until its building is restored to at least the Prospering restoration stage.
    - Rupert leaves Blackmere.
