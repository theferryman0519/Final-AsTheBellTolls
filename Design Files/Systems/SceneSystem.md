## Information

- The game will have six different types of scenes: Opening, Gameplay, Festivals, Cinematics, Sleep, and Transition.
- Opening Scenes include the logos, title, start menu, and load menu screens.
- All Gameplay Scenes are broken down into exterior regions and interior buildings.
- All Festival Scenes deal with the different main festivals and mini festivals.
- All Cinematics Scenes deal with the different cinematics that occur, including the intro.
- The Sleep Scene displays the UI for all end of the day actions.
- Transitioning between scenes is a fade to the transition scene, then fade to the next scene.
