## Information

- While using various items, the player will consume stamina.
- The player starts each day with a full amount of stamina, regardless on when they went to bed (or fainted) the night before.
- Consuming tonics, meals, drinks, or other edible items will replenish varied amounts of stamina.
- Once the player has at least 3 Hearts with Frederick and completed their 3 Heart quest, they will have access to Guest Rooms at the Winding Banks Inn.
- When renting a room at the Winding Banks Inn for 500 Bellnotes, the player will sleep in the room for 2 hours (as long as it is before 9:00pm) and regain 50 points of stamina.
- Once the player has at least 3 Hearts with Madison and completed their 3 Heart quest, they will have access to Emergency Supply Stations placed around Blackmere.
- There are five total Emergency Supply Stations around Blackmere, each accessible once per day. When accessed, the player will gain 10 points of stamina.
- When restoring the Main Bedroom, the player's maximum amount of stamina will increase.
- If the player ever fully runs out of stamina, they will faint and the day will promptly end, no matter what time of day.
