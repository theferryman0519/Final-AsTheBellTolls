## Information

- Time manipulation becomes available when the Central Room in the Manor is at or above the Rebuilding restoration stage.
- At Rebuilding stage, the player can use up to 6 time manipulation Chimes per day and the Advance time manipulation system.
- At Recovering stage, the player can use up to 8 time manipulation Chimes pers day and the Preserve time manipulation system.
- At Renewed stage, the player can use up to 10 time manipulation Chimes per day and the Synchronize time manipulation system.
- At Growing stage, the player can use up to 15 time manipulation Chimes per day.
- At Prospering stage, the player can use up to 20 time manipulation Chimes per day.
- At Flourishing stage, the player can use up to 30 time manipulation Chimes per day.

**<u>Activating Mechanics:</u>** While the Manor's Central Room is in its Weathered restoration stage, the use of time manipulation is not available. It only becomes available once the Central Room has been restored to its Rebuilding stage. Once available, the player will need to walk up to the grandfather clock and interact with it.

**<u>Mechanic (Advance):</u>** After interacting with the grandfather clock, the Time Manipulation Menu will appear. Here, the player will be able to choose the Advance selection. Once selected, the player will get to choose from an active event that has a time duration. These include animals maturing or giving byproductss, crops or flowers growing, or inventions being crafted and tested. Once the specific event has been selected, the player will need to confirm the use of time manipulation. If the player does not have enough Chimes, then an error will appear in place of the confirmation. After confirming, the event's time duration will decrease by a specified amount, and the player will lose 1 Chime. The player will be able to continue using time manipulation provided they have the Chimes required as well as events that have not yet completed.

**<u>Mechanic (Preserve):</u>** After interacting with the grandfather clock while the Manor's Central Room has been restored to at least the Recovering stage, the Time Manipulation Menu will appear. Here, the player will be able to choose the Preserve selection. Once selected, the player will get to choose from a stored crop, flower, meal, drink, or animal byproduct. These items can be stored either in the player's Inventory Satchel or their Pantry in the Kitchen. The item can still be fresh or it can already be spoiled. Once the item has been selected, the player will need to confirm the use of time manipulation. If the player does not have enough Chimes, then an error will appear in place of the confirmation. After confirming, the item will return to fresh (if was already spoiled) as if newly acquired, then add a specified duration to its Spoil Duration. The player will lose 1 Chime. The player will be able to continue using time manipulation provided they have the Chimes required as well as items in their Inventory or Pantry.

**<u>Mechanic (Synchronize):</u>** After interacting with the grandfather clock while the Manor's Central Room has been restored to at least the Renewed stage, the Time Manipulation Menu will appear. Here, the player will be able to choose the Synchronize selection. Once selected, the player will get to choose between two active events that have durations. These include animals maturing or giving byproducts, crops or flowers growing, or inventions being crafted and tested. Once the two events have been selected, the use of time manipulation will sychronize the two durations to the lower of the two times. For example, the player selects Apples having 3 days left to grow and Plums having 6 days left to grow. The use of the Synchronize mechanic makes the two durations become the lower of the two times, or 3 days in this case. Once the two events have been selected, the player will need to confirm the use of time manipulation. If the player does not have enough Chimes, then an error will appear in place of the confirmation. After confirming, the two events will synchronize to the lower duration, and the player will lose 2 Chimes. This is the only mechanic of the three to consume 2 Chimes instead of 1 Chime. The player will be able to continue using time manipulation provided they have the Chimes required as well as at least two events that have not yet completed.

|------------------|-----------------------------|
