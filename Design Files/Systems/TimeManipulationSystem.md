## Information

- Time manipulation becomes available when the Central Room in the Manor is at or above Rebuilding restoration stage.
- At Rebuilding stage, the player can use up to 6 time manipulation Chimes per day.
- At Recovering stage, the player can use up to 8 time manipulation Chimes per day.
- At Renewed stage, the player can use up to 10 time manipulation Chimes per day.
- At Growing stage, the player can use up to 15 time manipulation Chimes per day.
- At Prospering stage, the player can use up to 20 time manipulation Chimes per day.
- At Flourishing stage, the player can use up to 30 time manipulation Chimes per day.

**<u>Activating Mechanics:</u>** While the Manor's Central Room is in its Weathered restoration stage, Time Manipulation is not available. It only becomes available once the Central Room has been restored to its Rebuilding stage. Once available, the player will need to walk up to the grandfather clock and interact with it.

**<u>Mechanic:</u>** After interacting with the grandfather clock, the Time Manipulation Menu will pop up. Here, the player will be able to choose from an active event that has a time duration. These include animals maturing or giving byproducts, crops or flowers growing, or inventions being crafted and tested. Once the specific event has been selected, the player will need to confirm using Time Manipulation. If the player does not have enough Chimes, then an error will appear in place of the confirmation. After confirming, the event's time duration will decrease by a set amount, and the player will lose 1 Chime. The player will be able to continue using Time Manipulation provided they have the Chimes required as well as events that have not yet completed.
