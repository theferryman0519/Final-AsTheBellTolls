## Information

- The player has a set of tools to use throughout the game.
- The player is given five tools at the start of the game: Axe, Hammer, Herb Knife, Pickaxe, and Scythe.
- The player can visit Nora and receive the Fishing Rod.
- Once the Gardens or Orchard are restored to the Rebuilding stage, the player will receive the Hoe and Watering Can.
- Later, the player can visit Nora and receive (and purchase) a Fishing Net.
- All tools (except the Fishing Net) are stored in the Tool Belt portion of the player's Inventory.
- The Fishing Net is stored in the Satchel portion of the player's Inventory.

---

## Tools

### Axe

- Received at the start of the game during the Intro from Edward.
- Used for chopping trees to collect wood and sap.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.

### Fishing Net

- Received when visiting Nora and completing The Big One quest.
- Used for catching multiple fish in the river and lake.
- Is not upgraded, and only has one use before needing to be purchased again.

### Fishing Rod

- Received when visiting Nora and completing the Cast Into the Current quest.
- Used for catching fish in the river and lake.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.

### Hammer

- Received at the start of the game during the Intro from Edward.
- Used for restoring rooms in the manor and individual buildings in town.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.

### Herb Knife

- Received at the start of the game during the Intro from Edward.
- Used for gathering herbs from bushes.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.

### Hoe

- Received when the Garden or Orchard has been restored to Rebuilding stage.
- Used for tilling soil to be ready for seeds.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.

### Pickaxe

- Received at the start of the game during the Intro from Edward.
- Used for gathering stones, ore, and gemstones from deposits.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.

### Scythe

- Received at the start of the game during the Intro from Edward.
- Used for cutting tall grass for silk, fiber, and reeds.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.

### Watering Can

- Received when the Garden or Orchard has been restored to Rebuilding stage.
- Used for watering tilled soil or growing crops and flowers.
- Can be upgraded with Marcus.
