## Information

- The player will have eight different tutorial quests at the beginning of the game.
- These tutorial quests include:
  - A Friendly Face: Teaching the player how to interact and speak with characters.
  - Thoughtful Gifts: Teaching the player how to give gifts to characters.
  - Cast Into the Current: Teaching the player how to use the fishing minigame.
  - Never Go Hungry: Teaching the player how to cook meals and drinks.
  - My First Fabrication: Teaching the player how to use the workbench.
  - Down the Hatch: Teaching the player how to use the tonic making minigame.
  - Home Is Where the Heart Is: Teaching the player how to restore rooms in the manor.
  - Where We Thrive: Teaching the player how to restore buildings in town.
- Other tutorials that are not quests will implement the Tutorial Menu.
- Tutorial Menus will give detailed instructions on how to complete certain actions.
