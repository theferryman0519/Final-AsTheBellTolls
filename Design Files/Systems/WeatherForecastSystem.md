## Information

- Once the player has restored the Butler Quarters to Recovering stage, the Weather Forecast is unlocked within the Calendar.
- Once unlocked, the Calendar will highlight the weather for the next three days.
- The player will be able to plan ahead for the next three upcoming days.
- Each new day, the weather forecast will progress to show the next three upcoming days.
