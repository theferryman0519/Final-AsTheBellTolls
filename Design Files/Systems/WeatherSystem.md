## Information

- Weather will always be Clear for outdoor festivals
- Weather will always be Clear for the first 4 days of Spring.
- Weather affects the mood of NPCs.
- If the Garden in Pendrelle Manor is below Prospering restoration stage, Rainy and Rainy (Severe) days will water the crops in the morning.
- If the Orchard in Pendrelle Manor is below Prospering restoration stage, Rainy and Rainy (Severe) days will water the crops in the morning.
